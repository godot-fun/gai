# Praxinoscope Mirror Theatre

## Overview

A ring of mirrors reflects sequential images from a rotating strip, producing a bright stable loop inside a miniature theatrical housing.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: elegant optical-toy loops, miniature stage scenes, and reflected mechanical motion

## Signature Features

- A central polygon of mirrors faces an outer ring of sequential drawings.
- The reflected action appears steadier and brighter than the moving source strip.
- Decorative theatre framing separates the virtual performance from the mechanism.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Praxinoscope Mirror Theatre** style. Reveal the illustrated strip, central mirror crown, and drive mechanism before settling into the reflected performance. Keep the source drawings visibly rotating while the virtual image holds in one theatrical window. Use warm brass, dark wood, printed paper, and restrained stage lighting.

## Avoid

- Avoid viewing slits, a full-screen film projection, reflections that rotate with the source strip, or a loop whose frame registration drifts between mirrors.

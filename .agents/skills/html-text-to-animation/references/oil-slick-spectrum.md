# Oil-Slick Spectrum

## Overview

A thin oily layer curls into spectral contours whose color follows thickness and viewing angle.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: pollution, uneasy beauty, fluid interfaces, and surface tension
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Float a paper-thin oil layer over dark water; shape its color into contour bands around thickness gradients, with sharp black gaps where the film ruptures.
- Advect the film slowly, stretch necks, merge islands, and let interference rings reorganize continuously; use a near-orthographic macro view.
- Use small neutral captions in clear water areas, never over the most intricate bands.

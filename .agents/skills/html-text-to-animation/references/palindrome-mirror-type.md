# Palindrome Mirror Type

## Overview

Letters and phrases meet across a reflective axis to expose reversible structure and changed meaning.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: symmetry, wordplay, duality, and transformation
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Center a true palindrome or reversible structure on a precise reflection axis with paired spacing and controlled glyph symmetry.
- Build outward from the center, reverse direction at the axis, and test legibility in both orientations before adding reflection.
- For **Palindrome Mirror Type**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Palindrome Mirror Type** style. Center a true palindrome or reversible structure on a precise reflection axis with paired spacing and controlled glyph symmetry. Build outward from the center, reverse direction at the axis, and test legibility in both orientations before adding reflection. Build hierarchy from glyph anatomy, spacing, reading direction, linguistic timing, and the specific encoding or word structure being demonstrated. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.
- Change typographic scale, neutral surface, accent color, and motion amplitude while preserving valid glyphs, reading order, timing, and decodability.

## Avoid

- Avoid non-palindromic filler, generic mirrored logo effects, distorted glyphs, multiple axes, or hiding the actual wordplay.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

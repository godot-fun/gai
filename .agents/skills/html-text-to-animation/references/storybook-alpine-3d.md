# Storybook Alpine 3D

## Overview

A saturated sculptural landscape of rounded mountains, dense grass, and continuous aerial-to-ground camera movement.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A saturated sculptural landscape of rounded mountains, dense grass, and continuous aerial-to-ground camera movement.

## Signature Features

- Smooth rounded forms that are neither photoreal nor low-poly
- Emerald grass, cobalt sky, clean snow, warm-grey rock, and white cumulus
- Wind-responsive knee-high grass and wildflowers
- Atmospheric violet confined to the farthest ridge
- One unbroken flight descending into a low orbit

## Style Prompt

Model the world as a tactile illustrated diorama with rounded weathered ridges, smooth sculptural surfaces, and enough detail to avoid toy minimalism. Under a high sun just behind the camera, keep grass fully emerald, sky cobalt, snow and clouds clean white, and rock warm grey-ochre. Follow slopes with credible snow lines and moving cloud shadows. Confine pale violet atmospheric perspective to the farthest quarter of depth; do not wash the entire world purple. Build foreground meadows from dense knee-high blades and varied wildflowers that bend in passing gusts and recover at different speeds. Shape cumulus as clusters of illuminated cotton-like volumes with readable shaded undersides. When the sun lowers, lengthen shadows and warm cloud rims without abandoning the palette. Use one uninterrupted move: fly above the landscape, descend toward a grassy slope, then circle the subject at blade height while holding it at roughly one quarter of frame height.

## Avoid

needle peaks, grey photoreal rock, frozen vegetation, global haze, low-poly facets, and cuts that break the sense of flight.

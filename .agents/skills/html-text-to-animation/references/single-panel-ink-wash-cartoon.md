# Single-Panel Ink-Wash Cartoon

## Overview

A borderless editorial cartoon combining elastic dip-pen observation, restrained watercolor, and one dry handwritten caption. Choose this for one concise ink-wash observation or joke whose meaning resolves inside a single frame.

## Signature Features

- Hairline straights swelling through turns and flicking at release
- Boxy objects drawn in separate overshooting strokes
- Grey, sea blue, sand, and rust washes with pooled edges
- Expressive caricatures surrounded by specific everyday props
- Mostly still tableau with slight living tremor

## Style Prompt

Fill the frame edge to edge as a single editorial drawing with no comic-panel border. Use a dip pen whose long straights stay extremely fine, curves swell under pressure, and endings release in quick flicks. Construct furniture, rooms, buildings, and boxes from separate strokes that cross slightly at corners instead of forming perfect vector rectangles. Wash most objects lightly in cool grey, sea blue, sand, and restrained rust, allowing cold-press paper to show, pigment to pool at drying edges, and color to drift just beyond the ink. Give every person specific facial features, posture, and comic exaggeration, then surround them with enough ordinary objects to make the social situation legible while preserving breathing white space. Handwrite one short deadpan caption along the bottom in the user's language and reveal it word by word. Keep the composition almost still, with only a subtle tremor in living figures and a slight camera drift or push. When contrast helps, divide the page into two adjacent borderless situations.

# Polarized Crystal Light

## Overview

Crystalline stress patterns ignite in spectral color between crossed polarizers while the object itself stays transparent.

## Signature Features

- Center a transparent crystal or stressed plastic specimen between two dark polarizing planes; reserve spectral fringes for stress contours while untouched regions remain nearly black.
- Rotate one polarizer rather than the object, allowing interference colors to extinguish and flare; inspect details with a stable microscope-like push-in.
- Use compact scientific captions in white with one spectral key, separated from the specimen.

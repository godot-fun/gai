# Watershed Journey

## Overview

A purpose-built visual system in which rain follows elevation into tributaries, floodplains, rivers, and an estuary.

## Metadata

- Collection: Nature and Ecological Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: rain follows elevation into tributaries, floodplains, rivers, and an estuary

## Signature Features

- Make the process whereby rain follows elevation into tributaries, floodplains, rivers, and an estuary the dominant visual mechanism.
- Establish the specific causes, constraints, and scale governing **Watershed Journey** before revealing their consequences.
- Let labels, texture, and camera movement support the watershed journey mechanism instead of competing with it.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Watershed Journey** style. Preserve the governing spatial, temporal, material, or rhythmic relationship; add atmosphere and detail only after that relationship reads clearly.

- Reject motion that makes **Watershed Journey** physically or conceptually inconsistent, even when the surface effect looks attractive.

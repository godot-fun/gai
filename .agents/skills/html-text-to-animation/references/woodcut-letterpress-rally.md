# Woodcut Letterpress Rally

## Overview

A populist relief-print poster driven by bold ink slabs, wood type, registration errors, and beat-like camera impacts. Choose this for a mobilizing broadside that combines relief imagery, forceful movable type, and collective public energy.

## Signature Features

- Blue, red, and black relief plates plus one optional focal spot ink
- Linocut figures with gouged white lines and crescent shadows
- Coarse halftone limited to a small share of the image
- Short wood-type slogans embedded in banners and signs
- Visible grain, ink starvation, squeeze, and plate drift

## Style Prompt

Print the frame as a physical relief poster on cream stock. Assign blue to sky, water, and broad shadow masses; red to warm planes and stripes; black to contours, carved detail, and type. Add one separate muted focal ink only when the lead would otherwise disappear. Knock solid plates out from one another, but allow selected coarse halftones to overprint at distinct screen angles; keep those dots to less than a third of the frame. Carve people and objects with thick outer silhouettes, decisive white gouges, and crescent-shaped shadows. Cover around seventy percent of the poster with image. Integrate one or two short, meaningful lines of heavy wood type into actual placards, banners, storefronts, or architectural bands; give the type an offset block shadow and wood grain. Retain two-to-four-pixel plate drift, pinholed under-inking, squeezed edges, and uneven pressure. Use compact pushes and lateral tracks, with a slight camera jolt on a rhetorical beat.

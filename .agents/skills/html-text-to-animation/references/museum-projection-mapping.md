# Museum Projection Mapping

## Overview

Images align to a physical facade or artifact so architectural features become active narrative surfaces.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: exhibitions, history, events, and site-specific storytelling
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Start from a measured facade or artifact mesh with masks for edges, openings, relief, and protected details.
- Calibrate projection, animate along real architectural features, and use transformations that appear to carve, unfold, or illuminate the surface.
- For **Museum Projection Mapping**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Museum Projection Mapping** style. Start from a measured facade or artifact mesh with masks for edges, openings, relief, and protected details. Calibrate projection, animate along real architectural features, and use transformations that appear to carve, unfold, or illuminate the surface. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

Adapt subject matter, wording, duration, and aspect ratio to the user's material. Preserve supplied text unless rewriting is requested, and simplify secondary detail before compromising the defining construction logic.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust program, site context, human activity, viewpoint, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between section, elevation, and experiential space.
- Change material palette, weather, lighting, occupancy, and camera path without violating structure, circulation, assembly, or projection geometry.

## Avoid

- Avoid rectangular video pasted over the building, spill beyond masks, unrelated particle shows, or motion ignoring relief.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.
- Keep essential text outside dense movement and preserve a clear reduced-motion end state.

# Auction and Price Discovery

## Overview

Private offers become a public clearing result through explicit bidding, matching, and allocation rules.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: bids, asks, matching, scarcity, and emergent market prices

## Signature Features

- Introduce participants with distinct valuations or costs before revealing their offers.
- Animate the declared auction rule, bid sequence, clearing condition, allocation, and payment separately.
- Contrast auction formats only when identical participants and goods make the comparison valid.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Auction and Price Discovery** style. Stage participants around a central lot or matching field, reveal bids and asks according to a named rule, and let the clearing price emerge from the actual sequence. Keep valuation, submitted offer, allocation, and payment visually distinct. Use tension through timing and narrowing ranges, not casino decoration.

## Avoid

- Avoid unexplained price jumps, fake urgency, or mixing first-price, second-price, and continuous-market rules.
- Do not equate the winning bid with intrinsic value or hide losing and unfilled offers.

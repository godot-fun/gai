# Cobalt Editorial

## Overview

A polished professional system pairing parchment warmth with precise cobalt-blue typography and geometry. Choose this for polished professional communication led by cobalt typography, restrained geometry, and dependable corporate clarity.

## Metadata

- Collection: Ready-Made Layouts
- Medium: HTML, CSS, SVG, or Canvas
- Best for: business explainers, reports, institutional stories, and calm product narratives
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Warm paper rather than sterile white.
- Cobalt as the dominant structural color.
- Geometric sans-serif headline hierarchy.
- Thin rules and measured whitespace.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Cobalt Editorial** style. Use a disciplined editorial grid, wide margins, and a few cobalt panels or rules. Animate information through clean wipes, baseline-aligned movement, and quiet scale changes rather than corporate dashboard effects.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Choose **Cobalt Grid** for visible drafting structure, **Cartesian Editorial** for quieter analytical warmth, or **Editorial Forest** for literary character.
- Generic blue gradients, stock-dashboard cards, glossy icons, dense charts, or excessive rounded corners.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

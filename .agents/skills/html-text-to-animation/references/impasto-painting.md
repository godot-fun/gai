# Impasto Painting

## Overview

Visible loaded-brush strokes and layered color give the frame physical emotional weight. Choose this for broadly expressive painted illustration where tactile strokes support emotion without requiring a historically specific oil-painting process.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: intense emotion, dramatic portraits, and expressive climaxes
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Thick directional brush marks.
- Layered broken color.
- Strong light-dark structure.
- Edges vary from carved to lost.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Impasto Painting** style. Build the image in broad masses before revealing sharper strokes at the focal point. Animate through accumulating paint and changing light rather than liquid morphing.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Choose **Impasto Oil Painting** when physical oil ridges and palette-knife construction must be explicit.
- Smooth airbrush, photographic detail, uniform texture, pastel delicacy, or brush motion unrelated to form.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

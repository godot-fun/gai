# Anamorphic Cylinder Reveal

## Overview

A distorted drawing on a flat plane resolves into a coherent image only in the reflection of a polished cylinder.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: hidden-image reveals, reflective geometry, and viewpoint-dependent transformations

## Signature Features

- The source artwork remains visibly stretched around the cylinder base.
- Cylindrical reflection reconstructs the intended image through angular remapping.
- Camera placement and cylinder position determine whether the reveal is legible.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Anamorphic Cylinder Reveal** style. Present the strange radial drawing first, lower or uncover a polished cylinder at its calibrated center, and let the reflected image assemble without changing the source artwork. Move the camera slightly to demonstrate the narrow valid viewpoint while maintaining believable reflection and surface curvature.

## Avoid

- Avoid morphing the flat drawing directly, using a planar mirror, hiding the distorted source, or keeping the reflected image correct from every camera angle.

# Urban Pen and Wash

## Overview

Fine sepia location sketching and loose transparent watercolor create an observant, imperfect travel-journal page. Choose this when architectural ink line establishes an observed city before selective wash adds atmosphere.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: places, architecture, travel, memory, and human-scale reportage

## Signature Features

- Fine lines wobble, overshoot, and remain slightly open.
- Color sits loosely beyond the ink.
- Cream cold-press paper stays visible.
- Selective detail concentrates at the subject.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Urban Pen and Wash** style. Draw structural lines quickly and imperfectly, then drop a few translucent washes that bloom beyond them. Add concise handwritten observations at the page edge.

## Avoid

- Choose **Watercolor Journal** for diary-like mixed observations, or **Watercolor Illustration** when line is not structurally dominant.
- Closed vector contours, opaque coloring-book fills, ruler-perfect architecture, saturated digital gradients, or uniform detail.

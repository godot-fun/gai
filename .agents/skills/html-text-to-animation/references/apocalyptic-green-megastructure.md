# Apocalyptic Green Megastructure

## Overview

A near-monochrome industrial abyss where layered machinery disappears into fog, a remote ring dominates scale, and one red traveler supplies human measure.

## Signature Features

- Green-black machinery stacked through deep atmospheric layers
- Large distant ring or circular machine
- Sparse shafts leaking from overhead structure
- Tiny red-clad back-facing traveler on a walkway
- Slow advance with drifting vapor, dust, cables, and mechanical pulses

## Style Prompt

Construct an enormous industrial interior or ruined machine city from bridges, pipes, tanks, trusses, vents, hanging cables, platforms, and unidentifiable mass. Hold almost everything in black-green, oxidized olive, and pale toxic mist, using value and scale rather than varied color to separate depth. Repeat increasingly faint structure into the haze and place one huge circular machine, gate, or ring far away as the compositional anchor. Let a few narrow beams leak from overhead breaks and expose suspended dust without brightening the whole scene. Put one small traveler in a muted red coat or suit on the central walkway, back to camera and carrying a visible pack, so scale reads immediately. Advance slowly behind them with subtle parallax, vapor releases, distant rotating components, hanging cable sway, and occasional low mechanical light.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

generic spaceship corridors, blue sci-fi grading, empty geometric boxes, heroic close-ups, excessive LEDs, clean surfaces, and fog so dense that structure loses hierarchy.

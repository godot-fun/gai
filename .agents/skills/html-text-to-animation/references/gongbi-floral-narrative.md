# Gongbi Floral Narrative

## Overview

A meticulous mineral-color painting built from fine contour, layered flat washes, controlled edge shading, and symbolically arranged flowers, birds, figures, or objects.

## Metadata

- Collection: Chinese Art Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A meticulous mineral-color painting built from fine contour, layered flat washes, controlled edge shading, and symbolically arranged flowers, birds, figures, or objects.

## Signature Features

- Hair-fine even iron-wire outlines
- Successive flat color and controlled tonal washes
- Mineral blue, green, cinnabar, ochre, shell white, and restrained gold
- Detailed petals, feathers, textiles, and architecture
- Measured motion limited to breath, breeze, gaze, and small gesture

## Style Prompt

Plan every contour before coloring. Draw figures, peonies or other plants, birds, furniture, architecture, and props with an even hair-fine dark line whose pressure remains disciplined through curves. Fill each shape with a clean local mineral color, then deepen undersides, overlaps, and folds through several transparent edge washes rather than Western cast-shadow modeling. Add shell white, vein lines, feather barbs, textile patterns, and restrained gold only after the forms are established. Keep colors rich but harmonized by warm silk or paper and maintain clear separation between adjacent shapes. Arrange flowers and objects symbolically around a readable central relationship rather than as decoration. Animate only what a painted scroll could gently sustain: petal tremor, bird blink, sleeve lift, breath, eye movement, or a slow camera drift.

## Avoid

broad expressive splashes, thick oil texture, gradient airbrush, black outlines of changing cartoon weight, saturated digital RGB color, cloned petals, and constant full-body motion.

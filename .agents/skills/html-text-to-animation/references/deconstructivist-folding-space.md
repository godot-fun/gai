# Deconstructivist Folding Space

## Overview

Slanted planes, fractured grids, and displaced volumes challenge stable orientation without losing structural intent.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: disruption, conflicting narratives, architecture, and instability

## Signature Features

- Build fractured grids, non-orthogonal planes, displaced envelopes, and controlled collision around one navigable path.
- Fold and shear major planes in a few decisive moves while the camera follows a stable horizon reference.
- For **Deconstructivist Folding Space**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Deconstructivist Folding Space** style. Build fractured grids, non-orthogonal planes, displaced envelopes, and controlled collision around one navigable path. Fold and shear major planes in a few decisive moves while the camera follows a stable horizon reference. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid random broken polygons, impossible clipping, constant camera roll, neon cyberpunk styling, or chaos without structural tension.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

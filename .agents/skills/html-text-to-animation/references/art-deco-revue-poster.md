# Art Deco Revue Poster

## Overview

A black-lacquer theatrical poster animated through metallic geometry, stage symmetry, and rhythmic repetition.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Banded gold and silver gradients with hard tonal steps
- Stepped proscenium, sunbursts, streamlined curves, bulbs, and spotlights
- Elongated expressive figures with period costume detail
- Frontal symmetry and a single saturated hero accent
- Chasing lights and repeated forms that ripple toward center

## Style Prompt

Use a black lacquer ground framed by a receding gold proscenium. Construct metal from three to five discrete brightness bands—bronze shadow, warm gold body, pale specular edge—rather than flat yellow or smooth gradients. Pair this with cool silver, airbrushed halos, ziggurat steps, radial rays, and streamlined arcs. Place a real-faced, elongated performer at the center of a frontal composition, with fringe, sequins, or feathers only when the subject supports them. Keep skin in pale silver-cream and permit one saturated accent on the narrative lead. Animate bulb chases around the frame, concentric rings from sound sources, and rows of dancers, waves, columns, or products folding inward like choreography. Use slow pushes and cuts that land on musical beats.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

faceless mannequins, rainbow palettes, and generic neon.

# Natural-History Specimen Plate

## Overview

A hand-colored copperplate that studies one central subject through numbered details while a single element quietly moves.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Warm black-brown contour, parallel hatch, cross-hatch, and stippled transitions
- Recognizable transparent watercolor inside engraved structure
- Central figure plus three to six numbered studies
- Spaced small-cap title, numbered captions, bevelled plate mark, and foxing
- Static page with one living movement and a steady detail push

## Style Prompt

Engrave every object in warm brown-black using dense parallel lines that follow form, heavier and crossed in the shadows, with stippling reserved for soft transitions. Add a hatched cast shadow beneath the specimen and leave the sky as paper or a few sparse horizontals. Hand-color within the engraved structure at moderate saturation using warm paper, umber, rust red, yellow, and grey-violet, allowing minor human irregularity without obscuring the cuts. Design a formal plate: one central figure occupies about three fifths of page height, surrounded by three to six numbered studies of parts, stages, tools, tracks, or related objects, each about one fifth height. Add a widely spaced small-cap heading above, concise numbered captions below, an impressed bevelled plate boundary, and light foxing. Use even daylight by default; shift wash temperature rather than inventing dramatic illumination, except in a dark interior where cool color and hard side light are appropriate. Keep the sheet still while only one leaf, wing, hand, mechanism, or eye moves, then push at constant speed toward the revealing detail.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

smooth gradients, neon color, tiny central vignettes, animated labels everywhere, and modern infographic styling.

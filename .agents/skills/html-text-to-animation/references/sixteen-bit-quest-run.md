## Style Prompt

Choose a native low resolution, scale by nearest neighbor, and construct a side-scrolling world from consistent tiles under a violet night sky. Design an original player sprite, hazards, pickups, environmental actors, and one final challenge from the user's subject. Open with INSERT COIN or an equivalent start prompt, show a concise objective, then let the player run, jump, climb, avoid, collect, and confront the central obstacle. Use stepped sprite cycles, integer movement, limited colors, dithering, parallax tile bands, a readable HUD, and restrained CRT scan lines. Convert real metrics into score, energy, distance, or inventory only when the mapping is meaningful. End with a clear GAME CLEAR, achievement, or transformed landscape rather than an arbitrary score screen.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

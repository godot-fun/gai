# Ecological Corridor Suitability

## Overview

A purpose-built visual system in which habitat cores, resistance surfaces, and barriers reveal least-cost corridors.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartography and Geographic Storytelling
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: habitat cores, resistance surfaces, and barriers reveal least-cost corridors

## Signature Features

- Make the process whereby habitat cores, resistance surfaces, and barriers reveal least-cost corridors the dominant visual mechanism.
- Establish the specific causes, constraints, and scale governing **Ecological Corridor Suitability** before revealing their consequences.
- Let labels, texture, and camera movement support the ecological corridor suitability mechanism instead of competing with it.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Ecological Corridor Suitability** style. Build the composition around a clear demonstration of how habitat cores, resistance surfaces, and barriers reveal least-cost corridors. Preserve the governing spatial, temporal, material, or rhythmic relationship; add atmosphere and detail only after that relationship reads clearly.

## Avoid

- Do not replace **Ecological Corridor Suitability** with generic particles, glowing lines, a dashboard, or a slideshow.
- Reject motion that makes **Ecological Corridor Suitability** physically or conceptually inconsistent, even when the surface effect looks attractive.

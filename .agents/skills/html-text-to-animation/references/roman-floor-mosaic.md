# Roman Floor Mosaic

## Overview

A warm stone-and-glass narrative laid tessera by tessera, with contour-following borders, restrained figure movement, and glints confined to gold glass. Choose this for excavated or constructed stone tesserae arranged as a durable horizontal classical floor.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Small irregular natural-stone tesserae and dark recessed grout
- Two or three contour-following rows around each figure
- Warm gold, umber, terracotta, marble, pale blue-grey, and one imperial-purple accent
- Frontal, slightly formal figures on faded granular backgrounds
- Short camera moves while gestures, foliage, crowds, and light provide motion

## Style Prompt

Design the image first as broad flat areas, then replace every area with individually cut tesserae of consistent small scale. Run two or three rows parallel to all important contours before filling larger fields in looser horizontal courses, and wrap each figure in a dark tessera outline. Use only plausible stone and glass colors: burnt gold, warm umber, terracotta, cream marble, pale blue-grey for sky or water, and a single imperial-purple glass accent. Vary each cut slightly, light it from upper left, and cast a tiny shadow into narrow dark grout; keep grout darker than every stone. Place gold-leaf glass only near actual light sources and let those pieces glint slowly as the viewpoint changes. Pose figures frontally with the formal stiffness of a floor mosaic while preserving recognizable hands, faces, garments, and objects. Fill background zones with smaller, faded tesserae rather than smooth color. Use only short pushes or withdrawals; animate restrained gestures, crowd rhythm, plants, water, and light, not elastic bodies.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

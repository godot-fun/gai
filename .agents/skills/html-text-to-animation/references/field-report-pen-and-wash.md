# Field-Report Pen and Wash

## Overview

An urgent on-location sketchbook image using confident warm-black line, displaced transparent wash, and a handwritten field note.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: An urgent on-location sketchbook image using confident warm-black line, displaced transparent wash, and a handwritten field note.

## Signature Features

- Fast tapered pen with overshot corners
- Hatched figures, ground, smoke, and shadow
- Olive, earth yellow, smoke grey, and one hot light accent
- Wet uneven wash offset from drawing
- Layered lateral pans and hard page-like changes of viewpoint

## Style Prompt

Draw as a practiced field reporter working quickly under pressure. Use warm-black pen in decisive thin strokes that flick at the end and run beyond corners. Build darks with rapid parallel hatch, broken zigzags, and layered marks across both figures and terrain. Flood roughly seventy percent of the sheet with bold transparent washes of olive, earth yellow, smoke grey, and one hot orange reserved for light, sky, flame, or warning. Let washes have soft wet edges, uneven value, occasional backruns, and a small consistent displacement from the drawing; never reduce them to hard polygons. Keep skin as diluted earth yellow and leave paper only at the strongest lights, a narrow outer edge, and natural brush skips. Add one hurried pencil note in a corner—place, time, weather, or condition—in the user's language and in hand-drawn script. Separate foreground, action, and distance enough for a lateral pan, then hard-cut to a new viewpoint as though turning a sketchbook page.

## Avoid

polished editorial finish, mostly empty paper, muddy glazing, heavy artificial noise, decorative calligraphy, and generic military imagery detached from the user's subject.

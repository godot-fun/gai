# Airbrush Counting Diagram

## Overview

A bright 1980s airbrush world annotated by precise counting marks, chrome reflections, and grouped technical notation.

## Signature Features

- Hard-masked smooth gradients with a fine overspray fringe
- Mint and cream field with pink, magenta, checkerboard, and limited darkness
- Graphic pale faces, economical line, and solid dark hair
- Chrome and glass reflecting banded skies and star glints
- Cyan leaders, circles, dimensions, and monospaced counts tied to real objects

## Style Prompt

Construct every major form as a smooth airbrushed gradient contained by a crisp stencil, leaving only a hairline fringe of visible overspray. Keep the composition high-key: mint walls or sky and cream ground dominate, with neon pink and magenta restricted to accents, plus a black-and-white checkerboard surface where useful. Confine night purple or deep values to less than one fifth of the frame. Render people as elegant graphic portraits with pale planar skin, minimal decisive lines, solid dark hair, and mint or pink rim light while preserving age, clothing, and identity. Treat metal and glass as polished chrome with horizontal reflected sky bands, a hard horizon, and a few controlled star glints. Overlay—not frame—the image with thin cyan leaders, dotted grouping circles, dimension ticks, and small white labels. Count something meaningful to the story in monospaced numerals; each new group should be circled as its number advances. Use a slow push or withdrawal.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

striped retro suns, dashboard panels, fuzzy mask edges, decorative equations, unreadable labels, and annotations disconnected from visible objects.

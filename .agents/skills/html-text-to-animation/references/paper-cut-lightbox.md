# Paper-Cut Lightbox

## Overview

Six to nine stacked card layers form a shallow illuminated world with warm transmitted light and soft interlayer shadows. Choose this when translucent paper layers are defined by illumination from behind inside a shallow display box.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: fairy tales, nocturnal landscapes, intimate stories, and crafted title sequences

## Signature Features

- Clearly countable cut-card depth planes.
- Warm backlight burns through windows and moons.
- Each layer casts a soft shadow on the next.
- Macro camera moves inside the box.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Paper-Cut Lightbox** style. Build a physical depth hierarchy, move the camera gently between layers, and vary parallax according to actual spacing. Keep paper edges crisp and illumination warm.

## Avoid

- Choose **Layered Paper Cut** for front-lit paper scenes, or **Shadow-Box Diorama** for opaque miniature depth.
- Flat collage, glossy plastic, impossible depth, harsh black shadows, or a free-flying camera that ignores the box.

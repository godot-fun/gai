# Dichroic Glass Refraction

## Overview

Layered glass transmits one hue and reflects another, changing color decisively with view and light angle.

## Signature Features

- Arrange overlapping glass fins on a dark neutral stage; assign cool transmitted light and warm reflected light with crisp edge highlights and faint floor caustics.
- Orbit the light or camera slowly so hue changes are caused by angle; reveal depth through restrained lateral parallax and one controlled rack of focus.
- Use narrow uppercase labels aligned to glass edges with generous tracking.

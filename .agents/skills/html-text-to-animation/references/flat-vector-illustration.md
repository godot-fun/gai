# Flat Vector Illustration

## Overview

Clean shapes and uniform outlines create a bright, immediately readable illustration system.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: explainers, characters, products, and editorial diagrams
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Consistent line weight.
- Flat fills without modeled shading.
- Simple geometric silhouettes.
- Compact bright palette.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Flat Vector Illustration** style. Reduce every subject to its most recognizable shapes and preserve clear foreground-background separation. Animate with clean translation, rotation, and shape morphing.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Gradients, realistic light, textured brushwork, variable outlines, or excessive detail.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

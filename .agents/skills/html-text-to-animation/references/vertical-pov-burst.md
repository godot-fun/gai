# Vertical POV Burst

## Overview

A fast vertical first-person sequence packs action, cuts, and sound into an immediate opening hook.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML animation, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: the first seconds of short-form video and immersive action lists
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Nine-by-sixteen composition.
- First-person hand or body cues.
- Dense action-sound synchronization.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Vertical POV Burst** style. Open on an action already underway, change information every few beats, preserve a central safe zone, and end the burst on a clear question or destination.

Adapt the subject, copy, duration, aspect ratio, and implementation method while preserving the defining narrative and motion logic. Keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, copy, duration, aspect ratio, palette, pacing, and implementation method.

## Avoid

- Do not copy sample brands, characters, or wording from the source material.
- Do not add motion that has no explanatory, rhythmic, or emotional purpose.
- Preserve readability and one dominant idea per beat.

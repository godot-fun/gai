# Photogram Object Study

## Overview

Objects placed on sensitized paper leave sharp silhouettes, translucent interiors, and soft contact shadows.

## Metadata

- Collection: Experimental Camera and Image Processes
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: collections, botany, evidence, and camera-less photography

## Signature Features

- Arrange real silhouettes directly on sensitized paper with contact-sharp edges, translucent interiors, and distance-dependent soft shadows.
- Place objects, expose once, remove them, and develop the latent arrangement under a fixed overhead view.
- For **Photogram Object Study**, use a palette, contrast curve, grain structure, and edge behavior native to the named capture or darkroom process.

## Avoid

- Avoid ordinary object photography, cast shadows from a virtual scene, textured silhouettes with no translucency logic, or moving objects during a single exposure.
- Do not imitate a photographic, optical, or compression process with unrelated glitch decoration or a generic color filter.

# Voronoi Territory Growth

## Overview

Competing seeds divide space into living territories whose borders move as influence changes.

## Metadata

## Signature Features

- Introduce seeds sequentially, grow their reach, and let border pressure communicate gains, losses, and stalemates.
- The underlying rule remains visually legible so the effect communicates more than surface decoration.

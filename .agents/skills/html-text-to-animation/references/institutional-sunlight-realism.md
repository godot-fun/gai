# Institutional Sunlight Realism

## Overview

A restrained American realist painting where hard geometric sunlight interrupts pale green rooms and long stillness.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Large muted green, cream, and grey-green color planes
- Visible canvas and a few long low-contrast strokes per plane
- Hard warm parallelograms of sun crossed by precise frame shadows
- Figures modeled from three or four soft-edged planes without outlines
- Extended stillness broken by one sub-second event

## Style Prompt

Compose an interior or exterior in strict one-point perspective with broad, controlled planes of pale institutional green, grey-green floor, and warm cream. Let canvas weave remain visible and drag only a few long strokes across each field, shifting local value by no more than ten percent. Send low sunlight from one side as a sharply bounded warm parallelogram across wall and floor, then overlay exact shadows from windows, rails, furniture, or bars. Keep the sky pale cream-green and all walls relatively light rather than saturated or oppressive. Model realistic people from three or four gently joined light-and-shadow shapes without contour lines; separate multiple figures spatially so their loneliness or tension reads. Hold the camera and bodies almost completely still for most of the shot. Then introduce one event lasting less than a second—a whip pan, door flare, body crossing, thrown object, or sudden hard light—before returning to stillness.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

bold outlines, vector anatomy, dark saturated walls, blurry glow, constant motion, and random painterly texture that weakens the geometry.

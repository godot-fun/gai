# Wide-Gauge Sandal Epic

## Overview

A monumental 65mm-like 3D spectacle driven by hard sun, dense architecture, rhythmic mass movement, and low tracking cameras.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Hard directional sun with crisp short or long shadows
- Detailed marble, gold, red granite, and sandstone architecture
- Warm dust mixed into the middle distance
- Crowded repetitions of columns, statues, wheels, oars, ranks, or steps
- Saturated warm print grade with fine gate instability

## Style Prompt

Construct an expansive three-dimensional world with finished cornices, fluted columns, carved statuary, tiered seating, gates, paving, and material variation; never use bare boxes as monuments. Light it with a hard sun—high for short sharp shadows, low for long equally crisp shadows—and grade the sky from cobalt overhead toward sand-white or amber near the horizon. Mix a restrained veil of warm dust into the middle distance without softening the foreground. Fill every background zone with repeated structures, crowds, vehicles, banners, wheels, oars, animals, or marching feet so scale is proven by detail. Synchronize repetitive actions to travel speed. Give the focal subject the strongest saturation while keeping it integrated with sunlight and dust. Grade like a warm, rich dye-transfer print: high contrast, protected highlights, no bloom, fine grain, and slight gate weave. Favor a low camera tracking beside the moving lead with a moderate-wide lens, supported by vast wides, overhead geometry, close impacts, and upward monumental views.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

clean CG lighting, empty arenas, low-detail facades, overexposure, and weightless crowd loops.

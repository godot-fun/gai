# Mechanical Pop-Up Book

## Overview

Folded paper mechanisms rise, pivot, pull, and collapse as each spread opens and closes.

## Metadata

- Collection: Print, Textile and Physical Craft
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: children’s stories, explanation, surprise, and spatial paper engineering

## Signature Features

- Construct each spread from visible folds, tabs, slots, V-folds, and paper thickness within a fixed page gutter.
- Open pages through a believable hinge so mechanisms rise from their attachments, then collapse cleanly in reverse.
- For **Mechanical Pop-Up Book**, let tool marks, joins, surface response, and small process variation provide material truth; choose colors plausible for the named craft.

## Avoid

- Avoid objects floating off the page, rubbery paper, impossible intersections, hidden hinges, or 3D models unrelated to fold geometry.
- Do not apply craft texture as a surface filter; show how tools, pressure, fibers, joins, or pigment actually produce it.

# Business Flywheel System

## Overview

Operational outputs feed later inputs through measurable reinforcing loops that accumulate momentum or friction.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: reinforcing loops, operational momentum, growth, and compounding advantage

## Signature Features

- Build the loop from named actions, outputs, delays, and feedback rather than circular arrows alone.
- Accumulate momentum only when a completed stage improves a later input; expose friction and leakage.
- Allow interventions to strengthen, weaken, or reverse specific links while the rest of the system persists.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Business Flywheel System** style. Construct a restrained circular mechanism from operational stages whose measurable outputs feed subsequent inputs after visible delays. Store momentum across cycles, but subtract friction, capacity limits, and leakage. Introduce one intervention at a time and show exactly which link changes, using rotation only as a consequence of completed feedback.

## Avoid

- Avoid a decorative spinning ring with slogans placed around it.
- Do not claim compounding growth without a closed causal loop, delay, metric, and limiting condition.

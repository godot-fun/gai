# Weather Radar Evolution

## Overview

Time-stepped reflectivity cells grow, rotate, merge, and decay across a geographic radar field.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: storms, forecasting, risk, and spatial change

## Signature Features

- Use a geographic base with calibrated reflectivity colors, range rings, timestamps, and distinct storm cells rather than a decorative map.
- Advance in fixed time steps as cells initiate, translate, rotate, merge, and decay; keep the map locked and use tracked paths for comparison.
- Use broadcast-clear sans-serif labels, legends, and warnings without covering active cells.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Weather Radar Evolution** style. Use a geographic base with calibrated reflectivity colors, range rings, timestamps, and distinct storm cells rather than a decorative map. Advance in fixed time steps as cells initiate, translate, rotate, merge, and decay; keep the map locked and use tracked paths for comparison. Use broadcast-clear sans-serif labels, legends, and warnings without covering active cells. Make every transition arise from the optical or scientific process itself, and preserve enough negative space for the supplied message to remain primary.

## Avoid

- Avoid smooth liquid blobs, rainbow gradients without scale, random lightning icons, camera flyovers, or forecasts presented as observations.

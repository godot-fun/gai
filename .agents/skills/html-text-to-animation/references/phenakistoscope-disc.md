# Phenakistoscope Disc

## Overview

A hand-spun illustrated disc produces motion when its radial sequence is viewed through matching slots in a mirror.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: radial motion cycles, early-animation demonstrations, and graphic disc compositions

## Signature Features

- Sequential poses form a radial ring around one disc.
- Slots and drawings share exact angular registration.
- The animated cycle is perceived in a mirror while the physical disc visibly spins.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Phenakistoscope Disc** style. Begin with the printed radial artwork at rest, show how each pose advances around the circumference, then spin the disc toward a mirror until the slot-gated loop becomes legible. Make centrifugal layout, angular spacing, and hand-driven acceleration part of the composition.

## Avoid

- Avoid arranging frames in a linear strip, omitting the mirror or slots, smoothing the motion into an ordinary tween, or allowing radial decoration to obscure pose order.

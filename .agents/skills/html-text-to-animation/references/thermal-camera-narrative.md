# Thermal Camera Narrative

## Overview

Heat replaces visible color, letting bodies, machines, and fading contact traces tell the story.

## Metadata

- Collection: Experimental Camera and Image Processes
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: energy, physiology, search, and invisible evidence

## Signature Features

- Use a calibrated temperature palette, clear min/max scale, emissivity-aware surfaces, and residual heat traces.
- Let heat transfer, accumulate, and decay over time; keep the sensor viewpoint stable and mark measurements only where meaningful.
- For **Thermal Camera Narrative**, use a palette, contrast curve, grain structure, and edge behavior native to the named capture or darkroom process.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Thermal Camera Narrative** style. Use a calibrated temperature palette, clear min/max scale, emissivity-aware surfaces, and residual heat traces. Let heat transfer, accumulate, and decay over time; keep the sensor viewpoint stable and mark measurements only where meaningful. Use a tonal curve, grain or block structure, edge behavior, and color response native to the named capture, encoding, or darkroom process. Frame typography as a caption, contact-sheet note, instrument readout, or title outside the image-forming process. Let the capture, exposure, encoding, or feedback mechanism determine transitions instead of applying generic glitch or filter presets.

## Avoid

- Avoid x-ray vision, heat passing through insulation instantly, unlabeled rainbow palettes, glowing outlines, or precise temperatures without basis.
- Do not imitate a photographic, optical, or compression process with unrelated glitch decoration or a generic color filter.

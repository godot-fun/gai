# Aurora Glass System

## Overview

A slow premium interface landscape of drifting adjacent-color light fields, translucent frosted surfaces, and restrained breathing motion.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Graphics Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Three to five large blurred color fields
- Adjacent-color palette rather than complementary collision
- Frosted translucent panels with thin inner highlights
- Large radii, soft depth, and subtle grain
- Slow sine-like drift and minimal opacity-led typography motion

## Style Prompt

Create a dark or softly neutral field beneath three to five very large blurred color masses. Choose two or three adjacent hues—violet to blue to cyan, or orange to pink to red—and drift each along a different long Bézier path so their overlaps form a mesh-like aurora without muddy grey. Float a small number of translucent frosted panels above the field using modest white fill, real background diffusion, a hairline inner highlight, large rounded corners, and soft spatial separation. Let nearby light refract or tint through the panels rather than painting gradients directly onto them. Add subtle monochrome grain to suppress banding. Keep all motion slow and breath-like: gentle drift, slight scale, minor refraction, and typography entering by opacity plus a few pixels of travel. Preserve ample space and a clear hero panel or message.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

fast bounces, many small cards, strong complementary colors, opaque milky glass, thick white borders, glossy sci-fi HUD detail, noisy chromatic dispersion, and text that slides around independently of the system.

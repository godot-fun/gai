# Air-Traffic Control Radar

## Overview

A rotating radar sweep updates labeled aircraft tracks, vectors, altitude blocks, and separation zones.

## Metadata

- Collection: Interfaces and Information Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: aviation, coordination, congestion, and real-time operations

## Signature Features

- Keep a dark circular scope, rotating sweep, calibrated range rings, aircraft tags, vectors, and protected separation zones.
- Update plots at radar cadence, coast stale tracks, and project conflicts forward before resolving them.
- For **Air-Traffic Control Radar**, use a near-black, slate, or warm-white operational surface with one semantic accent and color reserved for status, confidence, or ownership.

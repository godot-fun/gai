# Broadcast News Package

## Overview

A complete coded newsroom identity joins stings, bugs, lower thirds, story rundowns, and full-frame fact graphics. Choose this for a complete reusable channel identity: stings, lower thirds, bugs, rundowns, and multiple story formats.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: bulletins, institutional updates, and recurring factual series
- Complexity: Advanced

## Signature Features

- One consistent on-air package system.
- Persistent but quiet lower-third zone.
- Reusable full-frame graphic families.
- Cutting rules as important as visual styling.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Broadcast News Package** style. Design the sting, channel bug, straps, maps, quote cards, price boards, and spec graphics from one grid and palette. Keep the frame clean and make every graphic enter, update, and leave according to a consistent broadcast grammar.

Translate the user's material into this grammar rather than copying the source examples. Preserve supplied wording and factual attribution, and choose an HTML-native or composited implementation appropriate to the evidence and visual material.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, evidence, copy, duration, aspect ratio, pacing, and implementation method.
- Adapt palette details only when the defining visual hierarchy remains intact.

## Avoid

- Choose **Caption News** for sound-off social reporting, **Urgent News Broadcast** for one breaking event, or **News Brief Roundup** for several short items.
- News crawls, burned-in subtitles, unrelated one-off graphics, overbuilt 3D globes, or packaging louder than the facts.
- Never invent documents, measurements, quotations, sources, or factual evidence.
- Do not use surface effects as a substitute for the style's narrative structure.

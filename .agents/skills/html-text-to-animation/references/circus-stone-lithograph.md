# Circus Stone Lithograph

## Overview

A crowded nineteenth-century spectacle poster printed from four emphatic stones.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Chrome yellow, vermilion, cobalt, and black on warm cream
- Heavy contours, crescent shadows, hatch, and slight plate drift
- Exaggerated perspective converging on simultaneous action
- Readable miniature crowds rather than dots
- Warm orange focal subject built by overprinting solids

## Style Prompt

Stage several feats at once inside a compressed circus-poster composition. Draw every principal form with a stout black contour and model it using a darker crescent plus sparse hatch, assuming upper-left light. Print only chrome yellow, vermilion, cobalt, and black on warm cream stock; derive orange, green, brown, ochre, pale blue, and grey through overprint or light screens. Keep most areas solid and reserve visible screens for sky, skin, or ground transitions. Offset the stones slightly and preserve lithographic grit, uneven pressure, and edge wobble. Use forced perspective and radiating architecture, ropes, limbs, flags, or sightlines to drive attention toward the central feat. Populate stands and rings with many individually readable silhouettes and gestures rather than circular crowd texture. Build the focal performer as a flat orange overprint of yellow and vermilion, never a pink screen. Tilt upward through the bill, pan across parallel attractions, and cut at the crest of motion.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

colors outside the four-stone logic, generic dot crowds, clean vector curves, and quiet centered symmetry.

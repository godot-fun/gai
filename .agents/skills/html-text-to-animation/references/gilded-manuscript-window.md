# Gilded Manuscript Window

## Overview

A parchment page where raised gold, Gothic text, miniature action, and mischievous marginalia form one illuminated narrative system. Choose this when gold leaf, jeweled borders, and an illuminated-page reveal dominate the composition.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Parchment, brown ink, lapis blue, vermilion, and true raised gold leaf
- Punched dots and moving candle highlight only on gold
- Miniature window beside Gothic text columns and a large initial
- Celtic interlace, ivy borders, and marginal creatures
- Layered miniature depth with occasional figure breaking the frame

## Style Prompt

Fill the view with a cropped page or open manuscript spread on warm parchment. Use brown ink contours, opaque lapis blue and vermilion, and real gilding built on raised gesso. Punch tiny ornamental dots into the gold and move one warm candle reflection slowly across only the metallic surface; never simulate gold with flat yellow. Let gilded areas occupy a meaningful part of the page, and use lapis plus gold stars for night scenes. Paint Gothic figures in flat opaque color with ink detail, avoiding glossy highlights on faces. Arrange the miniature as a framed window covering about half a page beside a column of story text in Latin or the user's language, written with a broad-nib Gothic hand, red-and-blue line fillers, and one large gilded initial. Surround it with Celtic knotwork, ivy, flowers, and humorous marginal beasts. Build the miniature in shallow layers for parallax during the push and allow one figure or banner to cross its border. Keep upper-left candle warmth, pigment grain, and a brown vignette.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

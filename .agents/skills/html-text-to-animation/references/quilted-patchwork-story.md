# Quilted Patchwork Story

## Overview

Fabric blocks from distinct sources join through seams, batting, and stitched paths into one shared narrative.

## Metadata

- Collection: Print, Textile and Physical Craft
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: community, memory, family, and collective construction
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Arrange distinct fabric blocks with seam allowance, batting loft, joins, and a unifying quilting path.
- Piece blocks into rows, press seams, assemble the top, then stitch a continuous pattern that crosses block boundaries.
- For **Quilted Patchwork Story**, let tool marks, joins, surface response, and small process variation provide material truth; choose colors plausible for the named craft.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust motif, material scale, tool visibility, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between workshop process and finished artifact.
- Change motif, plausible material colors, work-surface context, and camera distance while preserving the craft's actual construction order.

## Avoid

- Avoid flat collage, unrelated scrapbook scraps, perfect cloned fabric, or patch motion with no sewing logic.
- Do not apply craft texture as a surface filter; show how tools, pressure, fibers, joins, or pigment actually produce it.
- Keep essential text outside dense movement and preserve a clear reduced-motion end state.

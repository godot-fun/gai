# Ocean Current Simulation

## Overview

Seeded particles follow layered currents, eddies, and boundaries across a rotating ocean surface.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: climate, transport, ecology, and planetary systems

## Signature Features

- Map a globe or regional basin with land masks, bathymetry hints, and seeded paths colored by a meaningful scalar such as speed or temperature.
- Advect particles through coherent gyres and eddies, preserve boundary flow, and change seasons slowly; use an orthographic or gently rotating planetary camera.
- Use quiet cartographic labels and a compact legend in ocean-safe high contrast.

# Cursor Typing and Erasure

## Overview

A private thought is typed, reconsidered, erased, and replaced in real time.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: unsaid feelings, internal monologue, hesitation, and confession
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- A visible cursor anchors authorship and rhythm.
- Typing, pauses, deletion, and replacement carry equal narrative weight.
- The final wording reveals what the speaker can or cannot say.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Cursor Typing and Erasure** style. Use a nearly empty dark frame and one carefully placed line of type. Type at a human cadence, pause around emotionally difficult words, delete faster than typing, and hold long enough for each revision to register.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining behavior above and keep all supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject matter, on-screen wording, duration, aspect ratio, typeface, and accent color.
- Rendering may use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL according to the needs of the effect.

## Avoid

- Constant machine-gun typing, fake terminal chrome, random corrections, or a cursor that distracts after the final hold.
- Do not reproduce historical sample copy or hard-coded layouts from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice readability for motion density.

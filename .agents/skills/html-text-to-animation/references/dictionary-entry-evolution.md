# Dictionary Entry Evolution

## Overview

Headword, pronunciation, senses, quotations, and etymology change across historical editions.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: language, definitions, cultural change, and semantic history
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Maintain headword, pronunciation, part of speech, numbered senses, citations, and dated etymology in a disciplined reference layout.
- Move across editions chronologically, highlight additions and shifts, and preserve prior senses for comparison rather than overwriting them.
- For **Dictionary Entry Evolution**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Dictionary Entry Evolution** style. Maintain headword, pronunciation, part of speech, numbered senses, citations, and dated etymology in a disciplined reference layout. Move across editions chronologically, highlight additions and shifts, and preserve prior senses for comparison rather than overwriting them. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.
- Change typographic scale, neutral surface, accent color, and motion amplitude while preserving valid glyphs, reading order, timing, and decodability.

## Avoid

- Avoid fake quotations, decorative dictionary paper, random definitions, modern meanings projected backward, or animated page flips without semantic change.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

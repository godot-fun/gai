# Forced-Perspective Set

## Overview

Scaled objects and converging geometry create impossible distance or size from a fixed camera position.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: scale, cinema, illusion, and playful transformation

## Signature Features

- Use scaled props, converging floors and ceilings, controlled sightlines, and matched lighting around one fixed camera.
- Introduce performers at calibrated depth, preserve their marks, and reveal the trick only with a final side move.
- For **Forced-Perspective Set**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Forced-Perspective Set** style. Use scaled props, converging floors and ceilings, controlled sightlines, and matched lighting around one fixed camera. Introduce performers at calibrated depth, preserve their marks, and reveal the trick only with a final side move. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid digital scaling, subjects crossing forbidden depth zones, handheld camera drift, mismatched focus, or geometry that works from multiple views.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

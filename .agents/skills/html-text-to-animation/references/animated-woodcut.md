# Animated Woodcut

## Overview

A black relief block appears to carve itself, creating light only where the knife removes material. Choose this when carving away a black relief block and revealing the print process are the central animation.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: myth, labor, conflict, elemental nature, and severe dramatic passages

## Signature Features

- Black mass is the starting material.
- White marks read as carved-away light.
- Cuts follow form and energy.
- At most one spot color marks the burning focus.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Animated Woodcut** style. Reveal the image through directional gouges, broad cleared planes, and dense surviving black. Animate carving order so the subject emerges from darkness rather than being drawn on top.

## Avoid

- Choose **Antiwar Expressionist Woodcut** for anguished political imagery, or **Woodcut Letterpress Rally** for poster activism and type.
- Grey pencil shading, smooth vector contours, many colors, photographic texture, or white marks with no relationship to carving.

# X-Ray Botanical Study

## Overview

Leaves, stems, seeds, and flowers become translucent radiographs that reveal internal veins and layered density.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: botany, fragility, anatomy, and poetic science
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Lay translucent specimens on a luminous cool-gray plate; map tissue density from delicate leaf veins to opaque seeds without retaining ordinary surface color.
- Reveal specimens by exposure, rotate or separate layers gently, and compare density with a fixed orthographic camera.
- Use fine archival serif captions and specimen numbers in dark gray, leaving anatomy unobstructed.

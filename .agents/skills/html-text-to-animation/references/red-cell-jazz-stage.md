# Red-Cell Jazz Stage

## Overview

A black-and-signal-red revue built from backlit cells, silhouette choreography, incandescent signage, and a single warm-white lead.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Roughly forty percent signal-red light against black
- Rows of railed cells glowing sequentially through smoke
- Solid black dancers with narrow red rim light
- One white-hot orange-red letter or symbol reflected in lacquer
- Only the lead retains local color inside a warm spotlight

## Style Prompt

Construct a theatrical black void with a row or stack of dark railed cells. Backlight each cell through smoke in signal red, brightest at center and falling to black at the edges, then add a deep red curtain. Keep red across roughly two fifths of the image and prevent any extra palette from diluting it. Choreograph dancers as full-bodied solid black silhouettes with distinctive costume outlines and a thin red rim; add sequins or white gloves only as small accents. Place one oversized neon letter, word fragment, number, or graphic symbol on a black field, with an orange-red tube, white-hot core, mild flicker, and reflection on a black lacquer floor. Run warm marquee bulbs along the stage edge. Put the lead inside a warm-white spotlight as the only fully illuminated, locally colored person. Snap the cells on in rhythmic sequence, cut or move on jazz accents, and drive the camera toward the final spotlight.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

even front lighting, rainbow color, silhouettes without rim separation, unreadable neon on red, excessive smoke, and choreography that ignores the musical pulse.

# Handwritten Pattern Window

## Overview

A warm interior study where handwritten formulas or notes accumulate on leaded glass and resolve into a small luminous relationship.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Translucent wax-marker strokes appearing by hand
- Writing confined to several small clusters on a quarter of the glass
- Soft warm world beyond square leaded panes
- Amber room frame with shelves and a green-shaded lamp
- Selected marks connected by restrained gold lines and traveling points

## Style Prompt

Make handwriting the primary visual subject without turning it into interface graphics. Draw translucent white wax-marker strokes one by one across a window of square leaded panes, using formulas, numbers, diagrams, names, or notes genuinely derived from the user's content. Confine them to several compact clusters covering roughly a quarter of the glass, leaving the exterior world visible and interpretable. Keep that world softly focused, warm-neutral, and middle-valued—overcast golds and muted greens—never brighter than the writing. Frame the window with a warm interior containing shelves, wood, a green-shaded lamp, and amber practical light. At the moment of insight, brighten only a few chosen symbols and join them with soft gold segments into a star, polygon, route, or causal pattern while small points travel along those lines. A chalk-on-dark-green-slate variant may use erased ghost writing. Push slowly toward the glass and make each cut closer to the hand or marks.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

HUD panels, typed fonts, writing across the entire pane, cold blue exteriors, arbitrary equations, neon glow, and connections that do not express a real relationship.

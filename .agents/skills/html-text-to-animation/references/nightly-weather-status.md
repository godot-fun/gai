# Nightly Weather Status

## Overview

A polished forecast broadcast converts the user's status into radar, map, gauges, multi-day strip, and sunny sign-off under consistent on-air chrome.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Video Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Satellite or radar spiral opening
- Map, thermometer, wind or speed gauge, and forecast strip
- Persistent original logo, date badge, and ticker
- Locked camera with motion inside graphics
- Fast diagonal wipes and musical hits at scene changes

## Style Prompt

Design an original nightly weather package and map the user's information to weather metaphors only when the mapping stays clear. Open with a satellite or radar-style spiral, then move through a regional status map, thermometer-like magnitude, wind or speed gauge, precipitation or risk field, and a multi-period forecast strip. Keep a channel pill, live date badge, and concise scrolling ticker consistent across scenes. Lock the camera and let maps, icons, snow, stars, wind lines, bars, and numbers animate inside each composition. Use bright broadcast color, clean rounded panels, a strong hierarchy, and fast diagonal wipes timed to scene-change accents. Conclude with a clear sign-off state such as sun, clearing conditions, or a direct warning.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

imitating a real channel, false meteorological claims, decorative gauges without units, a ticker unrelated to the scene, camera movement, too many icon styles, unreadable maps, and playful visuals that soften a genuinely serious warning.

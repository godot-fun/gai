## Overview

A neon outrun title world combining moving perspective grid, striped sunset disk, chrome typography, mountains, and controlled analog tape damage. Choose this for deliberately retro title graphics built from VHS artifacts, horizon grids, and magenta–cyan nostalgia.

## Signature Features

- One-point neon grid scrolling toward the viewer
- Layered sunset disk and dark mountain silhouette
- High-contrast chrome title with bevel and reflected color
- Tight inner and broad outer glow
- Scan lines, small RGB split, rare tracking bends, and brief frame stutter

## Style Prompt

Build a central horizon with a one-point perspective grid whose longitudinal motion loops seamlessly toward camera. Place a large striped or layered sunset disk behind a dark angular mountain or skyline silhouette. Use a narrow palette of deep navy, magenta, violet, cyan, acid accent, and warm sunset color. Reveal one short original title in high-contrast chrome with beveled highlights and reflected horizon colors; use tight bright glow near edges and a broader faint bloom outside. Add sparse stars, lens streaks, and simple foreground travel only if they reinforce depth. Finish as an analog tape: subtle scan lines, one-to-two-pixel red-blue separation, occasional horizontal tracking bend, slight noise, and rare brief frame stutter. Keep damage intermittent and the title readable.

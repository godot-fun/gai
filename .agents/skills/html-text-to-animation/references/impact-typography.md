# Impact Typography

## Overview

Short words strike the frame one beat at a time, turning spoken emphasis into visual force. Choose this when a few short words strike the frame as forceful beat-level events.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: emotional release, quotable lines, chorus moments, and bold announcements
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- One word or phrase dominates each beat.
- Scale, crop, weight, and entry direction reinforce meaning.
- Earlier words recede while the newest impact takes control.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Impact Typography** style. Build each beat around a decisive arrival with brief anticipation, controlled overshoot, and a readable hold. Keep a strict underlying grid, high contrast, and one accent reserved for the emotional turn.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining behavior above and keep all supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject matter, on-screen wording, duration, aspect ratio, typeface, and accent color.
- Rendering may use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL according to the needs of the effect.

## Avoid

- Choose **Lyric Beat Typography** for continuous music-synchronized lyrics, **Word-Synced Narration** for complete spoken prose, or **Typographic Architecture** for a final monumental statement.
- Long paragraphs, arbitrary bouncing, many fonts, continuous camera shake, or impacts with no relationship to rhythm.
- Do not reproduce historical sample copy or hard-coded layouts from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice readability for motion density.

# Caption News

## Overview

A sound-off-first news format in which large captions lead and footage, documents, and source tags supply evidence. Choose this when large captions must carry the story without sound while footage and source tags remain supporting evidence.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: feed-native explainers, sourced facts, and concise public-interest stories

## Signature Features

- One short caption idea at a time.
- A single highlighted keyword per beat.
- Every number paired with its supplied source.
- Real evidence shown before interpretation.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Caption News** style. Open on the strongest verified fact, cut on a firm rhythm, and let captions cross picture changes when reading time requires it. Use original documents, restrained data cards, and clear footage credits as part of one editorial grid.

## Avoid

- Choose **Broadcast News Package** for a full channel system, or **Urgent News Broadcast** for live urgency.
- Invented sources, anchor desks, scrolling tickers, dashboard clutter, or captions too long to read.

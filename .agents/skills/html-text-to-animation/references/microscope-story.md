# Microscope Story

## Overview

Real microscopy and one continuous scale journey carry the viewer from a specimen in hand to a single cellular structure.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: biology, materials science, medical education, and hidden worlds

## Signature Features

- Licence-clean real imagery.
- Continuous macro-to-micro scale logic.
- Focus breathes with magnification.
- Scale bars and readings come from metadata.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Microscope Story** style. Anchor every change of magnification to a real field of view, sweep the focal plane after landing, and preserve specimen color and texture. Use labels sparingly and keep scale visible.

## Avoid

- Invented microscopic imagery, impossible zoom continuity, decorative particles, missing scale bars, or focus effects unrelated to optics.

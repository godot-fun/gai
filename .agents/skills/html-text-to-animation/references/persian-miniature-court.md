# Persian Miniature Court

## Overview

Flattened architecture, jewel color, patterned gardens, and multiple simultaneous episodes create an intricate painted world.

## Metadata

- Collection: Performance, Ritual and Living Culture
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: poetry, courts, gardens, and layered narrative space

## Signature Features

- Layer flattened architecture, gardens, textiles, jewel color, fine contour, and multiple episodes without Western depth hierarchy.
- Reveal the page by compositional zones, animate restrained gestures and environmental details, and keep the camera near-orthographic.
- For **Persian Miniature Court**, treat stage, material, performer, audience viewpoint, and timing as one coherent form; use culturally specific features only when they are verified and relevant.

# Minimal Geometric Motion

## Overview

Circles, squares, and semicircles explain an idea through weight, balance, and transformation.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: clarifying complex concepts, brand motion, and abstract explanation
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- A very small shape vocabulary carries the whole sequence.
- Every movement expresses a relationship or operation.
- Flat color and a strict alignment system create clarity.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Minimal Geometric Motion** style. Use a warm neutral field, a compact primary palette, and strong scale contrast. Let forms roll, fall, grow, divide, align, and recombine with physically legible easing.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining behavior above and keep all supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject matter, on-screen wording, duration, aspect ratio, typeface, and accent color.
- Rendering may use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL according to the needs of the effect.

## Avoid

- Decorative shape noise, gradients without purpose, arbitrary bouncing, heavy shadows, or inconsistent geometry.
- Do not reproduce historical sample copy or hard-coded layouts from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice readability for motion density.

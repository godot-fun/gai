# Technical Field Manual

## Overview

A warm-paper military-style instruction page that combines a one-point operational scene, exploded mechanisms, numbered leaders, warnings, and limited olive-and-sand wash.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A warm-paper military-style instruction page that combines a one-point operational scene, exploded mechanisms, numbered leaders, warnings, and limited olive-and-sand wash.

## Signature Features

- Fine black contour and hatch over a faint engineering grid
- Stippled ground and no gradient shading
- Olive and sand flats confined to a minority of the page
- Exploded views, dash-dot axes, circled callouts, parts table, and warning boxes
- Two-frame animation cadence and cuts from full page to enlarged detail

## Style Prompt

Lay out a complete technical-manual page on warm off-white stock over a very faint engineering grid. Draw in one consistent black ink: fine outlines, finer hatch for value, stipple for ground, and radial lines for illumination rather than halos. Limit flat olive and sand color to roughly one quarter of the image. Let the main one-point scene occupy most of the page and include both people and task-specific equipment. Around it, add exploded assemblies separated along dash-dot axes, circled numbers on leader lines, a parts list, FIG labels, a black chapter tab, and clearly differentiated WARNING and CAUTION boxes. Write concise meaningful labels in the user's language with typewriter-like lettering, not placeholder jargon. Animate on twos: components slide apart, tools operate step by step, and figures walk through the procedure. Begin on the full page, then cut to one or more enlarged illustration details that preserve the same ink language.

## Avoid

gradients, many colors, decorative fonts, empty margins, fake unreadable text, modern glass UI, and diagrams unrelated to the actual operation.

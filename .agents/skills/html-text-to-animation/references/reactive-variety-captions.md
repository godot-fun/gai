# Reactive Variety Captions

## Overview

An energetic caption layer for vlog, interview, comedy, or reaction footage, using thick multi-outline type and emotion-specific hand-drawn symbols.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Graphics Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Rounded heavy lettering with fill, white keyline, colored outer stroke, and shadow
- Eight-frame overshoot pop or two-frame-per-character stagger
- Color and shape matched to the speaker's emotion
- Burst frames, sweat marks, sparkles, question signs, and motion lines
- Frame-synced pops, boings, dings, and comic pauses

## Style Prompt

Use captions as timed reactions to a real visible beat, not as continuous subtitles. Set each short phrase in a rounded heavy face or hand-drawn alternative appropriate to the emotion. Build the standard stack from saturated fill, thick white keyline, colored outer stroke or offset shadow, and enough contrast to survive the footage. Enter with scale from zero through a modest overshoot to rest in about eight frames, or bounce characters in with a two-frame stagger. Tilt and distort teasing remarks, surround surprise with radial bursts, use sweat marks for awkwardness, sparkles for delight, and clustered question signs for confusion. Position captions near the relevant person or object without covering faces, hands, or action, and remove them as soon as the reaction resolves. Sync entrances and accents to concise sound cues.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

captioning every spoken word, uncontrolled rainbow palettes, tiny outlines, generic decorations, repeated identical pop motion, real network branding, and text that obscures the underlying footage.

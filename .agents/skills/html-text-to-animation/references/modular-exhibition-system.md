# Modular Exhibition System

## Overview

Panels, plinths, lights, labels, and partitions reconfigure into distinct layouts from one kit of parts.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: museums, events, adaptability, and design systems

## Signature Features

- Use a limited kit of panels, rails, plinths, lights, and connectors on a measured gallery grid.
- Disassemble and reconfigure the same parts into distinct layouts, keeping inventory visible and respecting circulation.
- For **Modular Exhibition System**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Modular Exhibition System** style. Use a limited kit of panels, rails, plinths, lights, and connectors on a measured gallery grid. Disassemble and reconfigure the same parts into distinct layouts, keeping inventory visible and respecting circulation. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid new parts appearing for each scene, furniture teleportation, impossible wall spans, decorative clutter, or inaccessible paths.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

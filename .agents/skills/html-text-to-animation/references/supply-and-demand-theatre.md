# Supply-and-Demand Theatre

## Overview

Quantity and price become a responsive stage where buyers, sellers, shortages, and surpluses visibly negotiate an equilibrium.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: supply, demand, shortages, surpluses, equilibrium, and price formation

## Signature Features

- Keep price and quantity scales stable while supply or demand shifts for an explicit reason.
- Connect every curve movement to visible buyer or seller behavior, then show the resulting shortage, surplus, or equilibrium.
- Use restrained economic notation, clear units, and distinct but accessible colors for supply and demand.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Supply-and-Demand Theatre** style. Treat the price-quantity plane as a living stage: cohorts of buyers and sellers enter, withdraw, or change willingness, shifting one curve while the other remains traceable. Animate the gap between quantity supplied and demanded before price converges toward a labeled equilibrium. Keep axes, units, causes, and counterfactual states readable throughout.

## Avoid

- Avoid moving both curves without identifying separate causes or presenting equilibrium as an automatic moral optimum.
- Do not replace the market mechanism with decorative chart pulsing, stock tickers, or unrelated dashboard panels.

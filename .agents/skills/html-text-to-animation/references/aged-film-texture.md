# Aged Film Texture

## Overview

Grain, flicker, scratches, and edge leaks give a sequence the tactile instability of projected film.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: memory, archival passages, old times, and visual continuity across mixed material
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Grain changes organically from frame to frame.
- Exposure flicker and gate movement remain subtle.
- Dust, scratches, vignette, and light leaks appear intermittently rather than constantly.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Aged Film Texture** style. Treat aging as a coherent finishing layer: fine grain, restrained exposure variation, slight vertical weave, sparse dust, occasional hairline scratches, soft vignette, and rare edge leaks. Match intensity to the emotional age of the material.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining behavior above and keep all supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject matter, on-screen wording, duration, aspect ratio, typeface, and accent color.
- Rendering may use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL according to the needs of the effect.

## Avoid

- Heavy damage covering the subject, constant scratches, repeated noise loops, fake timestamps, modern glitch effects, or sepia applied indiscriminately.
- Do not reproduce historical sample copy or hard-coded layouts from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice readability for motion density.

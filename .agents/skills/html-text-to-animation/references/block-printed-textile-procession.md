# Block-Printed Textile Procession

## Overview

A narrative cotton hanging printed block by block with natural dyes, border ornament, and visible seams in repeated color fields.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Unbleached cotton as dominant ground and skin
- Indigo, madder red, turmeric yellow, and carved near-black
- Two-to-four-pixel block drift and dye bleed along warp and weft
- Paisley-and-vine border with scalloped waves
- Profile figures with miniature-style faces

## Style Prompt

Treat the entire image as an unbleached hand-printed cotton hanging, leaving more than half of the cloth visible as ground and using it for skin. Print indigo, madder red, turmeric yellow, and a near-black carved key block in successive hand-sized impressions. Let neighboring impressions meet imperfectly: registration slips, broad color bands shift slightly at block seams, pressure varies, and dye feathers along warp and weft. Restrict fine mud-resist crackle to indigo areas and keep it sparse. Draw outlines as visibly carved, slightly rough edges rather than smooth vector paths. Compose a lateral procession or episode enclosed by a border of paisley, flowers, and vines; translate water or rhythmic ground into scalloped textile bands. Show people in profile with black-extended eyes, distinct nose, hands, clothing, and miniature-like facial detail. Reserve madder red most strongly for the focal subject. Pan along the hanging, push into one block-sized episode, and use a rotating flower or paisley unit to mask transitions.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

hard digital edges, perfect registration, faceless silhouettes, crackle over the whole cloth, dominant indigo, and faux fabric texture pasted above unrelated drawing.

# Social-Realist Tempera

## Overview

A humane, uneasy tempera illustration with trembling ink, displaced color blocks, and worn working-class forms.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Open contours drawn in two or three nervous passes
- Faint offset ghost lines beside selected edges
- Rough tempera blocks displaced from ink
- Dry-brush drag inside roughly one third of each color field
- Large heads, hands, long faces, heavy jaws, and hanging clothing

## Style Prompt

Draw figures with a thin ink contour that combines small tremor with a slower sway. Build an edge from two or three incomplete passes rather than one closed shape, occasionally leaving a pale transfer ghost beside it. Lay opaque tempera color two to four pixels away from the drawing, with fibrous edges and broad dry-brush drag across part of each field. Shade only with one harder, darker version of the same local color. Restrict the world to grey-blue, brick red, ochre, warm off-white, and near-black, mixing skin and secondary hues from this family; reserve the clearest warm accent for the lead garment. Give people large expressive hands, slightly oversized heads, long faces, weighty jaws, and clothing that hangs from tired bodies. Let setting details—brick, dock equipment, fences, rooms—remain specific but never polished. Use short pushes, a measured upward tilt, and finish close enough to read the face.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

gradients, cheerful saturated color, sealed vector contours, decorative distress, and heroic idealization.

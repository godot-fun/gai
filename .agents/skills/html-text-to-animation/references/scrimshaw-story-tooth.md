# Scrimshaw Story Tooth

## Overview

A wide polished whale-tooth artifact carries a densely engraved folk narrative, restrained hand color, maritime ornament, and subtly animated waves.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A wide polished whale-tooth artifact carries a densely engraved folk narrative, restrained hand color, maritime ornament, and subtly animated waves.

## Signature Features

- Broad flattened tooth on oak table with ink bottle and folding knife
- Fine needle hatch, swelling contour, and stipple over most of the surface
- Formal repeated waves, rope border, banner, and radiant sun
- Warm ivory, growth lines, polish scratches, and honeyed tip
- Large hand-colored lead with red, blue, and yellow accents

## Style Prompt

Present a broad, flattened whale-tooth artifact horizontally on an oak table, occupying most of the image, with an ink bottle and folding knife nearby for scale. Preserve warm ivory color, faint growth lines, polish scratches, a honey-yellow tip, and edge shading plus one window reflection that describes curvature without warping the engraving. Incise roughly seventy percent of the face with near-black ink: hair-fine parallel hatch, pressure-varied contours, stipple, dense sea and land, and lighter sky. Translate water into repeated formal wave rows. Keep the lead large enough to occupy about one third of the engraved height and hand-color it selectively; use red, blue, and yellow only for small supporting accents. Enclose the scene with a rope border, include a ribbon inscription in the user's language, a radiant sun, and subject-specific folk motifs. Begin on the whole artifact, push close, then track slowly across its surface as only waves, flags, or a small gesture come alive.

## Avoid

thick lines, solid color fills, horn-like narrow shapes, distorted edge projection, full-color illustration, and engraving too sparse to hold detail.

# Sand-Table Chronicle

## Overview

A story told across eras by dark sand poured onto a luminous table, shaped into scenes, illuminated by changing skies, then broken apart and blown onward.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Video Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation

## Signature Features

- Dark granular sand on warm glowing ground
- Hands or implied tools drawing, pouring, brushing, and erasing
- Sunbursts, night fields, silhouettes, and layered terrain
- Grain disintegration as the primary time transition
- Music and tactile pour, scrape, wind, and impact cues

## Style Prompt

Treat the frame as a real backlit sand table. Build each era by pouring broad dark masses, pushing edges with fingers or tools, scratching fine light lines through the sand, and tapping granules for texture. Use density, not added color, to separate sky, land, people, structures, and atmosphere. Let a radial light opening become a sun, a dense field become night, and loose grains create smoke, snow, stars, or crowds. Hold each completed tableau long enough to read, then disturb it physically: a wind front lifts the top layer, a hand sweeps one region, or the scene collapses into migrating grains that settle into the next era. Preserve residual grains so history feels cumulative. Sync major pours and dissolves to music and tactile sound.

## Avoid

digital particle clouds that ignore gravity, clean vector silhouettes, rapid cuts, multicolor sand, identical dissolve transitions, blank table between scenes, and hands obscuring the finished image.

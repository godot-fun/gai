# Light-and-Space Installation

## Overview

Precisely bounded light alters the perceived depth, edge, and color of an otherwise minimal room.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: perception, contemplation, galleries, and spatial color

## Signature Features

- Use an almost empty room, immaculate planes, one luminous aperture or color field, and subtle adaptation at boundaries.
- Change intensity or hue very slowly so perceived depth shifts; keep the camera on a deliberate human-height path.
- For **Light-and-Space Installation**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Light-and-Space Installation** style. Use an almost empty room, immaculate planes, one luminous aperture or color field, and subtle adaptation at boundaries. Change intensity or hue very slowly so perceived depth shifts; keep the camera on a deliberate human-height path. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid busy sculpture, visible projector graphics, rapid color cycling, lens flare, or explanatory text dominating the room.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

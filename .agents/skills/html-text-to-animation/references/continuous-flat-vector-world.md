# Continuous Flat-Vector World

## Overview

A high-energy brand explainer where one geometric world flows into the next through object-led transitions, disciplined color, and elastic staggered motion.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Graphics Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A high-energy brand explainer where one geometric world flows into the next through object-led transitions, disciplined color, and elastic staggered motion.

## Signature Features

- Five or six bold flat colors with no gradients
- Geometric people and objects with minimal or no outline
- One visual hero moving at a time
- Anticipation, ten-percent squash, overshoot, and follow-through
- Transitions carried by an exiting object or full-frame color shape

## Style Prompt

Reduce characters, environments, and ideas to clear vector geometry using a limited high-saturation palette plus cream and ink. Keep surfaces fully flat and use overlap, scale, and color rather than gradients to create hierarchy. Structure the sequence as one continuous visual journey: an object rolls, flies, expands, or wipes across frame and becomes the next scene, preserving direction and rhythm. Animate only one principal action at a time; stagger supporting entrances by a few frames. Give every arrival anticipation, modest squash or stretch, overshoot, and settle, then add secondary motion to hair, leaves, straps, or small props. Keep silhouettes readable, text brief, and negative space intentional. Build a decisive hero transformation around the latter middle of the sequence and hold the final composition with subtle living motion.

## Avoid

simultaneous movement everywhere, stock icon libraries, gradients, overcomplicated character rigs, random bounce on every element, transitions unrelated to the outgoing object, and elastic easing so strong that information becomes comic or unstable.

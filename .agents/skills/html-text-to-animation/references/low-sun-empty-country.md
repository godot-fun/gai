# Low-Sun Empty Country

## Overview

A nearly static realistic dusk landscape with a vast pale sky, sparse clustered vegetation, a backlit middle-distance figure, and one long shadow toward camera.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A nearly static realistic dusk landscape with a vast pale sky, sparse clustered vegetation, a backlit middle-distance figure, and one long shadow toward camera.

## Signature Features

- Sky occupying roughly three fifths of frame
- Dust-ochre horizon fading to pale cream above
- Sun one or two degrees above the horizon
- Hundreds of grey-green shrub clusters over dark warm earth
- Low fixed thirty-five-millimeter viewpoint

## Style Prompt

Build a restrained realistic 3D landscape at the final edge of daylight. Give the sky about sixty percent of the frame, moving from dust ochre near the horizon to pale cream above, with only a few long thin clouds lit from beneath. Place the sun one or two degrees above the horizon so it may clip but still casts a clear shadow and draws a narrow bright rim along distant ridges. Keep saturation low. Populate the ground with hundreds of small grey-green plant clusters receding in scale, plus rocks, dry grass, debris, or equivalent sparse repeated detail suited to the chosen biome; never leave the terrain as a bare plane. Put the lead backlit in the middle distance at about one third of frame height, its long shadow running toward the lens. Expose for the sky, allow the solar disk to overexpose, and limit halo to a small region. Use neutral tone mapping, warm highlights, fine grain, and mild vignette. Hold a low wide thirty-five-millimeter camera almost still.

## Avoid

saturated orange sunsets, giant white flare, barren ground, constant camera drift, crushed foreground black, and decorative clouds that compete with the figure.

# Pop Art Comic

## Overview

Primary colors, Ben-Day dots, speech bubbles, and bold outlines turn a transition into a graphic exclamation. Choose this for isolated graphic impact—speech bursts, primary color, and Ben-Day exclamation—rather than sustained panel storytelling.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: attention hooks, surprising turns, and playful emphasis
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Red-yellow-blue core palette.
- Bold black contour.
- Ben-Day dot fields.
- Speech or burst shapes.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Pop Art Comic** style. Build one iconic pop-art composition per beat and use a single bubble or burst as the focal device. Keep text short and monumental.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Choose **Halftone American Comic** for action sequences, or **Newspaper Four-Panel Comic** for dialogue-led timing.
- Many speech bubbles, muddy colors, realistic shading, generic stickers, or dots at every scale.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

# Impasto Oil Painting

## Overview

Palette-knife planes of thick oil color are raked by light so physical ridges support a strong value structure. Choose this for a materially observed painting process in which palette-knife ridges and raking light are the subject.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: emotional climaxes, portraits, dramatic landscapes, and material transformation
- Complexity: Advanced

## Signature Features

- Broad sharp-edged color planes.
- Visible paint ridges under raking light.
- Value structure precedes surface texture.
- Hard edges concentrate at the focus.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Impasto Oil Painting** style. Block the scene into large value masses, lay thick directional strokes where form turns, and reserve the highest relief and hardest contrast for the focal subject. Keep surrounding planes calm.

Translate the user's material into this grammar rather than copying the source examples. Preserve supplied wording and factual attribution, and choose an HTML-native or composited implementation appropriate to the evidence and visual material.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, evidence, copy, duration, aspect ratio, pacing, and implementation method.
- Adapt palette details only when the defining visual hierarchy remains intact.

## Avoid

- Choose **Impasto Painting** for a more general illustrated paint language, or **Technicolor Impasto Epic** for cinematic spectacle.
- Uniform texture everywhere, liquid paint simulation, photo detail under a filter, tiny fussy strokes, or light that ignores paint relief.
- Never invent documents, measurements, quotations, sources, or factual evidence.
- Do not use surface effects as a substitute for the style's narrative structure.

# Marbled-Paper Flow

## Overview

Pigment floats on thickened water, is combed into veins, and transfers once onto absorbent paper.

## Metadata

- Collection: Print, Textile and Physical Craft
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media

- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Use a shallow tray, floating pigment drops, comb teeth, organic veins, and an absorbent sheet with fibrous texture.
- Drop colors sequentially, rake them once into a controlled pattern, lay paper from one edge, and lift without looping the transfer.

# Making-of Scrapbook

## Overview

A tactile project journal on a kraft desk, combining ruled notes, taped instant photos, torn labels, stickers, stamps, and marker annotations into a chronological process.

- Rubber stamps, die-cut stickers, and marker doodles
- Chronological build from idea to outcome
- Hands, page turns, tape peels, and camera pushes

## Style Prompt

Present the subject as a making-of record rather than a decorative memory collage. Arrange ruled notebook pages, instant photos, sketches, receipts, samples, checklists, and torn labels on a kraft desk. Attach photos with patterned washi tape, use rubber stamps for dates or status, die-cut stickers for repeated categories, and marker doodles for arrows, circles, faces, and corrections. Give each spread one stage of the process—brief, research, attempt, problem, revision, result—and make text short enough to read. Animate through physical actions: a page turns, a hand places a photo, tape peels down, a stamp lands, marker strokes appear, and the camera pushes into evidence before pulling back to the updated page. Keep shadows, paper thickness, and overlap consistent.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

random lifestyle decoration, duplicated stock photos, perfectly aligned grids, floating digital cards, illegible handwriting, too many sticker styles, and a scrapbook that reports no progression.

# Phonetic Wave Typography

## Overview

Syllables and phonemes follow measured speech energy, pitch, duration, and articulation. Choose this when phonemes, pitch, duration, and articulation shape the typography at sub-word resolution.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: voice, pronunciation, music, and accessible narration
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Segment real speech into phonemes or syllables and bind text size, duration, vertical gesture, and spacing to measured articulation.
- Advance with the audio timeline, distinguish sustained vowels from transient consonants, and allow pauses to create visible silence.
- For **Phonetic Wave Typography**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Phonetic Wave Typography** style. Segment real speech into phonemes or syllables and bind text size, duration, vertical gesture, and spacing to measured articulation. Advance with the audio timeline, distinguish sustained vowels from transient consonants, and allow pauses to create visible silence. Build hierarchy from glyph anatomy, spacing, reading direction, linguistic timing, and the specific encoding or word structure being demonstrated. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.
- Change typographic scale, neutral surface, accent color, and motion amplitude while preserving valid glyphs, reading order, timing, and decodability.

## Avoid

- Choose **Word-Synced Narration** when semantic words and sentences—not acoustic detail—lead the animation.
- Avoid generic audio equalizers, per-letter bouncing, fabricated timing, every word at once, or typography detached from pronunciation.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

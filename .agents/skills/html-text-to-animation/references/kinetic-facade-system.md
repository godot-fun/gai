# Kinetic Facade System

## Overview

Thousands of facade modules open, rotate, or ripple in response to sun, wind, occupants, or data.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: responsive buildings, climate, public displays, and collective motion

## Signature Features

- Compose a rectilinear building face from repeated operable modules with clear pivots, daylight, and interior depth.
- Drive panels from a visible sun path, wind field, or data signal; propagate waves while preserving mechanical limits.
- For **Kinetic Facade System**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Kinetic Facade System** style. Compose a rectilinear building face from repeated operable modules with clear pivots, daylight, and interior depth. Drive panels from a visible sun path, wind field, or data signal; propagate waves while preserving mechanical limits. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid LED-screen substitution, panels rotating through walls, random ripples, impossible speed, or no relationship to environmental input.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

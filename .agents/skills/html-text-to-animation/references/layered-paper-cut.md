# Layered Paper Cut

## Overview

Visible cut edges, stacked silhouettes, and soft contact shadows create a tactile story world. Choose this for a general tactile world assembled from stacked cut silhouettes and contact shadows.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: narrative scenes, folklore, childhood, and gentle explanation
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Clearly separated paper layers.
- Visible cut edges.
- Restrained contact shadows.
- Planar parallax motion.

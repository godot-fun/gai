# Editorial Cutout Collage

## Overview

A tactile magazine collage of torn photos, paper shapes, type fragments, tape, and unexpected scale shifts animated as physical pieces. Choose this for controlled magazine-like cutouts, clear headline hierarchy, and deliberate negative space.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Graphics Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A tactile magazine collage of torn photos, paper shapes, type fragments, tape, and unexpected scale shifts animated as physical pieces. Choose this for controlled magazine-like cutouts, clear headline hierarchy, and deliberate negative space.

## Signature Features

- Visible torn or scissor-cut edges
- Mixed photographic, printed, handwritten, and painted sources
- Paper shadows, tape, staples, folds, and registration slips
- Surreal juxtaposition through scale and context
- Slide, hinge, flip, tear, replace, and stop-motion motion

## Style Prompt

Assemble each frame from physically distinct sources: clipped photographs, colored paper, maps, diagrams, printed headlines, hand marks, stamps, and painted fragments. Preserve every source's edge, paper tone, grain, and printing quality. Attach pieces with translucent tape, staples, glue stains, folds, or paper shadows, and let imperfect alignment remain visible. Create meaning through juxtaposition: enlarge a small object into landscape, replace a head with a symbol, make a road emerge from a sentence, or let one photograph open into another. Animate pieces as matter—slide them by hand, hinge them at tape, flip them over, tear away a layer, or replace them in stepped stop-motion increments. Keep one clear focal relationship per beat and use typography as an object, not an overlay.

## Avoid

- Choose **Mixed-Media Collage** when rough found texture and heterogeneous materials should remain visible.
clean digital masks, uniform stock-photo color, random scrapbook decoration, smooth floating 3D cards, excessive drop shadows, tiny unreadable scraps, and collage density without narrative logic.

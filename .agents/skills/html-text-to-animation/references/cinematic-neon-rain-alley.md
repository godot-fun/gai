## Style Prompt

Build a narrow street around one strict central vanishing point. Crowd both sides with vertically stacked signs, awnings, windows, pipes, cables, vents, and local clutter at several depth planes. Use cyan and blue-green for receding fog and reflective shadow, magenta or red only as controlled sign accents, and a concentrated warm amber destination at the far end. Let wet pavement fill nearly the lower half and mirror every major sign as elongated, broken color distorted by puddles, seams, footsteps, and rain rings. Illuminate rain only as it crosses real light cones. Move slowly forward at human height while near signs pass with strong parallax and distant architecture disappears into thicker cyan haze. Add steam, umbrellas, small vehicles, window activity, and rare irregular electrical flicker.

# Kintsugi Repair

## Overview

Broken ceramic fragments rejoin slowly while lacquered gold seams honor damage instead of hiding it.

## Metadata

- Collection: Print, Textile and Physical Craft
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: recovery, resilience, memory, and visible repair

## Signature Features

- Center a small set of broken ceramic fragments with real thickness, irregular fracture faces, dark lacquer, and restrained gold seams.
- Align fragments sequentially, allow lacquer joins to cure, dust gold onto wet seams, and rotate the repaired object in quiet light.
- For **Kintsugi Repair**, let tool marks, joins, surface response, and small process variation provide material truth; choose colors plausible for the named craft.

## Avoid

- Do not apply craft texture as a surface filter; show how tools, pressure, fibers, joins, or pigment actually produce it.

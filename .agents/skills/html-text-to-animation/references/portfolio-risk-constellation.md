# Portfolio Risk Constellation

## Overview

Assets orbit a portfolio center while allocation, correlation, volatility, and shocks reshape collective risk.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: allocation, correlation, volatility, diversification, and return

## Signature Features

- Encode allocation with mass, volatility with motion range, and correlation with explicit changing links.
- Show how shared shocks propagate and how diversification depends on relationships, not asset count.
- Keep return, risk, horizon, and scenario assumptions separated and consistently labeled.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Portfolio Risk Constellation** style. Arrange assets around a weighted portfolio center, using mass for allocation, bounded motion for volatility, and links for measured correlation. Apply named scenarios that move related assets together or apart, then update portfolio-level dispersion and return without erasing the individual paths. Favor analytical restraint and clear uncertainty over financial spectacle.

## Avoid

- Avoid guaranteed-return language, green-equals-good simplification, or random market-like jitter.
- Do not imply that more holdings automatically create diversification or hide the measurement period.

# Articulated Shadow-Puppet Stage

## Overview

A backlit hide-puppet performance uses pierced silhouettes, visible joints and rods, layered screen depth, and controlled colored translucency. Choose this for a culturally neutral constructed shadow-puppet mechanism where joints, rods, and stage engineering are the focus.

## Metadata

## Signature Features

- Translucent parchment screen and strong rear lamp
- Cut and pierced leather figures with articulated joints
- Visible control rods and hinge-driven motion
- Dark near silhouettes with restrained red, green, or amber translucent detail
- Layered scenery and performance-like entrances

## Style Prompt

Stage the story behind a warm translucent screen. Construct every character and prop as a flat cut leather puppet with pierced ornament, carved facial profile, colored translucent patches, and clear hinge joints at shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, jaw, wing, or tail as needed. Preserve visible control rods and let motion originate from those rods: limbs rotate around pins, bodies slide, and secondary parts lag mechanically. Place scenery in several flat layers at different distances from the lamp so edge sharpness and scale vary plausibly. Use dark brown-black silhouettes with only restrained red, green, amber, or blue transmitted color. Enter characters from the screen edge, perform readable profile gestures, and use a wipe, spinning prop, or scenery exchange for transitions.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

- Choose **Chinese Shadow Theatre**, **Wayang Kulit Shadow Performance**, or **Ottoman Shadow Comedy** when a specific performance tradition is intended.

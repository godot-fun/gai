# Phosphorescent Afterglow

## Overview

Light charges a surface and leaves a fading luminous memory after the source has moved away.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: memory, persistence, traces, and quiet transitions

## Signature Features

- Start with a matte dark-green or charcoal surface and a moving white excitation source; leave pale mint traces whose intensity records exposure time.
- Write, sweep, or mask with light, then remove the source and let every trace decay exponentially at its own age; keep the camera orthographic and still.
- Use simple rounded sans-serif lettering only when it is physically charged into the surface or placed outside it.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Phosphorescent Afterglow** style. Start with a matte dark-green or charcoal surface and a moving white excitation source; leave pale mint traces whose intensity records exposure time. Write, sweep, or mask with light, then remove the source and let every trace decay exponentially at its own age; keep the camera orthographic and still. Use simple rounded sans-serif lettering only when it is physically charged into the surface or placed outside it. Make every transition arise from the optical or scientific process itself, and preserve enough negative space for the supplied message to remain primary.

## Avoid

- Avoid additive neon tubes, traces that never fade, random particle tails, hard cuts between charged states, or equal brightness regardless of exposure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Bold Editorial Poster** style. Treat the headline as an image: crop it at the frame, stagger lines, and let italic motion sweep diagonally before locking to a strict baseline. Keep supporting copy small and exact.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Avoid

- Choose **Coral Condensed Poster** for tall direct sans-serif impact, or **Industrial Broadside** for dense newspaper-like authority.
- Many fonts, centered symmetry, soft floating motion, decorative gradients, or timid headline scale.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

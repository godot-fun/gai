# Victorian Paper Theatre

## Overview

A candlelit toy-stage world assembled from printed card layers, visible scissor borders, and exaggerated cast shadows.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Lithographed card figures and scenery with white cut edges
- Muted crimson, old gold, blue, greyed brown, and cream
- Parallel 2.5D flats with parallax but no internal perspective
- Flickering footlights casting enlarged soft shadows
- Gilded proscenium, red curtains, and painted audience boxes

## Style Prompt

Frame the entire action inside a Victorian toy theatre that occupies most of the image. Print black lithographic contours and sparse crayon hatch onto cream card, then hand-color with subdued crimson, tarnished gold, costume blue, greyed brown, and dusty neutrals. Preserve a narrow irregular white scissor margin around every actor, prop, and scenery flat. Arrange the pieces in parallel depth layers: each layer remains visibly flat while camera movement produces measured parallax; never bend them into full 3D perspective. Light the stage from candle footlights so each card casts an enlarged soft-edged shadow onto the layer behind, with size and intensity breathing as flames flicker. Enclose the world with gilded ornament, deep red curtains, and painted spectators in the boxes. Let figures move by plausible hidden tabs or pivots, retaining slight mechanical tremor. Push slowly through the proscenium, slide across layered action, allow a close view to reveal card wobble, then pull back to expose the dark room.

bright vector color, photographic softboxes, realistic depth, missing cut borders, and seamless digital puppetry.

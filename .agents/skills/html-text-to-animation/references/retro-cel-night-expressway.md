# Retro Cel Night Expressway

## Overview

An 1980s-to-1990s cel-animation chase through elevated roads, towering city angles, restrained light trails, and worn analog playback.

## Metadata

- Collection: Reference-Derived Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: An 1980s-to-1990s cel-animation chase through elevated roads, towering city angles, restrained light trails, and worn analog playback.

## Signature Features

- Hand-inked cel characters and vehicles over painted night backgrounds
- Low upward views of towers and road structures
- Red tail-light trails and fast foreground parallax
- Limited cel shadow steps and selective highlight streaks
- Subtle VHS chroma drift, scan noise, and tape instability

## Style Prompt

Draw moving vehicles and people as traditional cels with pressure-sensitive dark lines, flat local color, one hard shadow step, and a few sharply placed highlights. Paint the city separately with dense gouache-like windows, elevated roads, signs, barriers, and upward-looking architecture. Keep the night palette in deep navy, charcoal, sodium amber, restrained cyan, and red tail lights. Convey speed through rapidly passing rail posts, lane markings, road texture, hair, clothing, and two continuous tail-light streaks; keep the main vehicle readable rather than smearing the whole image. Use low oblique angles, tracking views, and brief upward reveals of buildings. Finish as an old tape playback with mild scan lines, slight red-blue channel misalignment, fine luma noise, rare horizontal disturbance, and modest softness—never enough to destroy the drawing.

## Avoid

modern vector cleanliness, full-frame glitch effects, neon rainbow cities, overly smooth 60fps motion, photoreal vehicles, and analog damage that changes every frame.

# Indigo Shibori Unfolding

## Overview

Bound and folded cloth opens after dyeing to reveal resist geometry, gradients, and mirrored surprises.

## Metadata

- Collection: Print, Textile and Physical Craft
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: transformation, textile craft, anticipation, and pattern

## Signature Features

- Begin with folded or bound natural cloth, visible ties and pressure points, then reveal deep indigo gradients around resisted areas.
- Bind, dip, oxidize from green to blue, repeat for depth, cut ties, and unfold symmetrically with a measured overhead camera.
- For **Indigo Shibori Unfolding**, let tool marks, joins, surface response, and small process variation provide material truth; choose colors plausible for the named craft.

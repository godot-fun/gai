# One-Point Title Grid

## Overview

A monochrome-plus-orange title world where lines grow into a rigorous one-point set before collapsing into overhead pattern.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Black opening from which hairline geometry slides and extrudes
- Central vanishing point with repeated people, desks, windows, or lamps
- Four or five discrete neutral fills rather than gradients
- Orange-red reserved for one lead and one narrative prop
- Fast axial push followed by a true overhead transformation

## Style Prompt

Begin in complete black. Send a few fine white lines into frame and extend them toward a centered vanishing point until they become a strict perspective grid. Over two or three seconds, fill the wire structure with four or five discrete neutral tones from near-black to warm off-white, then switch on repeated lights row by row. Give every completed form a very fine dark keyline and keep figures as readable two-tone shapes. Populate depth with a repeated motif meaningful to the subject—desks and workers, columns, street lamps, windows, shelves, or doors—shrinking precisely toward center. Reserve orange-red for a single lead plus one connected prop; all illumination remains white. Add only fine grain and a soft vignette. Hold the resolved environment long enough to read, accelerate straight toward the lead, hard-cut to a geometrically correct overhead view, and let the same grid become an abstract pattern without losing its objects.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

sketchy construction lines, gradients, leaking accent color, tilted perspective, empty wireframes, and unfilled dark outlines.

# Particle Fire Halo

## Overview

A pre-dawn 3D tableau where a physically evolving particle event becomes a huge backlight, illuminates the entire ground plane, and throws shadows toward camera.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- White flash, mottled rolling dome, ring formation, and outward dust wall
- Hundreds of thousands of textured orange-gold particles
- Fire halo occupying roughly two fifths behind the lead
- Warm ground and cloud illumination against deep pre-dawn blue
- Centered low wide view with a slow advance

## Style Prompt

Build a nighttime or pre-dawn 3D scene around one massive particle event appropriate to the subject—fireball, flare, eruption, industrial burst, wildfire front, or analogous release. Evolve it in clear stages: a half-second white flash, a rolling mottled dome within the next second, curl into a toroidal ring, then an outward-moving dust wall. Position it directly behind the centered lead as a halo occupying roughly two fifths of the image. Use hundreds of thousands of particles and turbulent density so the fire reads as textured orange-gold volume around a white-gold core; confine black smoke to thin internal veins and the upper edge rather than wrapping the fire. Let this source illuminate the whole environment with warm cream on ground and cloud undersides, strong hot rim on the lead, half-strength warm face light, long shadows toward camera, and drifting sparks. Keep the remaining world in deep pre-dawn blue and dark earth. Overexpose only the core; add fine large-format grain, warm halation, vignette, and slight gate instability. Advance slowly from a low wide lens.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

smooth CG spheres, smoke-dominated fire, an unlit foreground, tiny explosions, orange everywhere, and rapid cutting.

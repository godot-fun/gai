# Customer-Funnel Journey

## Overview

Customer cohorts move through acquisition, activation, retention, and return paths with losses and delays kept visible.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: awareness, conversion, retention, churn, and lifecycle optimization

## Signature Features

- Track persistent cohorts rather than replacing one stage total with the next.
- Encode conversion, delay, churn, re-entry, and channel source as distinct mechanisms.
- Pair the familiar funnel silhouette with a lifecycle path so post-purchase behavior is not hidden.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Customer-Funnel Journey** style. Introduce dated and sourced customer cohorts, move them through clearly defined stages, and retain the dropped, delayed, churned, and returning groups. Combine a disciplined funnel for initial conversion with a looping lifecycle for retention and advocacy. Label denominators, time windows, and stage definitions whenever rates appear.

## Avoid

- Avoid anonymous dots that vanish without accounting or rates calculated from changing denominators.
- Do not imply that every customer follows one linear path or that acquisition completes the journey.

# Perlin Noise Topography

## Overview

A continuous noise field rises into ridges, valleys, and weather-like terrain without hard random discontinuities.

## Metadata

- Collection: Generative Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: landscape formation, uncertainty fields, flow, exploration, and environmental change

## Signature Features

- Smooth correlated values drive contour spacing, elevation, color, or displacement as one coherent terrain.
- Reveal contours from low to high values, then let the field drift slowly enough to track landform evolution.
- The underlying rule remains visually legible so the effect communicates more than surface decoration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Perlin Noise Topography** style. A continuous noise field rises into ridges, valleys, and weather-like terrain without hard random discontinuities. Use composition, timing, color, and camera behavior to make the governing mechanism understandable before adding secondary detail.

## Avoid

- white-noise speckle, violent terrain boiling, decorative maps with fake coordinates, or unreadable contour density.
- Do not reproduce historical sample copy or hard-coded layouts from source material.
- Do not sacrifice readability for procedural complexity.

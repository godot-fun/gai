# Sound-Led Silent Short

## Overview

A dialogue-free miniature story gives picture and sound equal responsibility for meaning.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML animation, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: openings, endings, transitions, and visual storytelling

## Signature Features

- No explanatory dialogue.
- Actions remain readable in silhouette.
- Sound reveals off-screen space and consequence.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Sound-Led Silent Short** style. Build setup, action, turn, and resolution through visible cause and carefully placed sound; remove any shot whose meaning depends on narration.

## Avoid

- Avoid explanatory dialogue, sound with no visible or off-screen cause, actions unreadable in silhouette, or a resolution dependent on captions.

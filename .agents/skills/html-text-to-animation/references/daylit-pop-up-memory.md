# Daylit Pop-Up Memory

## Overview

A physically photographed pop-up book whose printed watercolor-and-pencil paper layers open into successive scenes on a walnut desk.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A physically photographed pop-up book whose printed watercolor-and-pencil paper layers open into successive scenes on a walnut desk.

## Signature Features

- Flat die-cut cards supported by visible parallel folds
- White paper core and grey board edge at every cut
- Printed watercolor and colored-pencil hatch on card faces
- Bright upper-left window light with soft interlayer shadows
- One continuous dive, pullback, page turn, and second dive

## Style Prompt

Stage a real open pop-up book on a walnut desk in a softly glowing room. Build every figure, building, tree, vehicle, cloud, and landscape as a flat die-cut card attached by a plausible parallel fold to pages opened at ninety degrees. Expose white paper core and grey board along cut edges so no element reads as a solid model. Print card faces with transparent watercolor and colored-pencil hatch that retains paper texture. Keep sky blue, warm sand, green, and the surrounding room in bright daylight from an upper-left window, even when the printed scene depicts night or sunset; only the ink changes time. Cast soft physical shadows between layers and use real optical depth of field focused on the protagonist's card. Start from a high three-quarter view of the spread, dive into one printed scene, withdraw until the whole book is visible, turn a page as the only edit, and advance into the next spread in the same continuous move.

## Avoid

thick sculpted assets, dark moody room lighting, cartoon faces, invisible folds, impossible layer intersections, and digital cuts that ignore the page turn.

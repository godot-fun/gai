# Urban Morphology Growth

## Overview

A purpose-built visual system in which streets, blocks, parcels, and transit accrete through planning eras.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartography and Geographic Storytelling
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: streets, blocks, parcels, and transit accrete through planning eras

## Signature Features

- Make the process whereby streets, blocks, parcels, and transit accrete through planning eras the dominant visual mechanism.
- Establish the specific causes, constraints, and scale governing **Urban Morphology Growth** before revealing their consequences.
- Let labels, texture, and camera movement support the urban morphology growth mechanism instead of competing with it.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Urban Morphology Growth** style. Build the composition around a clear demonstration of how streets, blocks, parcels, and transit accrete through planning eras. Preserve the governing spatial, temporal, material, or rhythmic relationship; add atmosphere and detail only after that relationship reads clearly.

## Avoid

- Do not replace **Urban Morphology Growth** with generic particles, glowing lines, a dashboard, or a slideshow.
- Reject motion that makes **Urban Morphology Growth** physically or conceptually inconsistent, even when the surface effect looks attractive.

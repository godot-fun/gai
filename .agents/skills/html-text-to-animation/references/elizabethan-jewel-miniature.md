# Elizabethan Jewel Miniature

## Overview

An opaque watercolor portrait set inside a gold-edged oval jewel on rust damask, surrounded by white briar roses and a Latin-like inscription.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation

## Signature Features

- Warm parchment, ultramarine, shell gold, crimson, lead white, and verdigris
- Fine shadow hatch and lead-white edge points
- Gold-edged oval with constant ultramarine ground
- Dense white-flowered briar around the sitter
- Small gold capital inscription and restrained living motion

## Style Prompt

Paint with opaque watercolor on warm parchment in the manner of a jewel-like court miniature. Use red-brown contours, extremely fine parallel hatch in shadow, and tiny lead-white points along lit edges. Restrict the palette to deep ultramarine, shell gold, crimson, lead white, verdigris, and warm flesh. Treat gold as fine metallic particles with a few polished highlights, never a flat yellow fill. Place the subject inside a gold-rimmed oval pendant resting on rust-red damask; keep the oval ground ultramarine even when it contains landscape, representing sky and terrain as flat color bands. Fill the space behind and around the sitter with a dense briar bearing small white flowers. Run a short ring of gold Roman capitals around the upper edge. For wider scenes, leave the pendant but preserve the same miniature technique. Move with a slow push and slight downward tilt; animate only flowers, cloth, breath, eye line, or one hand gesture.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

airbrushed gradients, thick oil strokes, broad modern shadows, empty blue backgrounds, flat digital gold, and full-body theatrical animation.

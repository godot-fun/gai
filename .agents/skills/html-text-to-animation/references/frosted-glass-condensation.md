# Frosted-Glass Condensation

## Overview

Mist and droplets gather on translucent glass, then trails clear temporary windows into the scene beyond. Choose this when condensation accumulates on cold translucent glass and touch or rivulets expose the scene behind it.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: privacy, longing, weather, and gradual revelation

## Signature Features

- Place the viewer close to cold translucent glass; build fog from microdroplets, larger beads, gravity trails, and a soft warm scene beyond.
- Let condensation accumulate from edges, merge into descending rivulets, and clear temporary paths through touch; hold a fixed intimate camera with shallow depth.
- Use understated serif or humanist sans text on the far side, becoming readable only through cleared regions.

## Avoid

- Choose **Rain on a Window** when falling exterior rain and intimate nighttime observation are primary.
- Avoid rain overlay on top of text, uniformly blurred glass, droplets moving upward, instant wipe transitions, or a fully sharp background.

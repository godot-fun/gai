# Soft Studio CGI

## Overview

A tactile product-ident world of candy materials, clone arrays, shallow focus, softbox lighting, and physically satisfying impacts.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Graphics Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Soft rubber, glass, metal, cloth, or translucent materials
- Pastel product palette under broad studio lights
- Clone arrays animated as waves or delayed fields
- Soft-body compression, collision, and long-tail settling
- Cinematic twenty-four-frame motion with shallow depth of field

## Style Prompt

Center the film on one product, symbol, or material behavior and build a clean studio world around it. Use physically coherent materials—subsurface-scattering rubber, frosted or clear glass, brushed metal, woven cloth, foam, or fluid—each with distinct roughness and response. Light with large soft sources and restrained rim separation, preserve contact shadows and ambient occlusion, and use shallow focus deliberately rather than blurring everything. Arrange clones into grids, spirals, rings, or stacks and drive them with phase-delayed waves. Let objects drop, squeeze, rebound, slide, drape, or collide with believable mass, completing most travel early and settling slowly. Keep candy color controlled and leave enough negative space for the hero to read. Use motion blur only on genuine speed and match impact deformation to sound or rhythm.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

default glossy primitives, weightless physics, overexposed HDR reflections, identical clone timing, excessive camera orbit, depth of field hiding weak composition, and materials that all behave like plastic.

# Faded Family Album

## Overview

A top-down memory sequence of worn instant photos and booth strips that open into softly moving, color-restored moments.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Cream album pages, white photo borders, captions, and plastic-film reflections
- Lifted cyan-grey blacks, creamy highlights, soft focus, and reduced saturation
- Tracking blur for movement and shallow focus for posed stillness
- Push through one photograph into a living scene
- Color briefly restores before the image returns to the page

## Style Prompt

Begin directly above a cream album page filled with believable instant prints, small lab photographs, or photo-booth strips. Preserve white borders, minor handling wear, and concise ballpoint captions in the user's language. Light the page softly and evenly, allowing a faint reflection from a protective plastic sheet but no dramatic beams. Grade each photograph as a faded 1970s or 1980s color print: black lifts toward cyan-grey, highlights become creamy and slightly overexposed, sharpness softens, grain stays fine, and saturation falls to roughly three fifths of normal. Keep people photographically proportioned with gentle modeling and modest face scale. In active snapshots, track the subject so it remains readable while the background smears horizontally; in posed frames, use shallow focus and slight optical softness. Push for several seconds toward one selected print until its border disappears, let the frozen instant breathe into motion as color partially returns, then withdraw and allow the aging to reappear.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

cartoon anatomy, pristine contemporary color, spotlight effects, hyper-sharp faces, saturated paper photographs, and arbitrary movement in every image.

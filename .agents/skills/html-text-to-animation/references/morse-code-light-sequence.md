# Morse-Code Light Sequence

## Overview

Short and long pulses encode real characters with disciplined intra-letter and word spacing.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: signals, secrecy, communication, and rhythmic reveals
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Encode the exact supplied message into dots, dashes, intra-character gaps, character gaps, and word gaps with one disciplined signal source.
- Transmit at a consistent tempo, show decoding only after a phrase, and use darkness as timed information rather than empty filler.
- For **Morse-Code Light Sequence**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Morse-Code Light Sequence** style. Encode the exact supplied message into dots, dashes, intra-character gaps, character gaps, and word gaps with one disciplined signal source. Transmit at a consistent tempo, show decoding only after a phrase, and use darkness as timed information rather than empty filler. Build hierarchy from glyph anatomy, spacing, reading direction, linguistic timing, and the specific encoding or word structure being demonstrated. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.
- Change typographic scale, neutral surface, accent color, and motion amplitude while preserving valid glyphs, reading order, timing, and decodability.

## Avoid

- Avoid random blinking, equal dot and dash duration, no word spacing, multiple unsynchronized lights, or adding unrequested secret text.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

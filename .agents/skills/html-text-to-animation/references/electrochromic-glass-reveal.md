# Electrochromic Glass Reveal

## Overview

A pane changes gradually from transparent to tinted as an electrical state travels across its surface.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: privacy, smart materials, before-and-after comparison, and controlled disclosure

## Signature Features

- Frame a clean architectural pane with visible electrodes; move between clear, smoke-gray, and deep blue tint while keeping the scene beyond spatially consistent.
- Propagate the tint as a measured electrical front or uniform ramp, then hold both states for comparison; use a fixed interior camera with subtle exposure compensation.
- Use quiet architectural sans serif text positioned on the frame or adjacent wall, not floating in the pane.

## Avoid

- Avoid simple opacity fades, magical dissolves, unchanged exposure, decorative gradients, or tint that behaves like liquid.

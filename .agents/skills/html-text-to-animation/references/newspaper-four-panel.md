# Newspaper Four-Panel Comic

## Overview

Black-and-white line art delivers a compact joke or observation across a few disciplined panels. Choose this for a disciplined black-and-white setup, development, turn, and punchline across a few equal panels.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: light satire, short anecdotes, and everyday humor
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Three or four fixed panels.
- Economical black line work.
- Minimal grey wash.
- Setup-to-punchline pacing.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Newspaper Four-Panel Comic** style. Give each panel one action, preserve character continuity, and hold the final panel long enough for the turn to land.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Choose **Clear-Line Comic** for longer visual adventure, or **Pop Art Comic** for a single loud statement.
- Long exposition, cinematic rendering, many camera angles, colorful backgrounds, or punchlines revealed too early.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

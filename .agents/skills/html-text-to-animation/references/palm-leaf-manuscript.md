# Palm-Leaf Manuscript

## Overview

Incised script darkens with pigment across narrow leaves bound by cord and turned in sequence. Choose this for narrow incised leaves, soot-darkened grooves, cord binding, and horizontal handling.

## Metadata

- Collection: Print, Textile and Physical Craft
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: South and Southeast Asian history, wisdom, and material writing
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Use narrow dried leaves, incised characters, soot-darkened grooves, cord holes, boards, and edge numbering.
- Scratch one line at a time, rub pigment into cuts, wipe the surface, then rotate leaves around their binding cord.

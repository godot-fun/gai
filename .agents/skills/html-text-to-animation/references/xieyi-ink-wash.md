# Xieyi Ink Wash

## Overview

Expressive ink painting uses blank rice paper and a few decisive strokes to suggest far more than it describes. Choose this for atmospheric ink-wash space, wet–dry tonal range, landscape rhythm, and slow poetic transition.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: poetry, landscape, gesture, cultural reflection, and quiet dramatic transitions
- Complexity: Advanced

## Signature Features

- Blank paper functions as space.
- One stroke may imply mountain, wave, figure, or cut.
- Ink ranges from dry black to pale wash.
- Composition follows breath and balance rather than full coverage.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Xieyi Ink Wash** style. Build the image through a small number of varied brush events: dry drag, loaded stroke, diluted bloom, and controlled bleed. Let the strongest mark carry the subject and keep seals or accent color minimal.

Translate the user's material into this grammar rather than copying the source examples. Preserve supplied wording and factual attribution, and choose an HTML-native or composited implementation appropriate to the evidence and visual material.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, evidence, copy, duration, aspect ratio, pacing, and implementation method.
- Adapt palette details only when the defining visual hierarchy remains intact.

## Avoid

- Choose **Xieyi Expressive Gesture** when one abbreviated brush action should carry the entire expression.
- Decorative smoke, full-frame grey wash, generic East-Asian motifs, many red accents, or strokes with no compositional weight.
- Never invent documents, measurements, quotations, sources, or factual evidence.
- Do not use surface effects as a substitute for the style's narrative structure.

# Industrial Broadside

## Overview

A newspaper-like industrial poster using ink black, aged cream, and a controlled fire-orange accent. Choose this for dense public-information hierarchy, aged cream stock, black ink, rules, and one fire-orange industrial accent.

## Metadata

- Collection: Ready-Made Layouts
- Medium: HTML, CSS, SVG, or Canvas
- Best for: historical arguments, labor themes, urgent notices, and documentary chapters

## Signature Features

- Dense but ordered broadsheet grid.
- Bold slab or condensed headline type.
- Rules, stamps, and registration-like marks.
- Fire orange used sparingly for urgency.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Industrial Broadside** style. Compose the page as a printed broadside with a dominant headline, narrow columns, and mechanical rules. Animate with press-like vertical reveals, registration shifts, and decisive hard cuts while maintaining reading order.

## Avoid

- Choose **Bold Editorial Poster** for fashion-led display type, or **Woodcut Letterpress Rally** for explicitly carved activist energy.
- Modern app UI, glossy photography treatment, playful candy colors, or random distressed texture.

# Imperial Gongbi Scroll

## Overview

A symmetrical court-scroll system of mineral color, controlled tonal washes, hairline contour, and measured architectural depth.

## Metadata

## Signature Features

- Flat local color followed by edge-darkening wash, iron-wire outline, gold, and white detail
- Cinnabar, mineral blue, mineral green, gamboge, and shell gold under warm ochre glaze
- Warm silk sky and ground with no cast shadows or lens effects
- Rule-drawn architecture and individually rendered tiles and people
- Centered one-point depth with a slow ceremonial push

## Style Prompt

Paint from distance to foreground on warm silk. For every form, follow the same disciplined sequence: establish an even local mineral color, deepen its lower edge and occluded side through controlled graduated wash, bind it with a uniform hair-fine dark-brown iron-wire contour, then add selective shell gold and opaque white detail. Use cinnabar, mineral blue, mineral green, gamboge, and muted gold, finally unifying the image with a thin warm-ochre glaze that leaves all contours clear. Keep sky and ground in a medium warm silk value rather than near-white or saturated blue; express time of day through subtle global temperature shifts, never orange sunsets. Do not cast Western-style shadows or add glare. Draw water as repeated fine current lines. Construct buildings with ruled architectural drawing and render roof tiles, brackets, paving, rails, and screens individually. When crowds appear, vary every face, garment, and gesture instead of cloning; a solitary subject should remain equally finished. Organize the scene symmetrically in one-point depth with larger figures forward and orderly recession behind, then move in with one slow ceremonial push.

## Avoid

flat unmodulated fills, freehand ink-wash looseness, poster saturation, copied crowds, bloom, and perspective borrowed from a wide-angle camera.

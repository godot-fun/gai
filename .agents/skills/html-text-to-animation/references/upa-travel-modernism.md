# UPA Travel Modernism

## Overview

A bright mid-century travel cartoon assembled from offset planes, angular figures, and poster-scale landmarks.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A bright mid-century travel cartoon assembled from offset planes, angular figures, and poster-scale landmarks.

## Signature Features

- Variable broken ink with consistently displaced color
- Overlapping flat cut-paper planes with one hard shadow tone
- Cream and pale aqua dominant over mustard, teal, and orange-red accents
- Crayon grain and occasional paper-white pinholes
- Large landmark shape paired with angular outlined figures

## Style Prompt

Build the world from overlapping flat planes as if cut from colored paper. Give each object a lit plane and one same-hue shadow plane about fifteen percent darker. Draw with a thin, lively near-black line that varies, breaks occasionally, and sits at a consistent small offset from the color shapes. Let cream paper and pale aqua occupy most of the image; use mustard, teal, and orange-red sparingly, mixing vegetation, sky shifts, and grey-blue from those pigments plus paper color. Cover all shapes with subtle crayon grain and leave a few tiny paper-white skips. Organize each scene around one oversized graphic landmark—a tower, sun disk, bridge, mountain, wheel, or ship—beside angular people with readable contour and gesture. Represent light rays as pale wedges rather than glow. Hard-cut between compositions every beat or two, but keep movement within each shot to slow layered parallax.

## Avoid

gradients, exact registration, fully saturated backgrounds, realistic volume, and ornamental linework unrelated to form.

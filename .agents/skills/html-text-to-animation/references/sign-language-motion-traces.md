# Sign-Language Motion Traces

## Overview

Hands, orientation, location, facial expression, and movement paths remain integrated rather than reduced to captions.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: signed communication, accessibility, gesture, and linguistic motion
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Keep hands, facial grammar, torso orientation, signing space, and nonmanual markers visible as an integrated linguistic performance.
- Use traces only to clarify path, repetition, or location after the signer establishes the form; keep a stable medium shot.
- For **Sign-Language Motion Traces**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Sign-Language Motion Traces** style. Keep hands, facial grammar, torso orientation, signing space, and nonmanual markers visible as an integrated linguistic performance. Use traces only to clarify path, repetition, or location after the signer establishes the form; keep a stable medium shot. Build hierarchy from glyph anatomy, spacing, reading direction, linguistic timing, and the specific encoding or word structure being demonstrated. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.
- Change typographic scale, neutral surface, accent color, and motion amplitude while preserving valid glyphs, reading order, timing, and decodability.

## Avoid

- Avoid hand-only icons, substituting spoken-word order, obscuring the face, decorative trails, or claiming linguistic accuracy without verified signing.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

# Eastern Cyber-Ink Fantasy

## Overview

A parchment-sky future landscape where red sun, ink mountains, neon pagoda, lantern cables, cranes, boat, and dissolving reflections coexist without losing quietness.

- Collection: Reference-Derived Styles

## Signature Features

- Warm paper sky and oversized red sun
- Five-story waterside pavilion with traditional eaves and restrained neon
- Pale ink mountains mixed with distant future towers
- Cables bearing small red lantern strings and a cyan holographic ring
- Boat and conical-hat figure reflected as bleeding ink

## Style Prompt

Compose the scene like a horizontal ink-and-color scroll under a warm paper sky. Place a large flat red sun high enough to silhouette a line of cranes. Anchor the water with a five-story pavilion whose traditional roof brackets and upturned eaves remain structurally convincing, then add a few justified vertical neon signs and one screen rather than covering it in technology. Wrap a thin cyan holographic ring around the upper tower and let distant future structures fade into pale ink mountains. Stretch cables through the middle distance and hang irregular strings of small red lanterns from them. In the foreground, place a narrow boat and a single conical-hat figure, both quiet and small. Let water reflections dissolve into wet ink blooms, dry-brush ripples, and broken pigment. Animate lantern sway, cranes, slow water, sign breathing, and the boat's passage.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

generic cyberpunk saturation, fake calligraphy, excessive UI, Western cathedral silhouettes, hard digital reflections, crowded skies, and technology that erases the scroll's negative space.

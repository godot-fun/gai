# Eye-Ring Multiverse

## Overview

A rapid sequence of genuinely different visual worlds connected by a recurring movable eye and an expanding tactile rainbow portal.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Three or four universes with distinct medium, lighting, and spatial logic
- Recurring movable eye attached to the same subject
- Half-second push with smear and RGB separation
- Rainbow ring packed with eyes and bead-like detail
- White flash only after the ring expands around the subject

## Style Prompt

Design three or four universes that differ fundamentally rather than merely by color: for example, a crowded realistic workplace under green fluorescent light, a surreal lensing void, a quiet distant landscape with restrained subtitles, and a glossy candy-colored product world with cyan-pink rim light. Derive each from an object, concern, or alternative in the user's source and hold each for two to four seconds under its own rendering rules. Attach one movable craft eye to the lead and keep it visible at roughly one tenth of frame height across every world. For each jump, push into that eye for half to three quarters of a second, add directional smear and controlled RGB separation, then burst outward into a thick rainbow ring of red, yellow, teal, blue, and pink blocks embedded with many eyes and small pink beads. Expand the ring until it surrounds most of the frame and the subject, then flash white into the next medium. Introduce the first jump early and use the full ring as the signature composition.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

universes sharing one visual grammar, tiny empty portals, generic random worlds, constant glitch noise, transition colors unrelated to the ring, and an eye that changes position or scale without continuity.

# Visual Investigation

## Overview

Footage, documents, maps, audio, and timelines from one real event are synchronized so each piece of evidence can verify or challenge the others.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: forensic explainers, accountability reporting, and evidence reconstruction

## Signature Features

- One shared clock across all evidence.
- Known facts separated from uncertainty.
- Frozen frames circled and magnified.
- Camera positions resolved on a final map.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Visual Investigation** style. Place timestamps inside the evidence, align media to a common playhead, highlight quotes on their source pages, and show field of view geometrically. End by pulling all verified elements into one spatial model.

## Avoid

- Conspiracy-board decoration, invented certainty, dramatic red strings, evidence without provenance, or a conclusion stronger than the material supports.

# Wartime Stone Lithograph

## Overview

A three-plate civic image with strong simplified figures, crayon shadows, and angled bands of hope-like light.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A three-plate civic image with strong simplified figures, crayon shadows, and angled bands of hope-like light.

## Signature Features

- Color, grey-blue shadow, and green-black key plates
- One-to-three-pixel registration drift
- Short slanted lithographic crayon grain instead of halftones
- Cream-dominant palette with restrained brick red and deep green
- Frontal poster figures under diagonal light bands

## Style Prompt

Reduce the scene to forceful, economical silhouettes suitable for a 1940s public-information poster. Print it from three visibly separate plates: a flat-color plate, a cool grey-blue shadow plate textured by short diagonal lithographic crayon marks, and a dark green-black key plate outlining every figure, cloud, and object. Offset the plates slightly and retain wandering edges, stone grain, and imperfect pressure. Use warm cream as paper and light, grey-blue for sky and shadow, and restrict brick red plus deep green to less than a quarter of the image. Build browns and skin from those same inks rather than introducing new hues. Airbrush only broad sky transitions. Send pale diagonal bands from the implied light source; wherever a band lands, remove the shadow plate rather than painting a glow on top. Arrange people frontally with clear hands, faces, and purposeful stance, and allow the environment to fill the frame. Drift the camera for shallow parallax or tilt upward through the composition.

## Avoid

dot screens, exact digital registration, polished vector boundaries, and giant saturated fields.

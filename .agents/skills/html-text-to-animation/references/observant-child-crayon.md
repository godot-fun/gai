# Observant Child Crayon

## Overview

A sincere school-age drawing with waxy pressure, uneven directional filling, invented labels, and richly observed narrative detail.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Black or brown trembling wax contours
- Paper tooth visible under light pressure and filled under heavy pressure
- Back-and-forth strokes escaping boundaries
- Ground line, flattened perspective, arrows, labels, patterns, and one reversed element
- Unsteady close inspection followed by a calm full-page scan

## Style Prompt

Draw as an attentive six-to-eight-year-old who cares about the event but has not learned academic perspective. Use waxy black or dark-brown contours that tremble, change pressure, and remain thick enough to expose paper tooth. Fill each region with repeated back-and-forth crayon marks in its own direction; vary gap, angle, and length, let strokes run beyond boundaries, and allow lower colors to show through overlaps. Use a direct box of red, orange, yellow, green, and blue, pressing lightly where much white paper should remain and heavily at emotional focal points. Include child-logic conventions when relevant: a ground line, flattened rooms or roads, an arrowed label, a smiling sun, individually colored pattern cells, and one reversed letter or component. Hand-draw all words in the user's language with shaky wax strokes; never substitute a font. Reward close viewing with many concrete objects and relationships rather than random scribble. Start with a slightly wavering close detail, then stabilize and scan the full sheet.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

adult-perfect geometry, empty symbolic doodles, regular dot or grid texture, smooth gradients, and fake childishness made only from messy lines.

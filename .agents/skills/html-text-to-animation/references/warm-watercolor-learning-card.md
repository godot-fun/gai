## Style Prompt

Design each frame as one self-contained learning card on warm cold-press paper. Use soft transparent watercolor in a small friendly palette, preserving paper white, pooled edges, mild granulation, and occasional blooms only in broad wet areas. Simplify people, animals, plants, machines, or scientific objects into approachable shapes with clear expressions and accurate key features. Give each card one teaching goal: name parts, compare two states, show a sequence, trace a cycle, or explain cause and effect. Set a short hand-lettered title and only the labels necessary, connected by clean lines or arrows that remain visually distinct from painted contours. Reveal the illustration from light wash to darker detail, then introduce labels in reading order. Keep background decoration sparse and relevant.

# 1920s Newsreel

## Overview

A brisk late-1920s sound newsreel assembled from archive material, newspaper devices, maps, title cards, and theatrical transitions.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: historical summaries, archival satire, and period documentary chapters
- Complexity: Advanced

## Signature Features

- Searchlight-and-globe opening grammar.
- Newspaper, map, and document inserts.
- Fast formal announcer cadence.
- Irises, wipes, and optical-film wear.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **1920s Newsreel** style. Cut real archive material with cheerful urgency, use place-and-date cards, and let a hot-band rhythm drive edits before stopping sharply for the punchline. Keep period wear light enough to preserve evidence.

Translate the user's material into this grammar rather than copying the source examples. Preserve supplied wording and factual attribution, and choose an HTML-native or composited implementation appropriate to the evidence and visual material.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, evidence, copy, duration, aspect ratio, pacing, and implementation method.
- Adapt palette details only when the defining visual hierarchy remains intact.

## Avoid

- Modern glitch, fake archival claims, constant sepia, illegible damage, or contemporary broadcast graphics.
- Never invent documents, measurements, quotations, sources, or factual evidence.
- Do not use surface effects as a substitute for the style's narrative structure.

# 1930s Rubber-Hose Cartoon

## Overview

Elastic limbs, pie-cut eyes, and rhythmic monochrome movement evoke early theatrical animation. Choose this as the medium-neutral foundation for elastic 1930s character acting without a required music or road-story premise.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: retro humor, musical motion, and playful surrealism

## Signature Features

- Rubber-hose arms and legs.
- Pie-cut eyes and white gloves.
- Bouncy looped rhythm.
- Black-and-white or faded two-tone finish.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **1930s Rubber-Hose Cartoon** style. Animate on a musical pulse with arcing limbs, clear anticipations, and mischievous transformations. Add restrained film wear without hiding the drawing.

## Avoid

- Choose **Jazz Rubber-Hose Cartoon** for music-led object performance, or **Rubber-Hose Road Comedy** for a chase narrative.
- Modern anime anatomy, smooth 3D motion, detailed gradients, horror distortion, or heavy fake damage.

# Technicolor Impasto Epic

## Overview

A blazing poster-scale oil painting shaped by palette-knife skies, monumental silhouettes, and a readable warm hero. Choose this when saturated large-format cinema, heroic scale, and painted texture must operate together.

## Signature Features

- Long horizontal impasto bands in the sky
- Shorter directional strokes on land, architecture, and figures
- Wine, scarlet, orange, gold, red earth, and warm near-black
- Strong backlight with gold rims and selective warm face light
- Low horizons, slow pushes, and crane reveals

## Style Prompt

Paint the sky in long palette-knife bands moving from wine through scarlet and orange to molten gold. Use shorter strokes that follow form on ground, buildings, trees, and people, leaving calmer thin-painted zones between clusters so the texture can breathe. Let raised ridges catch an upper light and expose canvas weave wherever pigment thins. Anchor the frame with large warm near-black silhouettes along a low horizon—trees, roofs, ruins, crowds, or rock masses—while keeping the narrative lead in a readable warm middle value instead of losing it to silhouette. Backlight the scene fiercely with gold edge light; in closer views, add a narrow warm side light across face and hands. Treat practical flames as distinct painted shapes, not glow filters. Begin with a slow push across the wide tableau, approach the lead from a low angle, then crane upward to expose the full scale.

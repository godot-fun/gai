# Magnetic Particle Field

## Overview

Iron-like particles align into curved field lines around moving poles, revealing attraction and repulsion. Choose this when particle orientation and density must reveal attraction, repulsion, and pole geometry.

## Metadata

- Collection: Generative Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: polarity, influence, invisible forces, and competing centers

## Signature Features

- Particle orientation follows a coherent vector field with dense arcs near poles and neutral zones between forces.
- Introduce poles one at a time, let particles settle, then move or reverse a pole to demonstrate field response.
- The underlying rule remains visually legible so the effect communicates more than surface decoration.

- Choose **Flow-Field Particle Formation** for nonmagnetic directional flow, or **Breathing Particle Sphere** for meditative motion without forces.
- generic swarms, sparks, uncontrolled turbulence, or field lines that ignore pole position.

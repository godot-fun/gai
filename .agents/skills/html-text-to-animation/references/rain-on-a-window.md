# Rain on a Window

## Overview

Sharp droplets on glass frame a soft night beyond, creating a private space for longing. Choose this when sharp moving raindrops on clear glass frame an emotionally distant night beyond.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: waiting, absence, nighttime reflection, and intimate memory

## Signature Features

- Foreground droplets and trails remain sharper than the background.
- Distant lights dissolve into soft bokeh and reflection.
- Text occupies a calmer patch of glass and feels part of the scene.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Rain on a Window** style. Layer slow and fast droplets over a cool defocused night, with one restrained warm light in the distance. Keep the camera nearly still and use subtle parallax only to preserve depth.

## Avoid

- Choose **Frosted-Glass Condensation** when mist, breath, touch, and temporary cleared paths—not rainfall—drive the reveal.
- Storm spectacle, sharp detailed backgrounds, many neon colors, fast camera moves, or text floating independently of the glass.

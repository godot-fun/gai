# Typographic Rain

## Overview

Falling language becomes a physical burden as words accumulate across the frame.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: repetition, criticism, pressure, and information overload

## Signature Features

- Words or short phrases fall with varied weight and speed.
- Fallen type accumulates into an increasingly oppressive mass.
- Shrinking negative space is the main measure of escalation.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Typographic Rain** style. Begin sparsely, let each phrase remain readable in motion, then increase density until the remaining open space feels compressed. Use a dark restrained field, pale type, and at most one warm accent.

## Avoid

- Decorative weather, rainbow type, uniform speed, or particles unrelated to language.

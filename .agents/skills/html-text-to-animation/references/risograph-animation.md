# Risograph Animation

## Overview

Two or three translucent spot inks overprint on warm paper with halftone grain and lively registration drift. Choose this for the general two- or three-ink risograph process, visible registration drift, and lively editorial motion.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: graphic essays, music, youth culture, and tactile editorial motion

## Signature Features

- Transparent spot inks mix where they overlap.
- Halftone and ink grain remain visible.
- Registration is slightly imperfect but controlled.
- Frames feel printed rather than digitally filtered.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Risograph Animation** style. Separate artwork into a few ink plates, animate each plate with restrained independent drift, and exploit overprint colors as part of the design.

## Avoid

- Choose **Three-Ink Noir Risograph** when the palette and narrative must stay dark, suspenseful, and noir.
- Opaque digital fills, random jitter every frame, full-spectrum color, perfect registration, or generic noise overlays.

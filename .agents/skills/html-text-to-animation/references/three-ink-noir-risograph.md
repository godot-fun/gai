## Signature Features

- Orange-red field with vertical drag marks

- Fluorescent-pink rims and halos only

- Handheld light-following camera with one reverse close-up

## Style Prompt

Print on warm white stock using exactly three inks. Let orange-red dominate roughly three fifths of the frame and show vertical roller or fluid drag. Use deep blue for silhouettes, sky caps, and shadow, knocking the orange plate out beneath blue solids so they stay pure; overlap the two only at the deepest near-black accents. Restrict fluorescent pink to narrow edge light, signs, and a halo around exposed paper. Treat illumination as absence of ink: lamps, beams, and lit ground reveal paper white and receive a thin pink perimeter. Build skin from a partial orange screen plus one blue shadow step. Give each ink its own halftone angle, grain, pinholes, and small frame-to-frame registration drift. Even in cool scenes, preserve the red field and merely increase blue near the top. Follow behind a person carrying or approaching the key light with restrained handheld sway, reverse once into a close face, and finish with a slow advance.

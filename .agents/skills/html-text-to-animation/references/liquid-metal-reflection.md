# Liquid-Metal Reflection

## Overview

A dense mirrorlike fluid beads, merges, stretches, and reforms while reflecting its environment.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: futurism, transformation, product identity, and adaptive systems
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Stage a few heavy mercury-like beads on a matte graphite plane; preserve crisp environment reflections, rounded surface tension, and a silver-black value range.
- Make beads attract, neck together, overshoot, and settle with convincing mass; use a low tracking camera and let reflected typography bend across the surface.
- Use bold neutral grotesk type as environmental panels, readable directly and only distorted in reflection.

# Acoustic Sculpture Chamber

## Overview

Sound activates resonant plates, strings, tubes, and spatial wave patterns inside an architectural instrument.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: music, physics, immersion, and responsive environments

## Signature Features

- Compose resonant plates, strings, tubes, membranes, and room surfaces around a clear excitation point.
- Strike or drive one element, show coupled vibration and standing-wave zones, then let energy decay naturally through the chamber.
- For **Acoustic Sculpture Chamber**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Acoustic Sculpture Chamber** style. Compose resonant plates, strings, tubes, membranes, and room surfaces around a clear excitation point. Strike or drive one element, show coupled vibration and standing-wave zones, then let energy decay naturally through the chamber. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid generic music visualizers, sound moving as glowing balls, perpetual vibration, random equalizer bars, or camera shake as acoustics.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

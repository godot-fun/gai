# Ink-Paper Pixel Mascot

## Overview

A hybrid story world where warm paper and spare hand drawing are punctuated by a small 8-bit actor, dark title cards, and brief arcade states.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Video Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A hybrid story world where warm paper and spare hand drawing are punctuated by a small 8-bit actor, dark title cards, and brief arcade states.

## Signature Features

- Warm paper with a thin ink horizon
- Minimal drawn scenery and generous empty space
- Small pixel mascot performing the narrative
- Dark typographic cards and occasional arcade screen
- Clear switches between analog drawing and pixel logic

## Style Prompt

Establish a warm lightly textured paper field with one thin hand-drawn horizon and only the essential ink objects needed to orient the story. Introduce a small original 8-bit mascot whose limited sprite cycles physically act out each idea—walking, lifting, pointing, failing, retrying, and celebrating—while the surrounding world stays loose and hand drawn. Use dark full-frame title cards for chapter turns and brief arcade screens for goals, counters, warnings, or results, but never let interface dominate the paper world. Match pixel scale consistently, move sprites in integer steps, and allow ink marks to become platforms, obstacles, or paths. Make transitions explicit: ink folds into a dark card, a card opens as a game screen, or the mascot exits back onto paper. Keep copy short and typography purposeful.

## Avoid

mixing anti-aliased pixels with rough drawing, dense sketch backgrounds, stock game characters, decorative arcade UI, abrupt visual-language changes, and a mascot that merely stands beside narration.

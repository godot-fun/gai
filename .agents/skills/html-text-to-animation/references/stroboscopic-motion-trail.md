# Stroboscopic Motion Trail

## Overview

Repeated flashes isolate successive positions of a moving subject inside one dark exposure.

## Metadata

- Collection: Experimental Camera and Image Processes
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: movement analysis, dance, machinery, and rhythmic performance

## Signature Features

- Keep a dark environment and expose the moving subject only at evenly timed flash instants, producing separated sharp phases.
- Lock the camera, set a readable flash frequency, and allow phase spacing to reveal acceleration or periodicity.
- For **Stroboscopic Motion Trail**, use a palette, contrast curve, grain structure, and edge behavior native to the named capture or darkroom process.

## Avoid

- Avoid continuous light trails, motion blur, irregular copies, moving backgrounds, or flashes too dense to distinguish.
- Do not imitate a photographic, optical, or compression process with unrelated glitch decoration or a generic color filter.

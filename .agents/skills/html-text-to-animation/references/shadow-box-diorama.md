# Shadow-Box Diorama

## Overview

Layered cut planes recede inside a shallow box with controlled depth, lighting, and framed viewpoint.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: miniature stories, memory, nature, and handcrafted space

## Signature Features

- Layer cut foreground, middle ground, and background inside a shallow framed cavity with tactile edges and contact shadows.
- Move the camera only slightly for parallax, animate elements within their layer, and transition by replacing physical planes.
- For **Shadow-Box Diorama**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Shadow-Box Diorama** style. Layer cut foreground, middle ground, and background inside a shallow framed cavity with tactile edges and contact shadows. Move the camera only slightly for parallax, animate elements within their layer, and transition by replacing physical planes. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid flat collage with fake blur, objects crossing layers, deep cinematic fly-throughs, invisible frame, or weightless paper.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

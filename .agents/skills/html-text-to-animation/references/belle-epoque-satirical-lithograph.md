# Belle Époque Satirical Lithograph

## Overview

A lively nightlife poster with blunt brush contours, flat stones, abrasive dry brush, and observant caricature.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Loaded brush outlines with blunt starts and split dry endings
- Yellow, orange-red, olive, black, muted bottle green, and pale violet
- Sparse toothbrush speckle limited to shadow and halo regions
- Large cropped foreground observer against a social middle ground
- Flat light and flat printed shadows

## Style Prompt

Compose the frame as a Belle Époque color lithograph observed from within the crowd. Outline principal people and furnishings with a heavily loaded brush: blunt at entry, swollen through the stroke, and split into dry hairs at exit. Print broad flat stones in yellow, orange-red, olive, black, muted bottle green, and pale violet on grainy cream paper. Use grouped same-direction dry-brush bars inside large fields. Confine tiny toothbrush flecks to backlit edges, cast shadows, or smoky halos and keep them below a small fraction of the image. Exaggerate profiles with sharp social specificity—nose, chin, posture, hat, glance—while retaining real facial features. Crop one oversized spectator or performer at the foreground edge and let the middle ground carry the social action. Keep illumination even and shadows as separate flat plates. Pan slowly across layered tables, dancers, signs, or streets using shallow parallax, then settle on a middle-distance exchange.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

Art Nouveau halos, gradients, regular halftones, indiscriminate black speckle, and pretty anonymous faces.

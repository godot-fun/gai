# Bold Sticker Explainer

## Overview

A friendly educational system of thick outlined sticker characters, simplified objects, cut-paper shadows, and clear visual metaphors.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Graphics Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Chunky dark outline and white sticker border
- Simple high-contrast fills and expressive faces
- Offset paper-like shadow separating stickers from the field
- Objects and labels arranged as visual explanation
- Pop, slide, peel, stamp, and squash motion

## Style Prompt

Translate the subject into a small cast of expressive sticker people, creatures, objects, arrows, containers, and symbols. Draw every sticker with a bold dark keyline, clean flat color, and a consistent white cut border plus a short offset paper shadow. Keep anatomy simplified but hands, eyes, posture, and props specific enough to explain actions. Build each beat around one visual metaphor—stacking, sorting, filling, comparing, opening, connecting, or transforming—rather than decorating narration with unrelated icons. Introduce elements through peel-on, stamp, slide, or elastic pop; use squash on contact and small reaction poses to keep the cast alive. Keep text short, large, and integrated as labels or counters. Preserve a calm background and strong hierarchy so the explanation reads immediately.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

copying a recognizable presenter or studio mascot, tiny encyclopedic labels, generic emoji, outlines of inconsistent thickness, shadows that imply 3D lighting, frantic simultaneous motion, and sticker clutter that replaces actual reasoning.

# Gothic Stained-Glass Narrative

## Overview

A story told as luminous cut glass, with disciplined lead geometry and colored light continuing beyond the window.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Cut-glass shapes bounded by heavy black leads and fine brown grisaille
- Cobalt fields, ruby borders, gold highlights, amber skin, and deep green scenery
- Diamond quarries in otherwise empty areas
- Seed bubbles, flow lines, value variation, and bloom into lead
- Colored light pooling onto sill and floor

## Style Prompt

Design one dominant Gothic window that nearly fills the frame, allowing neighboring windows to glow at the sides instead of surrounding it with dead wall. Construct every object from plausible cut pieces bounded by black lead, with individual glass value variation, flow striations, seed bubbles, and a narrow brown edge mat. Divide sky and unoccupied ground into regular diamond quarries; use beaded ruby geometry around the perimeter. Make the main figure at least half the window height and draw its face, hands, folds, and small objects in pointed dark-brown grisaille. Assign cobalt to broad background fields, ruby to borders, gold to highlights, pale amber to skin, and deep greens to landscape. Reserve a warm orange family for the narrative focus. Let bright panes bloom slightly into the lead while their colored projection moves across stone, sill, or floor beneath. Tilt down through the iconography, pan across connected panels, and settle on the pool of light.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

arbitrary mosaic fragments, flat digital fills, illegible faces, and large black architectural voids.

# Handcrafted Paper Art

## Overview

Folded, curled, and assembled paper details emphasize the warmth of visible making. Choose this when folds, curls, scoring, assembly, and visible handwork matter more than flat silhouette layering.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: friendly science, craft, ecology, and tactile education

## Signature Features

- Paper fibers and slight irregularity.
- Hand-cut and hand-folded construction.
- Warm natural palette.
- Visible joints and layering.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Handcrafted Paper Art** style. Let components assemble by hand-like motions, use modest shadows to explain construction, and keep imperfections consistent rather than random.

## Avoid

- Choose **Layered Paper Cut** when the image depends mainly on stacked flat cut shapes.
- Perfect factory geometry, plastic sheen, excessive shadow, photoreal clutter, or invisible construction.

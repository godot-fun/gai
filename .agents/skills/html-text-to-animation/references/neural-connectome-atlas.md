# Neural Connectome Atlas

## Overview

Colored fiber bundles trace long-range pathways through a translucent brain volume.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: neuroscience, communication, complexity, and network anatomy
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Place a translucent anatomical volume around directional fiber bundles; encode major tracts by orientation rather than arbitrary rainbow color.
- Seed streamlines from selected regions, grow them along continuous paths, and isolate one network at a time before a slow anatomical orbit.
- Use small clinical labels anchored to regions, plus a restrained orientation legend.

# MRI Slice Reconstruction

## Overview

Sequential cross-sectional slices align and accumulate into a navigable three-dimensional volume.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: anatomy, layered evidence, medical education, and reconstruction

## Signature Features

- Center a neutral volumetric subject with axial, sagittal, and coronal planes; use grayscale tissue contrast plus one restrained segmentation color.
- Step through evenly spaced slices, align crosshairs, then stack selected contours into a translucent volume; keep the camera clinically stable before a slow 3D orbit.
- Use monospaced orientation markers, slice numbers, and clean sans-serif explanatory text.

## Style Prompt

## Avoid

- Avoid arbitrary glowing scans, slices with changing registration, saturated rainbow anatomy, fast fly-throughs, or medical diagnoses unsupported by data.

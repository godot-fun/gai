# Break the Frame

## Overview

A subject physically escapes the border of its poster or panel to mark a shift beyond an old assumption.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML animation, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: turning points, liberation, reframing, and category breaks
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Border is established as a real constraint.
- Subject crosses it through motivated action.
- Outside space has a distinct visual logic.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Break the Frame** style. Spend time making the frame believable, build pressure against it, break at the narrative turn, and let the new space change composition rather than merely add overflow.

Adapt the subject, copy, duration, aspect ratio, and implementation method while preserving the defining narrative and motion logic. Keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, copy, duration, aspect ratio, palette, pacing, and implementation method.

## Avoid

- Do not copy sample brands, characters, or wording from the source material.
- Do not add motion that has no explanatory, rhythmic, or emotional purpose.
- Preserve readability and one dominant idea per beat.

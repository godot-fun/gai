# Sodium-Cyan Long Exposure

## Overview

A layered night street image where warm sodium architecture, cyan signage, wet reflections, and continuous traffic trails surround one sharply held subject.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Half the frame in warm sodium light against cyan neon and cyan-black shadow
- Thin continuous white and red traffic ribbons with hot cores
- Foreground pedestrians, middle storefronts, and an upper roadway or lit route
- Snow or rain visible only inside light cones
- One continuous tilt-pan-refocus camera movement

## Style Prompt

Layer the scene spatially: people and pavement in the foreground, active storefronts and signs in the middle, and a road, bridge, rail line, or lamp-lined path crossing the upper third. Keep about half of architecture, signs, and wet ground in warm sodium orange, balance it with cyan neon, and hold blacks slightly cyan without lifting them to grey. Stretch moving vehicle lights into thin continuous ribbons along the route—warm white forward, red behind—with incandescent centers and restrained bloom. Only traffic and precipitation smear; keep the lead figure sharply readable in the center of a single lamp cone. Divide snow or rain into near, middle, and far streak lengths and reveal it only where illuminated. Finish with two-scale bloom, one subtle horizontal blue flare, edge bokeh, vignette, and dynamic fine grain. Move through the shot continuously by combining tilt, pan, and one intentional rack focus.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

uniformly bright precipitation, large empty black zones, dotted or broken trails, global motion blur, orange-only grading, and a lead dissolved by bloom.

# Electron Microscope Landscape

## Overview

Extreme magnification turns microscopic surfaces into grayscale cliffs, pores, fibers, and crystalline terrain.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: materials, scale shifts, nanotechnology, and unfamiliar worlds

## Signature Features

- Fill the frame with a grayscale micro-surface whose pores, fibers, grains, or facets are defined by directional electron-like shading and extreme depth.
- Traverse slowly as if on a precision sample stage, then change magnification in discrete calibrated steps rather than a cinematic zoom.
- Use compact monochrome instrument labels, magnification, and scale bars along the frame edge.

# Brick Stencil Narrative

## Overview

A daylight mural sequence sprayed through physically plausible multilayer stencils onto old red brick, complete with bridges, overspray, runs, paste-up, and tags.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Three to five cut stencil layers per image
- Hard apertures, lifted-edge mist, connecting bridges, speckle, and drips
- Black, grey, gold, and red over cream-painted wall patches
- Brick relief, mortar shadows, rain stains, and paint entering joints
- Layer-by-layer spray reveal followed by close lateral inspection

## Style Prompt

Use a real old red-brick wall as both surface and subject: preserve raised brick, recessed mortar, rain streaks, chips, and earlier paint. Brush irregular cream patches where the major works will sit. Build each image from three to five physically cut stencil layers with a minimum viable opening size. Include the bridges needed to hold internal islands, hard cut edges where the stencil lies flat, softer mist where it lifts, scattered spray grains, small runs, and uneven opacity. Let paint sink into mortar and break over brick texture rather than floating above it. Restrict the palette to black, middle grey, aged gold, and red. Arrange several related works along one wall, add a short hand-sprayed phrase in the user's language, older tags, and one torn halftone paste-up. Illuminate with soft angled daylight at moderate contrast and let cable shadows move gently; do not bury the wall in vignette. First spray each color layer into existence, then cut to close details and track laterally across the sequence.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

vector-clean edges, unlimited colors, pristine brick, digital decals, dark nightclub lighting, and stencil imagery that cannot physically remain connected.

# Double Screenprint Portrait

## Overview

A repeated photographic figure printed over brushed silver with unstable black dots and displaced hand-painted color.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A repeated photographic figure printed over brushed silver with unstable black dots and displaced hand-painted color.

## Signature Features

- High-contrast black photo screen with little middle tone
- Brushy taxi yellow, flesh pink, cream, and red underplates
- Softly reflective silver-painted ground
- Uneven squeegee pressure, clogged dots, dry drag, and pinholes
- Two-then-three repetitions with overlap and shifting clarity

## Style Prompt

Prepare the subject as a nearly binary photographic stencil and print it through a coarse black halftone at a fixed angle, retaining only decisive face, garment, and gesture information. Beneath it, hand-brush broad fields of yellow, flesh pink, cream, and red whose crooked edges miss the photo by a visible distance. Use the strongest color on the focal subject. Print everything over a softly reflective silver-painted canvas whose brush direction and weave remain visible. Vary squeegee pressure across the frame: let some dots clog into black islands, others starve into horizontal dry streaks, and small pinholes expose the ground. Repeat the same full figure or object twice across open silver, then three times with slight overlap, changing registration, ink load, and clarity each time. Reduce environmental context to pale silver photo remnants within those repetitions. Move with small handheld instability, push into dot and paint texture, then hold frontally as a flat graphic object.

## Avoid

clean uniform screens, precise color registration, identical copies, equal focus everywhere, and elaborate scenery outside the repeated impressions.

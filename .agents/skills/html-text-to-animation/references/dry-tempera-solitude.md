# Dry Tempera Solitude

## Overview

A matte, hairline-detailed landscape where a small off-center subject sits inside pale weather and wind-combed ground.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A matte, hairline-detailed landscape where a small off-center subject sits inside pale weather and wind-combed ground.

## Signature Features

- Dense crossed micro-strokes over a dry gesso-like surface
- Pale overcast, straw, umber, grey-green, and rust palette
- Ground built from tonal masses, dark clumps, and individual wind-aligned blades
- Tiny focal accent with no glow
- Very slow drift and quiet advance

## Style Prompt

Paint on a dry, absorbent ground with countless hair-fine short strokes layered in changing directions until color becomes matte and granular. Keep the palette desaturated: chalky overcast, straw yellow, umber, grey-green distance, and restrained rust. Mix water, stone, clothing, skin, and architecture from those families rather than adding bright local color. Build fields or other ground in three stages: broad light-and-dark masses, denser shadow clumps, then individual blades, cracks, or fibers aligned by wind and terrain. Describe rocks and buildings with small strokes that turn with each plane. Place the principal subject small and away from center inside broad lonely space; give it only one compact accent such as a hat, scarf, tool, or coat. Any lamp or sun remains a weak pale point with no digital halo. Maintain cool overcast illumination even when the implied time changes. Let the camera drift almost imperceptibly and approach without breaking the stillness; a circular wipe may mark a temporal or emotional cut.

## Avoid

wet watercolor blooms, glossy impasto, saturated sunsets, dramatic warm light, large centered heroes, and texture that ignores form.

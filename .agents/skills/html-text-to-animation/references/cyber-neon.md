# Cyber Neon

## Overview

Sparse neon light on a deep field creates a focused future-facing visual language. Choose this for a sparse, abstract neon system on darkness rather than a photographed city or physical sign.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: future technology, networks, nightlife, and high-stakes digital moments
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Near-black ground.
- Two controlled neon hues.
- Sharp luminous contours.
- Depth through haze and reflection.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Cyber Neon** style. Use glow as a hierarchy tool, not a blanket effect. Reveal structures through scanning light and keep text crisp above atmospheric depth.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Choose **Cinematic Neon Rain Alley** for cinematic streets, **Practical Neon Wall Sign** for one real fixture, or **Synthwave VHS Title** for retro graphics.
- Rainbow cyberpunk clutter, glow on every edge, unreadable type, excessive rain, or generic HUD overlays.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

# Cash-Flow River System

## Overview

Money moves through a business as conserved streams that enter, divide, pool, and leave over time.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: revenue, expenses, liquidity, runway, and the movement of money

## Signature Features

- Preserve value across labeled inflows, operating channels, reserves, financing, and outflows.
- Encode amount with stream width and timing with moving markers, using a persistent cash reservoir to reveal liquidity.
- Show delayed payments, recurring costs, and runway as temporal mechanisms rather than static totals.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Cash-Flow River System** style. Build a legible network of conserved monetary streams flowing into and out of a persistent cash reservoir. Vary channel width by amount, mark timing and delay, and let the reservoir level expose liquidity and runway. Use accounting clarity, explicit periods and units, and a calm palette with warning color reserved for genuine shortfall.

## Avoid

- Avoid implying that revenue, profit, and cash are interchangeable.
- Do not use arbitrary particles, leaking value, or stream widths that contradict the labeled amounts.

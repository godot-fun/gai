# Clear-Line Comic

## Overview

Even black contours, flat color, and carefully staged panels produce a crisp European-comic clarity. Choose this for even contours, flat restrained color, European album clarity, and spacious sequential staging.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: adventure, sequential explanation, and observational storytelling
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Uniform precise contour lines.
- Flat restrained color.
- Readable environments with selective detail.
- Strong panel-to-panel staging.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Clear-Line Comic** style. Compose each beat like a clear comic panel with unambiguous action and readable props. Animate through panel reveals, pans, and limited character poses.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Choose **Halftone American Comic** for aggressive pulp energy, **Pop Art Comic** for graphic exclamation, or **Newspaper Four-Panel Comic** for a compact gag.
- Painterly shading, scratchy lines, superhero exaggeration, speed-line overload, or muddy backgrounds.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

# Variable-Font Axis Performance

## Overview

Weight, width, slant, optical size, and custom font axes animate as semantic dimensions rather than simple scaling.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: identity, emphasis, responsive typography, and vocal nuance

## Signature Features

- Choose a genuine variable family and map weight, width, slant, optical size, or a custom axis to distinct semantic or acoustic changes.
- Interpolate axes continuously within valid ranges, hold readable extrema, and keep line breaks stable unless width change is the subject.
- For **Variable-Font Axis Performance**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Avoid

- Avoid scaling glyphs instead of changing axes, arbitrary wobble, mixing unrelated fonts, illegible extremes, or every axis moving at once.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.

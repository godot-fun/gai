# Ink Bloom on Paper

## Overview

Dark ink blooms organically through absorbent paper, turning diffusion into emotion.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: Chinese-inspired atmosphere, memory, grief, and poetic transitions
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Ink spreads from a few intentional origins.
- Dense black cores feather into diluted grey edges.
- Paper texture and untouched white space remain visible.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Ink Bloom on Paper** style. Use an asymmetrical composition with large areas of unmarked paper. Let blooms spread at varied rates, merge selectively, and pair soft diffusion with one or two decisive brush traces.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining behavior above and keep all supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject matter, on-screen wording, duration, aspect ratio, typeface, and accent color.
- Rendering may use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL according to the needs of the effect.

## Avoid

- Smoke-like clouds, glossy liquid, full-frame coverage, many colors, or symmetrical ornamental splashes.
- Do not reproduce historical sample copy or hard-coded layouts from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice readability for motion density.

# Historic Border Palimpsest

## Overview

A purpose-built visual system in which dated borders advance, split, recede, and dissolve over persistent geography.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartography and Geographic Storytelling
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: dated borders advance, split, recede, and dissolve over persistent geography

## Signature Features

- Make the process whereby dated borders advance, split, recede, and dissolve over persistent geography the dominant visual mechanism.
- Establish the specific causes, constraints, and scale governing **Historic Border Palimpsest** before revealing their consequences.
- Let labels, texture, and camera movement support the historic border palimpsest mechanism instead of competing with it.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Historic Border Palimpsest** style. Build the composition around a clear demonstration of how dated borders advance, split, recede, and dissolve over persistent geography. Preserve the governing spatial, temporal, material, or rhythmic relationship; add atmosphere and detail only after that relationship reads clearly.

## Avoid

- Do not replace **Historic Border Palimpsest** with generic particles, glowing lines, a dashboard, or a slideshow.
- Reject motion that makes **Historic Border Palimpsest** physically or conceptually inconsistent, even when the surface effect looks attractive.

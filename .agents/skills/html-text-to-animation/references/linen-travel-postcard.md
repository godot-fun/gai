# Linen Travel Postcard

## Overview

A saturated mid-century postcard whose color catches only the raised weave, oversized dimensional lettering, and printed scene come alive after a tabletop push-in.

## Signature Features

- Fine crosshatched linen stock with white skips in recessed weave
- Airbrushed sky blue, grass green, pink, teal, and cream
- Large frontal landmark occupying half the card
- Navy dimensional place name with white keyline and inset miniatures
- One-to-two-pixel color drift and star glints

## Style Prompt

Print on visibly woven cream postcard stock. Let ink touch primarily the raised warp and weft so a fine crosshatch and small white skips remain throughout every gradient. Use saturated hand-separated sky blue, grass green, pink, and teal with soft airbrushed transitions, retaining one-to-two-pixel registration drift. Place a large frontal or side-view landmark, vehicle, object, or character at roughly half the card height and simplify perspective toward the illustrative rather than photographic. Add a few four-point star glints to polished lit surfaces. Across the upper third, set a large invented place name or title in navy dimensional capitals with a white keyline, embedding a tiny landscape or subject detail inside each letter, and attach a small red ribbon above. Start with the whole postcard resting on a table, push until its edges leave frame, then animate only the printed world—water, pedestrians, flags, clouds, or traffic—and continue with a gentle lateral move.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

gradients without linen breakup, grey desaturated ink, realistic deep perspective, generic text unrelated to place, modern clean typography, and animation beginning before the camera enters the card.

# Prestige Announcement Trailer

## Overview

An ultra-wide near-black reveal uses centered questions, dates, figures, product name, and release information separated by slow bloom, held silence, and dissolves through darkness.

## Signature Features

- Wide cinematic letterbox and warm near-black vignette
- Sparse drifting dust
- One centered idea per screen
- Soft light bloom, long hold, and black dissolve
- Single major flare and impact at the name reveal

## Style Prompt

Use a wide cinematic frame with a warm near-black background, subtle vignette, and only a few slow dust motes. Reduce the announcement to a deliberate sequence: one question, one date or context, a short run of carefully chosen figures, the central name or title, and the release or action date. Give each idea its own centered screen. Fade it in from a soft local bloom, hold long enough to absorb, then dissolve fully through black before the next. Increase rhythmic pulse and scale gradually through the number sequence. Reserve the brightest flare, strongest visual impact, and largest typography for the name reveal, then decrescendo into the final date and silence. Use premium type spacing and restrained warm metallic or white color.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

hard cuts, constant lens flare, trailer clichés unrelated to content, many words per screen, fake urgency, microscopic legal text, dust storms, moving cameras, and every card receiving the same intensity.

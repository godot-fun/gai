# Fluorescence Microscopy

## Overview

Selected cellular structures glow in distinct emission colors against a deep field as focus moves through optical depth.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: cell biology, medical explanation, hidden structure, and scale

## Signature Features

- Compose a deep-black field with a few labeled cellular channels—such as cyan membranes, magenta nuclei, and green filaments—each restricted to its structure.
- Scan through focal planes so organelles enter and leave focus, then toggle channels to show colocalization; use a stable microscope view and calibrated scale bar.
- Use tiny white scientific labels and one unobtrusive channel legend outside dense cells.

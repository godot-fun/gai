# Monumental Shan Shui Journey

## Overview

A vertical-depth landscape leads from foreground path and water through pavilion and travelers toward towering mist-separated peaks.

## Metadata

- Collection: Chinese Art Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- High-distance composition with three depth zones
- Textured rock strokes, contour lines, and layered ink washes
- Waterfall, river, path, bridge, or pavilion guiding the eye
- Tiny human scale against monumental mountains
- Mist as unpainted structure between spatial layers

## Style Prompt

Compose the landscape as a journey through near, middle, and far distance rather than a single scenic backdrop. Anchor the foreground with dark rocks, pines, bank, or path; place a bridge, pavilion, boat, traveler, waterfall, or river in the middle; lift pale mountain masses behind through reserved bands of mist. Build cliffs from structural contour strokes and varied texture marks that follow geology, then layer diluted washes to establish depth. Keep distant peaks paler and softer while foreground ink stays dry and dense. Let water remain largely paper white with only current, bank, and reflection lines. Make people very small but legible so human scale clarifies the mountain's magnitude. Move the camera slowly along the implied route, reveal a waterfall through fog, or travel from path to pavilion before lifting toward the peaks.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

photographic one-point perspective, uniform mountain triangles, full grey wash across mist, giant protagonists, random ink clouds, mirrored symmetry, and busy motion across every layer.

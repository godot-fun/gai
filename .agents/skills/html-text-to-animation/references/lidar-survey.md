# LiDAR Survey

## Overview

Measured laser points form a navigable cloud, then condense under a scan plane into a surface that can be inspected.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: terrain, buildings, archaeology, infrastructure, and spatial measurement
- Complexity: Advanced

## Signature Features

- Authentic point-cloud density and scale.
- A scanning line or cutting plane.
- Cloud-to-surface condensation.
- Hairline measurement after reconstruction.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **LiDAR Survey** style. Carry the piece through one designed camera flight inside the point cloud. Let a plane reveal structure, transition to a raking-lit surface, and add only measurements supported by the data.

Translate the user's material into this grammar rather than copying the source examples. Preserve supplied wording and factual attribution, and choose an HTML-native or composited implementation appropriate to the evidence and visual material.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, evidence, copy, duration, aspect ratio, pacing, and implementation method.
- Adapt palette details only when the defining visual hierarchy remains intact.

## Avoid

- Generic particle fields, arbitrary neon colors, impossible point density, static screenshots, or measurements detached from geometry.
- Never invent documents, measurements, quotations, sources, or factual evidence.
- Do not use surface effects as a substitute for the style's narrative structure.

# Granular Athletic Lithograph

## Overview

A pale 1920s sports poster built entirely from four particulate inks, classical bodies, and measured frontal movement.

## Signature Features

- Sea blue, ochre, deep brown, and warm coral on aged cream
- Every value formed from clustered lithographic grain rather than flat fill
- Classical proportion and a dark terminator line along limbs
- Horizontal ranks of athletes
- Long-lens frontal and ground-level views

## Style Prompt

Print from four slightly misregistered stones: sea blue for sky, water, and cool shade; ochre for ground and skin; deep brown for contours, clothing, and dark structure; warm coral for limited accents. Build every printed value from fine lithographic particles gathered into soft clusters. Light regions cover only part of the paper, middle values increase particle density, and near-solids approach full ink while retaining occasional pinholes; never scatter isolated dots across untouched paper. Keep the overall print pale and calm. Draw athletes with classical proportions, heads about one eighth of height, ochre on the lit side, and a single brown boundary turning along the shadow side of each limb. Arrange groups in readable horizontal ranks rather than icon-like stick figures. Let motion remain measured and use water spray, dust, hair, fabric, and repeated stride to convey speed. Favor a frontal long lens and an extremely low ground-level angle; integrate a restrained serif title band only when useful.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

coarse noise, vector flats, pictogram bodies, black-heavy contrast, and uniformly dense grain.

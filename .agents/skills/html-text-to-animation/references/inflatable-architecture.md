# Inflatable Architecture

## Overview

Soft membranes expand from packed fabric into inhabitable volumes whose pressure remains visibly alive.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: temporary spaces, events, transformation, and soft structure

## Signature Features

- Begin with folded coated fabric, seams, valves, anchoring, and a blower before revealing the pressurized volume.
- Inflate progressively from chambers, show wrinkles smoothing under pressure, then respond softly to occupants and wind.
- For **Inflatable Architecture**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Inflatable Architecture** style. Begin with folded coated fabric, seams, valves, anchoring, and a blower before revealing the pressurized volume. Inflate progressively from chambers, show wrinkles smoothing under pressure, then respond softly to occupants and wind. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid soap bubbles, rigid plastic geometry, instant scale-up, absent anchors, or sharp creases remaining under full pressure.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

# Fiber-Optic Light Weave

## Overview

Thousands of flexible strands carry pulses of light through a woven luminous volume.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: connectivity, communication, neural metaphors, and immersive technology

## Signature Features

- Build a dense bundle of hair-thin dark fibers with luminous tips and a few visible woven crossings; use black, deep blue, and restrained cyan, violet, and warm-white pulses.
- Launch packets from distinct inputs, route them through bends, and let only endpoints flare; track along the bundle before pulling back to expose network topology.
- Use monospaced micro-labels at ports and a clean grotesk headline outside the fiber mass.

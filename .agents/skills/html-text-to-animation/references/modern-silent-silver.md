# Modern Silent Silver

## Overview

A clean contemporary recreation of late-silent-era staging with elegant monochrome sets, intertitles, restrained wear, and one terracotta-colored protagonist.

- Luminous whites, soft middle greys, deep blacks, and modest silver glow
- Fine grain, faint flicker, rare dust, and pillarboxed one-point-three-three frame
- Art Deco intertitles in the user's language
- Large orderly stage interiors, stairways, railings, curtains, and backlit crowds
- Only the full protagonist carries terracotta color

## Style Prompt

Present the image as a carefully photographed modern silent film rather than a heavily damaged antique print. Use luminous highlights, gentle middle grey, deep black, a mild S-curve, grey halation near lamps, fine grain, faint exposure flutter, and only occasional dust. Place a 1.33 image inside the wider output with clean black side bars. Design full-frame intertitles on black with an original Art Deco border and widely spaced lettering in the user's language. Build the subject's world as a grand, ordered interior or theatrical set with iron stair rails, layered platforms, curtains, polished floors, and backlit crowd silhouettes; favor elegant profiles and decisive pantomime. Keep every element monochrome except the complete protagonist, modeled through several values of restrained terracotta. Lamps, fire, reflections, and shadows remain grey. Use smooth pushes and pullbacks, let intertitles snap in cleanly, and cut on a completed gesture such as a bow, turn, raised hand, or exchanged object.

## Avoid

heavy scratches, sepia tint, color bleeding into greys, frantic slapstick speed, tiny underbuilt sets, and distressed title cards that compromise legibility.

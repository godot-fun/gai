# Dunhuang Mineral Mural

## Overview

An aged cave-wall painting combines mineral pigments, flying celestial figures, patterned halos, cloud ribbons, and surface loss without becoming a generic fantasy poster.

## Metadata

- Collection: Chinese Art Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Ochre plaster with cracks, abrasion, and missing pigment
- Azurite, malachite, cinnabar, earth yellow, white, and carbon line
- Flying figures with long ribbons and controlled weightlessness
- Mandorlas, lotus, clouds, instruments, and architectural bands
- Slow lateral reveal under grazing cave light

## Style Prompt

Paint directly onto warm ochre plaster and let its grain, cracks, edge loss, abrasions, and earlier repair remain visible beneath mineral color. Use azurite blue, malachite green, cinnabar red, earth yellow, shell white, and carbon-brown contour in a harmonized, age-softened range. Arrange flying celestial figures or subject-specific airborne forms around a central scene, giving them long ribbon paths, turned torsos, expressive hands, and graceful but structurally clear weightlessness. Integrate lotus motifs, flame or petal halos, stylized cloud bands, instruments, donors, animals, and architectural borders where relevant. Model with flat color plus narrow darker edges and line detail, not glossy volume. Animate ribbons, cloud scrolls, drifting pigment dust, and one gesture while the camera tracks slowly across the mural under grazing cave light.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

saturated fantasy concept art, random mandalas, clean new walls, floating figures without directional ribbon flow, gold digital glow, thick oil shading, and excessive damage that erases the scene.

# Redacted Document Reveal

## Overview

Opaque bars, partial evidence, and released passages control what can be known at each moment.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: investigation, secrecy, archives, and information ethics
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Use a credible document hierarchy, black or paper-colored redaction bars, page numbers, stamps, and visible provenance.
- Reveal only authorized fragments in deliberate stages, preserve line length clues where appropriate, and distinguish removal from later declassification.
- For **Redacted Document Reveal**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.

## Avoid

- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

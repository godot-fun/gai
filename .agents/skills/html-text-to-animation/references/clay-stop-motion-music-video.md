# Clay Stop-Motion Music Video

## Overview

Handmade clay characters perform through tactile pose-to-pose animation synchronized to a song.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML animation, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: playful music, character comedy, and lighthearted interludes

## Signature Features

- Fingerprints and handmade texture.
- Frame-stepped motion.
- Performance follows musical structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Clay Stop-Motion Music Video** style. Design a small repeatable set, block poses around beats and lyrics, and use clay transformations only where they serve the song.

## Avoid

- Avoid frictionless digital motion, textureless clay, or transformations that ignore the song phrasing and beat structure.

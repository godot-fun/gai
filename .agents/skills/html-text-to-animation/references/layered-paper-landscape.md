# Layered Paper Landscape

## Overview

Stacked paper silhouettes move at different speeds to create a gentle handcrafted world. Choose this for wide environmental depth built from horizontal paper layers and gentle parallax.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: childhood, storytelling, nature, and tender passages

## Signature Features

- Foreground, middle ground, and background read as separate paper layers.
- Soft contact shadows describe overlap rather than realism.
- Near layers move faster than distant layers.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Layered Paper Landscape** style. Build a simple scene from matte cut-paper silhouettes, subtle fiber texture, and a harmonious muted palette. Use planar sliding and parallax, with small physical reveals such as a house or sun emerging between layers.

## Avoid

- Choose **Layered Paper Cut** for object- or character-led scenes, or **Shadow-Box Diorama** for a framed cavity.
- Glossy plastic, deep 3D extrusion, hard drop shadows, elastic motion, or too many tiny cut pieces.

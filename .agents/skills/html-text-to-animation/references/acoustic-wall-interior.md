# Acoustic Wall Interior

## Overview

An off-center painted room where a large upholstered or distressed wall becomes the visual field and thin gold waveforms show speech changing from broken to controlled.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: An off-center painted room where a large upholstered or distressed wall becomes the visual field and thin gold waveforms show speech changing from broken to controlled.

## Signature Features

- Deep brown and muted gold diamond-tufted wall as primary state
- Cool grey-green worn plaster as quiet secondary state
- Clean-edged forms with only a faint directional brush veil
- Four to eight restrained gold acoustic lines
- Small displaced figure and one sound-source close-up

## Style Prompt

Let the wall dominate the composition and push the person toward an edge rather than centering the frame. In the principal state, use diamond-tufted upholstery with muted gold laid over deep brown, each button catching a small brass highlight, and place one warm golden field behind the microphone or other sound source. In quieter scenes, switch to a cool grey-green plaster wall locally worn through to blue, pink, ochre, and raw plaster, with tide marks and fine cracks; keep irregular loss below one third of the wall. Preserve clean silhouettes and overlay only a subtle directional oil texture. Visualize speech as four to eight hairline gold traces spreading across the wall: initially spiked, interrupted, or uneven, then gradually smoother and more regular. They should remain embedded in the paint, never glow like neon. Leave broad negative wall space around a small off-center figure. Push slowly through the room and insert one precise close view of the sound source before returning to the relationship between body and wall.

## Avoid

centered symmetry, chunky stipple, map-shaped peeling everywhere, fluorescent waveforms, oscilloscope UI panels, and texture that obscures faces or objects.

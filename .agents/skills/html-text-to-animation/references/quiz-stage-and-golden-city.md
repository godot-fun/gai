# Quiz Stage and Golden City

## Overview

A two-mode story alternating between a luminous blue-violet question stage and a dense orange-teal real-world environment, with no imitation of a branded show.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A two-mode story alternating between a luminous blue-violet question stage and a dense orange-teal real-world environment, with no imitation of a branded show.

## Signature Features

- Bright underlit circular platform and concentric blue rings
- Full audience, smoke-cut violet-blue beams, and generic hexagonal answer fields
- One amber locked choice and controlled tension falloff
- Hard full-frame cuts to crowded golden-hour city imagery
- Magenta, teal, and orange accents across clean rounded forms

## Style Prompt

Use two complete visual modes. In the primary mode, build an original television question stage from a glowing glass circle, concentric blue light rings, a full audience, and moving fixtures casting violet-blue beams through light haze. Show answer choices as long generic hexagonal fields on a large screen; lock one field to amber at the decision point. Keep the stage bright, full, and illustration-like, with rounded clean people and soft dimensional shading. As tension rises, darken the perimeter gradually but never beyond half the frame or into total black, then narrow beams toward one participant. Push between the screen and seat cluster. For the secondary mode, hard-cut full-frame to a dense golden-hour environment relevant to the user's story—rooftops, lanes, cables, crowds, workshops, markets, or transit—graded in saturated orange and teal with magenta accents and slow lateral observation. Never combine modes in one frame.

## Avoid

real program logos or signature layouts, empty black studios, equal illumination during tension, grey vacant city scenes, and generic crowds without local detail.

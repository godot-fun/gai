# Glass Mosaic Assembly

## Overview

Cut tesserae rotate into mortar, building image through reflected color, grout, and local imperfection. Choose this when cut glass tesserae, mortar, grout, and reflected light are visibly assembled into an image.

## Metadata

- Collection: Print, Textile and Physical Craft
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: collective pieces, sacred spaces, light, and durable imagery

## Signature Features

- Use irregular colored tesserae, cut edges, mortar bed, grout gaps, and directional reflections within a bounded design.
- Place pieces from key contours outward, rotate each for light, press into mortar, then grout and clean the surface.
- For **Glass Mosaic Assembly**, let tool marks, joins, surface response, and small process variation provide material truth; choose colors plausible for the named craft.

# Data-Moshing Transition

## Overview

Video compression motion vectors carry one scene into another after keyframe information is deliberately removed.

## Metadata

- Collection: Experimental Camera and Image Processes
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: digital rupture, memory, transformation, and media critique

## Signature Features

- Begin with a readable compressed frame, remove an intended keyframe, and let inherited motion vectors carry blocks of the old image into the new scene.
- Trigger corruption at a motivated cut, sustain it for a short transformation, then restore a clean intra-frame before text must be read.
- For **Data-Moshing Transition**, use a palette, contrast curve, grain structure, and edge behavior native to the named capture or darkroom process.

Frame typography as a caption, contact-sheet note, instrument readout, or title outside the image-forming process. Let the capture, exposure, encoding, or feedback mechanism determine transitions instead of applying generic glitch or filter presets.

## Avoid

- Avoid random RGB glitch, scan lines, endless corruption, unreadable text, or smooth optical-flow morphing.
- Do not imitate a photographic, optical, or compression process with unrelated glitch decoration or a generic color filter.

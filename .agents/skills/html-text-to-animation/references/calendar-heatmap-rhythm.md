# Calendar Heatmap Rhythm

## Overview

Daily cells accumulate intensity into weeks, seasons, streaks, gaps, and long-term behavioral rhythm.

## Metadata

- Collection: Interfaces and Information Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: habits, activity history, consistency, and seasonality

## Signature Features

- Use a year or multi-month grid with consistent weekday alignment, restrained intensity steps, and annotated streak boundaries.
- Fill days chronologically, hold on gaps and seasonal shifts, and compare periods without reordering calendar geometry.
- For **Calendar Heatmap Rhythm**, use a near-black, slate, or warm-white operational surface with one semantic accent and color reserved for status, confidence, or ownership.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Calendar Heatmap Rhythm** style. Use a year or multi-month grid with consistent weekday alignment, restrained intensity steps, and annotated streak boundaries. Fill days chronologically, hold on gaps and seasonal shifts, and compare periods without reordering calendar geometry. Use a disciplined operational surface with color assigned consistently to status, confidence, ownership, or risk. Set typography in a compact UI sans serif or monospaced face; align values, units, timestamps, and labels to a stable grid. Organize the message around the operational decision so every state change, alert, route, or dependency has a readable cause.

## Avoid

- Avoid random mosaic color, misleading nonlinear scales, omitted missing days, or celebratory particles over sparse data.
- Do not add dashboard chrome, alerts, metrics, or map layers that do not support the operational decision being shown.

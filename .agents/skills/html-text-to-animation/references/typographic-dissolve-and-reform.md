# Typographic Dissolve and Reform

## Overview

One statement breaks into particles and reforms as a different statement, making a change of interpretation visible.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: reframing, recovery, changed beliefs, and emotional transformation

## Signature Features

- The source sentence remains legible before it breaks apart.
- Fragments retain continuity between the old and new wording.
- The destination statement receives a calm, complete hold.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Typographic Dissolve and Reform** style. Stage four clear phases: readable source, controlled dissolution, guided transit, and deliberate reassembly. Let fragments follow varied but coherent arcs and use a subtle color shift to mark the new understanding.

## Avoid

- Generic explosions, unrelated confetti, instant morphing, or a final phrase that never becomes fully readable.

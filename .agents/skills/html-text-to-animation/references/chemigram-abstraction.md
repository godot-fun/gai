# Chemigram Abstraction

## Overview

Developer, fixer, and resists act directly on photographic paper without a camera, producing chemical gestures.

## Metadata

- Collection: Experimental Camera and Image Processes
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: material experiments, abstraction, decay, and photographic process

## Signature Features

- Treat glossy photographic paper as a reactive surface for developer, fixer, varnish, wax, or oil resists.
- Apply chemistry locally, let edges creep and crystallize, peel or dissolve resist, and stop reactions at distinct stages.
- For **Chemigram Abstraction**, use a palette, contrast curve, grain structure, and edge behavior native to the named capture or darkroom process.

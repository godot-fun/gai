# Metabolist Capsule City

## Overview

Plug-in residential modules attach, detach, and expand around permanent infrastructural cores.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: urban futures, modularity, growth, and adaptable systems

## Signature Features

- Anchor repeated plug-in capsules to a permanent service core with visible connectors, circulation, and phased growth.
- Attach, replace, and remove modules along valid sockets while the camera rises through generations of the structure.
- For **Metabolist Capsule City**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Metabolist Capsule City** style. Anchor repeated plug-in capsules to a permanent service core with visible connectors, circulation, and phased growth. Attach, replace, and remove modules along valid sockets while the camera rises through generations of the structure. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid random shipping containers, modules with no services, organic blob growth, crowded flying traffic, or scale inconsistency.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

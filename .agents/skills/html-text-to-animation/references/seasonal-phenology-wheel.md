# Seasonal Phenology Wheel

A purpose-built visual system in which annual rings synchronize flowering, migration, rainfall, temperature, and harvest.

## Metadata

- Collection: Nature and Ecological Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: annual rings synchronize flowering, migration, rainfall, temperature, and harvest

- Establish the specific causes, constraints, and scale governing **Seasonal Phenology Wheel** before revealing their consequences.
- Let labels, texture, and camera movement support the seasonal phenology wheel mechanism instead of competing with it.

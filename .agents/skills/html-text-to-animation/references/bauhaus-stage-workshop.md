# Bauhaus Stage Workshop

## Overview

Primary geometry, masked performers, mechanical choreography, and functional color inhabit a constructed stage.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: design history, abstraction, education, and disciplined performance

## Signature Features

- Stage primary geometric costumes and props on a functional black, white, red, yellow, and blue theater grid.
- Choreograph circles, rods, planes, and masked bodies in mechanical counts, alternating ensemble patterns with held tableaux.
- For **Bauhaus Stage Workshop**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Bauhaus Stage Workshop** style. Stage primary geometric costumes and props on a functional black, white, red, yellow, and blue theater grid. Choreograph circles, rods, planes, and masked bodies in mechanical counts, alternating ensemble patterns with held tableaux. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid generic Memphis decoration, gradients, naturalistic acting, cluttered scenery, or geometry moving without choreographic rules.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

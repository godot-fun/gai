# Semaphore Letter Performance

## Overview

A pair of flags moves through standardized arm positions to spell messages one character at a time.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: communication history, performance, coded messages, and maritime stories
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Use standardized two-flag positions around a clear human figure, high-contrast flags, and an unobstructed frontal or rear convention.
- Move through neutral between letters, hold each valid position long enough to decode, and pace whole words rather than waving continuously.
- For **Semaphore Letter Performance**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Semaphore Letter Performance** style. Use standardized two-flag positions around a clear human figure, high-contrast flags, and an unobstructed frontal or rear convention. Move through neutral between letters, hold each valid position long enough to decode, and pace whole words rather than waving continuously. Build hierarchy from glyph anatomy, spacing, reading direction, linguistic timing, and the specific encoding or word structure being demonstrated. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.
- Change typographic scale, neutral surface, accent color, and motion amplitude while preserving valid glyphs, reading order, timing, and decodability.

## Avoid

- Avoid invented poses, mirrored letters without notice, flags crossing the body ambiguously, rapid dance motion, or camera rotation.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

# Transit-Time Space Distortion

## Overview

A purpose-built visual system in which geography stretches or compresses according to a declared travel-time metric.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartography and Geographic Storytelling
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: geography stretches or compresses according to a declared travel-time metric

## Signature Features

- Make the process whereby geography stretches or compresses according to a declared travel-time metric the dominant visual mechanism.
- Establish the specific causes, constraints, and scale governing **Transit-Time Space Distortion** before revealing their consequences.
- Let labels, texture, and camera movement support the transit-time space distortion mechanism instead of competing with it.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Transit-Time Space Distortion** style. Build the composition around a clear demonstration of how geography stretches or compresses according to a declared travel-time metric. Preserve the governing spatial, temporal, material, or rhythmic relationship; add atmosphere and detail only after that relationship reads clearly.

## Avoid

- Do not replace **Transit-Time Space Distortion** with generic particles, glowing lines, a dashboard, or a slideshow.
- Reject motion that makes **Transit-Time Space Distortion** physically or conceptually inconsistent, even when the surface effect looks attractive.

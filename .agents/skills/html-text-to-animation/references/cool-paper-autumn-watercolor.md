# Cool-Paper Autumn Watercolor

## Overview

A layered transparent landscape where warm leaves float against cold mist, pale sky, and untouched paper light.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Two or three nested transparent glazes per shape
- Pooled drying rims, restrained granulation, and a few blooms only in wet fields
- Gold, scarlet, and rust against cool grey-blue and violet-grey
- Paper white reserved for highlights and pale objects
- Slow pan with foreground leaves crossing the lens

## Style Prompt

Work transparently on cold-press paper, defining clear shapes before laying two or three successively smaller and deeper glazes. Let each layer dry with a slightly darker tide line and gentle pigment granulation in the tooth. Introduce only a few backruns in broad wet sky, water, or ground washes, never across faces or tightly controlled figures. Preserve bare paper as the brightest white and plan highlights before painting. Use autumn gold, scarlet, and rust for foliage and small accents against cool grey-blue mist, violet-grey shadow, and subdued green-ochre earth. Keep every sky pale and cool, regardless of time of day. Build tree crowns from a light wash, scalloped clusters, darker touches, and scattered leaves; when trees are absent, transfer the warm notes to shrubs, roofs, clothing, or objects. Paint water and sky wet-in-wet, but use dry brush for ripples and crisp edges. Light from one side, pan slowly across layered depth, push gently toward the subject, and drift a few leaves through the foreground.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

opaque gouache coverage, airbrushed blur, hard vector edges, muddy mixtures, saturated sunset skies, and decorative blooms everywhere.

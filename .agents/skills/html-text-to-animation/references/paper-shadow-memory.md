# Paper Shadow Memory

## Overview

Cut-paper silhouettes perform behind warm light with restrained sound and intimate vertical staging.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML animation, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: home, family, memory, and gentle folklore

## Signature Features

- Flat articulated paper silhouettes.
- Warm transmitted light.
- Quiet sound and slow gesture.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Paper Shadow Memory** style. Stage profiles in layered depth, move joints with physical restraint, and let light and shadow carry emotional changes without busy camera work.

## Avoid

- Avoid weightless joints, glossy digital silhouettes, busy camera moves, or cool frontal lighting that destroys the transmitted-paper atmosphere.

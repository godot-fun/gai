# Cyanotype Engineering Drama

## Overview

An animated engineering blueprint in which measurement, construction, annotation, and one hand-painted subject coexist on aged blue paper. Choose this when blueprint-like engineering imagery must retain cyanotype exposure, paper chemistry, and dramatic narrative light.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Mottled Prussian-blue sheet with folds, stains, and greenish overexposed edges
- Sharp printed white plans with cyan exposure fringe
- Dense elevations, sections, details, dimensions, and title block
- Red-pencil notes and motion paths
- One enlarged opaque warm gouache subject

## Style Prompt

Fill the frame with a physical cyanotype sheet, revealing the walnut drawing table only during the final pullback. Vary the Prussian blue through uneven exposure, fold lines, pale water stains, and slightly green overexposed borders. Draft the subject densely in printed white: orthographic elevations, sections, exploded details, dimension chains, dashed hidden lines, single-stroke engineering lettering, symbols, and a title block. Make main contours stronger than section hatch and dimensions; break lines in weak exposure areas and give them a subtle cyan halo rather than neon glow. Add concise red-pencil annotations, arrows, revisions, and moving paths in the user's language. Paint the lone narrative subject by hand in opaque warm gouache, enlarged beyond blueprint scale and modeled with several values of one hue. Animate plan layers with slight depth during a push, let annotations draw themselves, and cut into a detail circle when the mechanism matters.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

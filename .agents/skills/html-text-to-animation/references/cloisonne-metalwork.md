# Cloisonné Metalwork

## Overview

Fine metal partitions define cells that fill with colored enamel and polish into a jewel-like surface.

## Metadata

- Collection: Print, Textile and Physical Craft
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: ornament, transformation, luxury craft, and symbolic motifs

## Signature Features

- Use a metal base with soldered wire partitions, powdered enamel cells, repeated firing depth, and polished highlights.
- Bend and attach wire first, fill colors in controlled layers, fire with slight settling, then grind and polish the surface.
- For **Cloisonné Metalwork**, let tool marks, joins, surface response, and small process variation provide material truth; choose colors plausible for the named craft.

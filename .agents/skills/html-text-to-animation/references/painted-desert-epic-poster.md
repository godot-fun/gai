# Painted Desert Epic Poster

## Overview

A hand-painted adventure poster with solid three-value forms, cobalt-to-sand atmosphere, and a central wind-cut gesture.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Opaque gouache underpainting enriched by directional oil highlights
- Clear light, middle, and dark planes on every major form
- Cobalt sky, sandy earth, ochre-red bodies, and violet distance
- Large centered figure on a strong diagonal with streaming fabric
- Slow rise into a low close view followed by a whip exit

## Style Prompt

Establish each object first as a solid opaque middle-value shape, then model it with directional strokes that follow anatomy, cloth, terrain, and architecture. Give every major form a clear light, middle, and dark family, reserving smaller brushes for face and hands. Blend important edges softly while keeping the final warm-cream edge highlight sharp; use dry brush only for dust, rough cloth, and distant ranks. Move the sky from deep cobalt to pale blue and warm it around a low sun. Use sandy gold for any ground family, ochre-red for skin, earth, and costume, and violet for distant shadow, so warm and cool zones remain decisive. Center the principal figure at the largest scale, drive its pose across a diagonal, and let cloth, banner, hair, or dust trail in the same wind. Place looser, smaller figures behind to establish depth. Add board grain, a warm vignette, and atmospheric dust. Rock or surge the action, push slowly upward into a low close view, then whip past the moving edge.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

evenly scratched brush noise, digital flat fills, cold-only palettes, timid poses, and microscopic poster subjects.

# Chinese Shadow Theatre

## Overview

Translucent carved-hide puppets perform against a cotton screen lit from behind by warm oil lamps. Choose this for Chinese carved-hide shadow-puppet conventions, warm backlight, side-on articulation, and traditional screen staging.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: folklore, myth, historical allegory, and ceremonial storytelling

## Signature Features

- Jointed flat puppets with lace-like cutwork.
- Dyed translucent hide glows in transmitted light.
- Control rods and articulated motion remain believable.
- Screen texture and warm lamp falloff are visible.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Chinese Shadow Theatre** style. Stage profiles and overlapping silhouettes like a live performance. Move joints with measured rod-driven arcs, let color deepen where layers overlap, and use side entrances rather than camera cuts.

## Avoid

- Choose **Wayang Kulit Shadow Performance** for Indonesian epic staging, or **Ottoman Shadow Comedy** for dialogue-led comic performance.
- Opaque black silhouettes, 3D characters, modern neon backlight, invisible articulation, or frantic puppet motion.

# Inflation Basket Chronicle

## Overview

A persistent household basket changes price, weight, and purchasing burden across dated periods.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: prices, purchasing power, household costs, and change over time

## Signature Features

- Preserve a visible weighted basket while individual prices and substitution choices change over time.
- Separate price level, inflation rate, wages, and purchasing power with explicit baselines and periods.
- Show that different households experience different burdens through transparent weighting scenarios.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Inflation Basket Chronicle** style. Place a dated, weighted basket of recognizable goods and services on a stable ledger-like stage. Change item prices with labeled causes or source periods, aggregate them into an index, and compare the result with income or purchasing power. Allow alternate household weights to reveal distributional differences without changing the underlying price observations.

## Avoid

- Avoid using one expensive item as proof of general inflation or confusing a high price level with a high inflation rate.
- Do not silently change basket weights, base years, units, or nominal-versus-real values.

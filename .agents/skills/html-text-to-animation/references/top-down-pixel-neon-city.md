# Top-Down Pixel Neon City

## Overview

A dense top-down cyber-city map with rain, umbrellas, traffic, bridges, readable signage, and strict low-resolution pixel construction. Choose this for an overhead urban simulation with pixel traffic, wet neon streets, and readable city circulation.

## Signature Features

- Fixed low-resolution canvas and nearest-neighbor enlargement
- Oblique top-down street crossed by an elevated bridge
- Large destination screen at the street end
- Pixel rain, umbrellas, compact vehicles, and flying traffic
- Limited neon palette with stepped animation

## Style Prompt

Render on a small fixed canvas and enlarge only by integer nearest-neighbor scaling. Show a long street from an oblique top-down viewpoint, pack its edges with signs, stalls, windows, roof machinery, cables, and small interiors, and cross it with one bridge or elevated rail. Place a large destination screen or sign at the far end using short original text relevant to the user's subject. Build pedestrians with distinct umbrella colors and stepped walk cycles; move compact ground vehicles along lanes and only a few flying vehicles across separate depth. Draw rain as short pixel clusters and accumulate bright broken reflections on the road. Use a limited palette of deep violet, navy, cyan, magenta, warm amber, and a small red accent. Animate in discrete frames with no subpixel interpolation, and scroll or drift the camera slowly enough for environmental detail to read.

# Typographic Tunnel

## Overview

Concentric rings of language create a tunnel that pulls the viewer deeper into a thought.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: rumination, obsession, unanswered questions, and conceptual descent
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Characters or phrases form repeated depth rings.
- A stable vanishing point creates forward travel.
- Rotation and scale suggest descent without sacrificing orientation.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Typographic Tunnel** style. Arrange repeated language on receding circular planes and move steadily toward a controlled central void. Increase contrast and scale toward the foreground, keep rotation slow, and resolve at a final word or point of light.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining behavior above and keep all supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject matter, on-screen wording, duration, aspect ratio, typeface, and accent color.
- Rendering may use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL according to the needs of the effect.

## Avoid

- Chaotic spinning, nausea-inducing camera motion, unreadable perspective, or endless travel without a destination.
- Do not reproduce historical sample copy or hard-coded layouts from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice readability for motion density.

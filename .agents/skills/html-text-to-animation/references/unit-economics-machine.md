# Unit-Economics Machine

## Overview

One customer travels through an operating machine that exposes acquisition cost, contribution margin, retention, and payback.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: acquisition cost, margin, payback, retention, and per-customer economics

## Signature Features

- Follow a persistent customer unit through acquisition, service cost, repeat value, churn risk, and payback.
- Keep cohort assumptions and time periods visible while cumulative value approaches or fails to reach acquisition cost.
- Separate revenue from gross margin and show sensitivity when retention, price, or variable cost changes.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Unit-Economics Machine** style. Send one representative customer token through a precise modular machine whose stations add acquisition cost, revenue, variable cost, retention probability, and cumulative contribution. Synchronize the moving unit with a payback timeline and expose every assumption. Let parameter changes alter the mechanism visibly instead of merely updating a headline number.

## Avoid

- Avoid presenting lifetime value without a time horizon, retention model, or margin basis.
- Do not turn the mechanism into a generic funnel or imply profitability from revenue alone.

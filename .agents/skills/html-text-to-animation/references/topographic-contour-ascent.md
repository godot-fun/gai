# Topographic Contour Ascent

## Overview

A purpose-built visual system in which indexed contours rise into coherent ridges, valleys, slopes, and landmarks.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartography and Geographic Storytelling
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: indexed contours rise into coherent ridges, valleys, slopes, and landmarks

## Signature Features

- Make the process whereby indexed contours rise into coherent ridges, valleys, slopes, and landmarks the dominant visual mechanism.
- Establish the specific causes, constraints, and scale governing **Topographic Contour Ascent** before revealing their consequences.
- Let labels, texture, and camera movement support the topographic contour ascent mechanism instead of competing with it.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Topographic Contour Ascent** style. Build the composition around a clear demonstration of how indexed contours rise into coherent ridges, valleys, slopes, and landmarks. Preserve the governing spatial, temporal, material, or rhythmic relationship; add atmosphere and detail only after that relationship reads clearly.

## Avoid

- Do not replace **Topographic Contour Ascent** with generic particles, glowing lines, a dashboard, or a slideshow.
- Reject motion that makes **Topographic Contour Ascent** physically or conceptually inconsistent, even when the surface effect looks attractive.

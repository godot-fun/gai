# Chromatic Shadow Theatre

## Overview

Multiple colored lights cast overlapping shadows whose intersections create new hues and silhouettes.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: relationships, overlap, performance, and color education
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Use a pale stage with three separated red, green, and blue lamps; subjects remain neutral while overlapping shadows mix subtractively into distinct colored regions.
- Move one lamp or performer at a time so shadow displacement explains source position; keep a frontal theatrical camera and show lamp geometry when useful.
- Use black or dark-gray stage typography with labels matching each light source.

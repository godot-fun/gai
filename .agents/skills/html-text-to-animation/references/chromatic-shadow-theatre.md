# Chromatic Shadow Theatre

## Overview

Multiple colored lights cast overlapping shadows whose intersections create new hues and silhouettes.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: relationships, overlap, performance, and color education

## Signature Features

- Use a pale stage with three separated red, green, and blue lamps; subjects remain neutral while overlapping shadows mix subtractively into distinct colored regions.
- Move one lamp or performer at a time so shadow displacement explains source position; keep a frontal theatrical camera and show lamp geometry when useful.
- Use black or dark-gray stage typography with labels matching each light source.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Chromatic Shadow Theatre** style. Use a pale stage with three separated red, green, and blue lamps; subjects remain neutral while overlapping shadows mix subtractively into distinct colored regions. Move one lamp or performer at a time so shadow displacement explains source position; keep a frontal theatrical camera and show lamp geometry when useful. Use black or dark-gray stage typography with labels matching each light source. Make every transition arise from the optical or scientific process itself, and preserve enough negative space for the supplied message to remain primary.

## Avoid

- Avoid colored silhouettes instead of colored shadows, ambient rainbow wash, hidden light logic, soft shadows with no separation, or excessive scenery.

# Sports Telestrator

## Overview

Real match footage is paused, rewound, annotated, and flattened into a tactical board to explain a decisive play.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: sports analysis, team movement, timing, and spatial decisions

## Signature Features

- Full-speed play before analysis.
- Freeze, rewind, and alternate-angle proof.
- Tracked circles, arrows, and uncovered-space patches.
- Frame counts and a final tactical abstraction.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Sports Telestrator** style. Show the action once without interruption, then rewind and annotate only what the audience needs to notice. Track marks to moving players, switch angles to verify the claim, and finish on a simplified pitch diagram.

## Avoid

- Decorative arrows, analysis before the play is seen, inaccurate tracking, excessive studio packaging, or marks that hide the ball.

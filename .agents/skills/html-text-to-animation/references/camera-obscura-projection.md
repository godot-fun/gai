# Camera Obscura Projection

## Overview

An exterior scene enters a dark room through a tiny aperture and appears inverted across the interior surface.

## Metadata

- Collection: Experimental Camera and Image Processes
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: optics, observation, history, and inside–outside relationships

## Signature Features

- Darken a real room, admit exterior light through one small aperture, and project an inverted low-contrast view across interior surfaces.
- Open the aperture gradually, let eyes adapt, and track the sun or street outside while the projection remains optically inverted.
- For **Camera Obscura Projection**, use a palette, contrast curve, grain structure, and edge behavior native to the named capture or darkroom process.

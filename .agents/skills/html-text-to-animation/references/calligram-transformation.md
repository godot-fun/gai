# Calligram Transformation

## Overview

Words follow contours until the text itself becomes the subject it describes.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: poetry, identity, symbolic illustration, and compact narratives
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Shape supplied words into the contour, mass, or movement of their subject while retaining a traceable reading path.
- Assemble from lines of text, bend baselines gradually, then alternate between readable phrase and pictorial whole.
- For **Calligram Transformation**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Calligram Transformation** style. Shape supplied words into the contour, mass, or movement of their subject while retaining a traceable reading path. Assemble from lines of text, bend baselines gradually, then alternate between readable phrase and pictorial whole. Build hierarchy from glyph anatomy, spacing, reading direction, linguistic timing, and the specific encoding or word structure being demonstrated. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.
- Change typographic scale, neutral surface, accent color, and motion amplitude while preserving valid glyphs, reading order, timing, and decodability.

## Avoid

- Avoid decorative text masks, repeated filler words, unreadable tiny copy, arbitrary silhouettes, or replacing text with an image.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

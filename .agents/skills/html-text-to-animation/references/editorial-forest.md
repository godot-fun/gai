# Editorial Forest

## Overview

A literary magazine palette of forest green, blush pink, and cream with elegant serif type and fine rules. Choose this for a literary magazine mood built from forest green, blush, cream, fine rules, and elegant serif pacing.

## Metadata

- Collection: Ready-Made Layouts
- Medium: HTML, CSS, SVG, or Canvas
- Best for: nature essays, culture, sustainability, and reflective editorial narratives
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Forest green as the structural tone.
- Blush used as a restrained counterpoint.
- Literary serif headline.
- Fine rules and spacious magazine rhythm.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Editorial Forest** style. Use botanical silhouettes and editorial lines as framing rather than decoration. Let panels and type reveal with slow page-like wipes, preserving calm asymmetry and generous margins.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Choose **Cobalt Editorial** for professional coolness, or **Bold Editorial Poster** for louder cover energy.
- Literal scrapbook pages, dense foliage, rustic craft fonts, bright greens, or page-turn gimmicks.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

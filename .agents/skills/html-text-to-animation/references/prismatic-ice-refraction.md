# Prismatic Ice Refraction

## Overview

Faceted translucent ice bends cold light into restrained spectra while cracks and trapped bubbles establish scale.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: winter, preservation, fragility, and crystalline transformation

## Signature Features

- Construct one irregular ice block from milky depth, trapped bubbles, hairline cracks, wet edges, and sparse spectral refraction on a cold slate field.
- Rotate the block slowly under a hard side light; let a crack advance once and send a restrained prism across the background while the camera remains macro-close.
- Use thin uppercase typography in frost white, placed in negative space and never frosted into illegibility.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Prismatic Ice Refraction** style. Construct one irregular ice block from milky depth, trapped bubbles, hairline cracks, wet edges, and sparse spectral refraction on a cold slate field. Rotate the block slowly under a hard side light; let a crack advance once and send a restrained prism across the background while the camera remains macro-close. Use thin uppercase typography in frost white, placed in negative space and never frosted into illegibility. Make every transition arise from the optical or scientific process itself, and preserve enough negative space for the supplied message to remain primary.

## Avoid

- Avoid gemstone polish, saturated rainbow interiors, fire-like glow, fast shattering, or perfectly clear glass mislabeled as ice.

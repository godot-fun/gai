# Ferrofluid Sculpture

## Overview

Glossy black fluid rises into ordered spikes and traveling ridges under a changing magnetic field.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: invisible control, responsive matter, science, and dramatic form

## Signature Features

- Keep a glossy black pool centered over an invisible magnet array; shape spikes by field strength and rim them with a single cool highlight against pale gray or black.
- Raise peaks in rings as poles approach, reverse them when polarity changes, and allow viscous relaxation; alternate a fixed side profile with one overhead field view.
- Use minimal laboratory labels and numeric field markers in a precise monospaced face.

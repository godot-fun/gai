# Engineering Briefing Board

## Overview

A physical whiteboard wall accumulates photographs, measurements, marker logic, magnets, and stamps before the camera reveals the whole argument. Choose this for a physical operations wall combining photos, measurements, magnets, stamps, and engineering decisions.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: design reviews, option comparison, incident analysis, and engineering decisions
- Complexity: Advanced

## Signature Features

- Real enamel board with erased ghosts.
- Three marker colors with fixed meanings.
- Physical photos, tags, rulers, and magnets.
- Camera follows lines before the final pullback.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Engineering Briefing Board** style. Introduce evidence as weighted physical objects, draw connections in a stable color code, and travel from fact to consequence along the marker paths. Reserve the wide shot until the complete reasoning structure exists.

Translate the user's material into this grammar rather than copying the source examples. Preserve supplied wording and factual attribution, and choose an HTML-native or composited implementation appropriate to the evidence and visual material.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, evidence, copy, duration, aspect ratio, pacing, and implementation method.
- Adapt palette details only when the defining visual hierarchy remains intact.

## Avoid

- Choose **Live-Drawn Whiteboard** for pure marker drawing, or **Used Whiteboard Lecture** for an academic teaching surface.
- Digital sticky-note UI, random marker colors, decorative arrows, illegible handwriting, or an early overview that removes discovery.
- Never invent documents, measurements, quotations, sources, or factual evidence.
- Do not use surface effects as a substitute for the style's narrative structure.

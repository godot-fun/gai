# Welsh Hillside Gouache

## Overview

An opaque, wind-cut landscape assembled from directional brush marks, stepped buildings, and coal-dark ridges.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: An opaque, wind-cut landscape assembled from directional brush marks, stepped buildings, and coal-dark ridges.

## Signature Features

- Ragged opaque strokes aligned with terrain
- Pale-gold light, field green, slate blue, coal black, and a restrained focal rust
- Broken dark dashes around walls, roofs, and ridgelines
- High viewpoint with a road descending through repeated small structures
- Drifting cloud or smoke under lateral camera motion

## Style Prompt

Cover the surface with opaque gouache strokes whose direction describes slope, cloud, wall, and water. Make each mark visibly bristled, two to five times longer than it is wide, with no transparent wash underneath. Define buildings, stone walls, and ridges using interrupted coal-dark dashes; scrape a few lighter green or slate marks across dark hills. Keep fields and sky broadly luminous in pale gold and green while hills and buildings carry the weight in coal black and slate blue. Mix secondary hues from this narrow palette, reserving a subdued rust for one focal figure large enough to remain readable. View the scene from high ground in convincing perspective, with a road or river pulling the eye downward and cottages, walls, rocks, or posts stepping along the contour. Let low side light catch the ridge edges. Animate only drifting smoke, cloud, grass, and a measured lateral track.

## Avoid

watercolor transparency, hard vector shapes, mustard skies, muddy grading, and decorative brush marks that ignore the landform.

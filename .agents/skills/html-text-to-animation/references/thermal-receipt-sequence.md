# Thermal Receipt Sequence

## Overview

A compact narrative emerges from a real thermal printer as successive slips print, advance, receive marks, tear off, and accumulate on a colored desk.

## Signature Features

- Teal work surface and visible printer mechanics
- Monochrome heat marks with banding and slight fading
- Narrow typographic hierarchy, rules, barcodes, icons, and totals
- Physical paper advance, curl, stamp, tear, and stack
- Each new receipt functions as one chapter

## Style Prompt

Place a physical thermal printer on a teal or similarly restrained desk and keep paper width consistent in horizontal or vertical output. Treat each slip as one chapter: header, time or category, one small icon or diagram, concise line items, a key subtotal or metric, and a final action or result. Print through progressive darkening as the paper advances, including subtle thermal banding, imperfect heat density, edge curl, and occasional pale gaps. Use monospaced or receipt-like type, simple rules, authentic barcode or QR-like geometry only when it serves the story, and no color on the paper. After printing, apply a physical stamp, handwritten circle, fold, or tear where appropriate, then rip the slip across the cutter and let it land in a growing stack before the next scene. End on one final receipt whose total summarizes the outcome.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

instantly appearing text, endless fake itemization, decorative barcodes, laser-printer sharpness, floating paper, repeated identical layouts, hidden printer mechanics, and color graphics on a monochrome thermal medium.

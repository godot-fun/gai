# Ember-Lit Fantasy Watercolor

## Overview

A parchment-toned concept landscape where pencil-bound watercolor recedes through layered atmosphere toward one dominant source of light.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Granular transparent washes, blooms, and darker drying edges
- Fine dark-brown pencil on every silhouette and ridge
- Three to five atmospheric depth layers
- Half the image held in brown-violet shadow
- One ember-colored light source pushing through mist

## Style Prompt

Paint on warm parchment with transparent watercolor whose pigment granulates visibly, pools along drying edges, and produces a few deliberate backruns. Bind every mountain, tree, ruin, figure, and cloud ridge with a fine dark-brown pencil contour and add short hatch only on the shadow side. Build three to five depth layers that become progressively cooler, paler, and less detailed. Organize each scene around one powerful source—a low sun, flame, lantern, furnace, or molten opening—whose ember-orange atmosphere fills roughly a quarter of the image while about half remains in deep brown and violet shadow. Reserve intense red for the core of that source and keep the rest on parchment, umber, orange, and pale lavender. Let haze reveal the direction and scale of light rather than applying a generic glow. A brief hand-drawn map may bridge travel scenes, but the painted landscape remains primary. Pan slowly across large terrain and then advance toward the focal figure or destination.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

smooth digital gradients, vector flats, milky grey values, multiple competing light sources, full-frame orange, and pencil lines that vanish under the wash.

# Registered Color Woodblock

## Overview

A carved key block and a small set of color blocks produce a tactile print with gouged lines, wood grain, pressure variation, and deliberate registration character.

## Metadata

- Collection: Chinese Art Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Warm fibrous paper and carved black key line
- Separate flat color blocks with visible overlap
- White gouges, tapered cuts, and grain direction
- Slight registration drift and uneven ink transfer
- Block-by-block printing sequence

## Style Prompt

Design the image as a set of printable relief blocks. Carve a dark key block with tapered contours, expressive white gouges, and hatch that follows form. Assign broad local areas to a small number of separate color blocks and decide where they knock out, overlap, or leave paper exposed. Preserve wood grain direction, chipped cuts, compressed edge ink, small dry gaps, and modest registration drift; every irregularity should follow the printing process rather than a generic distress overlay. Simplify figures, landscape, architecture, or still life into strong positive and negative shapes while retaining specific gesture and identity. Animate by pressing one block at a time: paper contact, pressure, lift, and reveal, with the key block arriving at the stage that best clarifies the design. End on the complete print and hold for inspection.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

smooth gradients, dozens of colors, perfect alignment, vector-clean contours, etched copperplate detail, random noise, photographic shading, and wood texture floating independently of the carved marks.

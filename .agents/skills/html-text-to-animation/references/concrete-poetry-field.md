# Concrete Poetry Field

## Overview

Spacing, repetition, direction, and absence carry meaning as strongly as the words themselves.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: poetry, emotion, silence, and experimental language

## Signature Features

- Treat word placement, gap, repetition, rotation, and page boundary as syntax, using a sparse typographic field.
- Animate spatial relationships slowly—compression, dispersal, descent, interruption—while protecting the poem's intended sequence.
- For **Concrete Poetry Field**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Concrete Poetry Field** style. Treat word placement, gap, repetition, rotation, and page boundary as syntax, using a sparse typographic field. Animate spatial relationships slowly—compression, dispersal, descent, interruption—while protecting the poem's intended sequence. Build hierarchy from glyph anatomy, spacing, reading direction, linguistic timing, and the specific encoding or word structure being demonstrated. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Avoid

- Avoid kinetic-type presets, decorative shapes, random word clouds, continuous bouncing, or rewriting the supplied poem.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.

# Market-Segment Landscape

## Overview

A bounded market becomes a navigable landscape of customer needs, sizes, overlaps, and underserved territory.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: audience groups, market sizing, positioning, and underserved opportunities

## Signature Features

- Define the segmentation axes before placing groups, competitors, and opportunity regions.
- Encode segment size, accessibility, value, and overlap independently instead of using arbitrary bubble area.
- Preserve the total market boundary while zooming from broad categories into actionable subsegments.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Market-Segment Landscape** style. Map a declared market onto meaningful need or behavior axes, then form labeled territories whose area and density follow stated evidence. Place offers and competitors relative to those needs, reveal overlap and whitespace, and use semantic zoom to connect total market, serviceable market, and target segment without changing definitions midstream.

## Avoid

- Avoid invented precision, decorative personas, or bubbles whose size has no declared meaning.
- Do not confuse demographic labels with demonstrated needs or present every empty region as an opportunity.

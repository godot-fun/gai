# Elastic Ink Caricature

## Overview

An elegant black-line ensemble where long calligraphic contours exaggerate character without abandoning the setting.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: An elegant black-line ensemble where long calligraphic contours exaggerate character without abandoning the setting.

## Signature Features

- Single sweeping contours that taper and swell through curves
- Solid black hair, garments, and large props
- Sparse parallel hatch only where a middle tone is essential
- One proportion or posture exaggeration per figure
- Dense environmental linework separated by generous paper white

## Style Prompt

Draw each major contour in the longest plausible continuous stroke, tapering delicately at entry and exit and swelling through bends. Use solid black for dark hair, suits, curtains, and selected furniture so the composition has weight; introduce middle tone only through sparse parallel hatch. Caricature each person by choosing one dominant truth—a long neck, angular shoulder, compact torso, expansive gesture, or oversized head—while keeping hands, expression, and stance specific. Describe architecture, furniture, props, and background activity with fine economical lines so the scene feels complete, yet keep white channels between objects and allow roughly three fifths of the paper to remain exposed. Replace a lead character's principal black garment or body mass with one flat muted accent color. Write the drawing on in confident strokes during the opening beat, then stop altering it. A short hand-lettered remark may occupy a naturally quiet patch. Pull back to reveal relationships, pan across the ensemble, and push toward the decisive expression.

## Avoid

stick figures, rigid limbs, generic faces, empty rooms, and nervous scratchy lines.

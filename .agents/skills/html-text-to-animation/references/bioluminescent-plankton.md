# Bioluminescent Plankton

## Overview

Tiny organisms emit blue-green light only when water motion disturbs them, turning interaction into glow.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: night oceans, wonder, ecological systems, and touch-responsive narratives
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Use a nearly black teal water volume populated by invisible organisms that appear only as tiny cyan-blue flashes around disturbances.
- Trace a hand, wave, or vessel wake so light blooms locally, trails briefly, and decays; follow at water level before rising to show the wake pattern.
- Use small warm-white captions above the waterline with long reading holds.

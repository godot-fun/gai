# Chladni Sand Resonance

## Overview

Fine sand migrates across a vibrating plate and settles along nodal lines that reveal each resonant mode.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: sound made visible, resonance explanations, and geometric frequency transitions

## Signature Features

- Loose grains begin across a physical plate rather than on a blank graphic field.
- Frequency changes reorganize sand into mode-specific nodal geometry.
- Grains remain active in vibrating regions and collect where displacement approaches zero.

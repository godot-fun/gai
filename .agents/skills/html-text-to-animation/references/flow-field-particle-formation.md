# Flow-Field Particle Formation

## Overview

Thousands of luminous points travel through a coherent vector field, condense into legible forms, release, and reorganize with conservation of motion. Choose this when a broad vector field carries particles into structures through visible currents.

## Signature Features

- Dense but individually resolved luminous particles
- Continuous flow-field drift
- Attraction into words, logos, symbols, or numbers
- Scatter with inherited velocity and delayed regrouping
- Depth, color, and brightness tied to role

## Style Prompt

Define a smooth flow field with large coherent curls and let particles inherit velocity from it before any formation begins. Assign a subset to each target shape and attract them gradually along curved trajectories, preserving momentum so they spiral and settle rather than teleport. Form one large word, number, symbol, object outline, or logo at a time and hold until fully readable. Release it through a directional force, let particles scatter with their current velocity, then redirect them into the next target. Use brightness and point size to separate foreground, core form, and background drift, and restrict color to a small gradient family plus one highlight. Avoid filling every pixel; leave dark space around the formation. Add a few near-camera streaks only during high speed.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

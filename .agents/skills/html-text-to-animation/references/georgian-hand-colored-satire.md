# Georgian Hand-Colored Satire

## Overview

A crowded copperplate comedy with disciplined hatch, overflowing transparent color, and mischievous social detail.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A crowded copperplate comedy with disciplined hatch, overflowing transparent color, and mischievous social detail.

## Signature Features

- Swelling brown-black burin contours heavier away from light
- Form-following hatch everywhere and cross-hatch only in darks
- Transparent wine, brass, flesh, slate, ochre, and brown washes
- Watercolor intentionally bleeding beyond engraved boundaries
- Caricatured ensemble, comic props or animals, and one handwritten speech balloon

## Style Prompt

Engrave the scene before coloring it. Use warm brown-black burin contours that swell through pressure and become heavier on the shadow side. Cover every surface with hatching that follows volume, adding cross-hatch only in the deepest passages and ruler-straight lines on flat architecture or furniture. Print on lightly ribbed tan paper with foxing, a plate impression, and an engraved border. Hand-color nearly the whole image in transparent but rich wine red, brass yellow, flesh pink, slate blue, warm ochre, and brown; let washes overflow the ink by a few pixels and pool unevenly rather than behaving like digital masks. Arrange a broad-nosed, strongly gesturing cast around the main incident, adding spectators, animals, dropped objects, food, furniture, or other secondary jokes that reward inspection. Include one concise handwritten italic speech balloon in the user's language. Hold on the full plate, rush toward one joke, slide laterally to another, then finish on an expressive close detail.

## Avoid

mechanically uniform hatch, empty paper, perfectly registered color, muddy washes, and modern comic-panel framing.

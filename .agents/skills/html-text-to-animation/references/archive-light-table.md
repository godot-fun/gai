# Archive Light Table

## Overview

Real archival objects rest on a cool backlit table while a loupe activates details hidden inside glass, paper, and emulsion.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: history, provenance, research, and document-led storytelling

## Signature Features

- Physical negatives, slides, letters, and clippings.
- Cool even backlight without a vintage filter.
- Loupe-led magnification.
- Catalogue numbers and dates remain attached to objects.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Archive Light Table** style. Arrange a small evidence set, bring the loupe down on one item, and let a negative turn positive, a line illuminate, or a photograph open into depth. Preserve quiet modern handling and exact archival metadata.

## Avoid

- Sepia nostalgia, floating documents, fake catalogue codes, scrapbook clutter, or magical effects that obscure the object.

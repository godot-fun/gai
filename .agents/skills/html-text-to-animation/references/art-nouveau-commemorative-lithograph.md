# Art Nouveau Commemorative Lithograph

## Overview

A ceremonial color lithograph combining a central halo, mosaic panels, whiplash ornament, and restrained pastel motion.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Tapered brown contours heavier on the shadow side
- Cream, sage, pale gold, rose, and one stronger focal accent
- Ringed halo and arched mosaic panels
- Flowing vines, flowers, hair, and fabric in three-tone strands
- Inscription band integrated into the composition

## Style Prompt

Frame the subject as a wide ceremonial lithograph. Draw with warm brown tapered contours, doubling their weight on the side away from an upper-left light. Fill shapes with muted cream, sage, pale gold, and dusty rose, adding only one hard-edged darker tone for shadow. Center the principal figure before a large ringed halo that can become a clock, sun, diagram, or emblem tied to the story. Flank it with arched mosaic fields in muted blue-green tiles, then weave local flowers and whiplash vines through every remaining gap. Model hair, ribbons, and drapery as grouped three-tone strands rather than gradients. Place a short serif inscription in a lower band and keep it subordinate to the image. Add lithographic grain, paper mottling, slight edge darkening, and slow swaying or petal motion. Tilt toward the emblem, then pull back to reveal the complete poster.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

thin sterile lines, saturated modern colors, empty backgrounds, and detached typography.

# Ultrasound Echo Imaging

## Overview

A sweeping acoustic beam builds a grainy depth image from returning echoes and moving interfaces.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: prenatal care, diagnostics, sonar analogy, and live measurement

## Signature Features

- Use a dark sector or linear scan field with depth ticks, speckle, bright interfaces, acoustic shadows, and limited grayscale dynamic range.
- Sweep the beam line-by-line, update moving anatomy at scan cadence, and let probe angle alter echoes coherently; keep the display camera fixed.
- Use compact instrument typography with clear depth and orientation markers.

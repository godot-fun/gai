# Competitive Strategy Board

## Overview

Competitors make sequential moves across capabilities, customers, channels, and constraints on a shared strategic board.

## Metadata

- Collection: Economics, Markets and Business Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: competitors, differentiation, strategic moves, and market response

## Signature Features

- Give every player explicit resources, advantages, constraints, and objectives before moves begin.
- Show action, likely response, and second-order consequence as a traceable sequence rather than isolated rankings.
- Maintain a stable strategic terrain so changes in position remain comparable over time.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Competitive Strategy Board** style. Arrange a restrained strategic field of customer territories, capabilities, channels, and constraints. Move distinct competitor pieces only when an explicit decision occurs, then animate responses and consequences through ownership, access, cost, or differentiation. Keep uncertainty visible and distinguish observed facts from modeled reactions.

## Avoid

- Avoid battle imagery that turns ordinary competition into conquest or treats rivals as static stereotypes.
- Do not reduce strategy to a feature checklist, quadrant snapshot, or unexplained score.

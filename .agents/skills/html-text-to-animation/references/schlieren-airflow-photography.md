# Schlieren Airflow Photography

## Overview

Invisible density gradients bend light into flowing plumes around heat, breath, pressure, and aerodynamic forms.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: airflow, heat, engineering, and invisible processes

## Signature Features

- Place a sharp high-contrast subject against a neutral field; render refractive density gradients as bright and dark ribbons around heat, breath, or pressure.
- Start with still air, introduce one source, and let plumes convect, curl, and dissipate; use a locked side view that preserves flow direction.
- Use minimal black labels and arrows outside the plume, with temperature or direction only when supplied.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Schlieren Airflow Photography** style. Place a sharp high-contrast subject against a neutral field; render refractive density gradients as bright and dark ribbons around heat, breath, or pressure. Start with still air, introduce one source, and let plumes convect, curl, and dissipate; use a locked side view that preserves flow direction. Use minimal black labels and arrows outside the plume, with temperature or direction only when supplied. Make every transition arise from the optical or scientific process itself, and preserve enough negative space for the supplied message to remain primary.

## Avoid

- Avoid colored smoke, particle trails, turbulent motion without a source, soft-focus footage, or airflow passing through solid objects.

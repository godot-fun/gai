# Practical Neon Wet Night

## Overview

A photographic night exterior where constructed glass tubing, warm facial light, wet vertical reflections, snow, and changing angles remain grounded in one believable location. Choose this for documentary-feeling exterior night photography where practical signs reflect across wet surfaces.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Visible glass tubes, mounts, dark jump wires, bright cores, and mild breathing
- Pink, cyan, orange, green, sodium, and deep violet-blue night
- Wet lower quarter carrying broken vertical reflections
- Warm key on faces with narrow dual-color rim lights
- Two-to-three-second views of the same location, each slowly advancing

## Style Prompt

Place neon only where it belongs within the subject's world: storefront, workshop, pier bar, terminal, apartment, sign tower, or another justified structure. Construct every sign from visible glass tubing with clamps, dark jump wires, colored gas glow, a white-hot core, gentle brightness breathing, and at most one rare electrical twitch every several seconds. Use pink, cyan, orange, and green alongside warm sodium light over a deep violet-blue night. Reserve the lower quarter for genuinely wet pavement, boards, tile, or shallow water, stretching signs into long vertical reflections broken by seams and ripples. If snow is present, settle it in low piles and tint only its edges with spill. Keep the lead's face under a nearby warm practical key, with one narrow neon rim on each side; soften distance into bokeh. Apply restrained bloom, grain, and vignette. Cut among two-to-three-second angles within the same mapped location, giving each a slow push and consistent geography.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

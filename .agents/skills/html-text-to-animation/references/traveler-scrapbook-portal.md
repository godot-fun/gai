# Traveler Scrapbook Portal

## Overview

A densely layered travel notebook whose central watercolor becomes a warm-lit world as the camera crosses its taped edge.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Six to ten overlapping maps, photos, pages, notes, flowers, and taped artworks
- Soft object shadows and yellowed translucent tape
- Wet watercolor with warm ochre light and hard purple-rose shadow
- Dark foreground surrounding one warm source and a bright exterior
- Push through the painting until all page edges leave frame

## Style Prompt

Cover the frame edge to edge with a crowded travel scrapbook of six to ten overlapping artifacts: a dominant taped watercolor, hand-drawn map, dark-brown photograph, printed clipping, handwritten note, pressed flower, ticket, or material sample. Let pieces extend beyond the crop, cast soft shadows, and attach with aged translucent tape. Keep the page family in amber paper, deep brown, ochre, purple-rose, and dark ink. Paint the main image wet-in-wet but retain decisive value structure: warm ochre on lit planes, harder purple-rose shadow planes, visible pen contour, granulation, and pooled edges. Within that image, place one warm practical light in a dark foreground while the exterior or distance stays bright. Apply amber aging only to the paper objects; preserve clean color inside the painting. Use a restrained vignette and light paper texture. Advance directly into the watercolor until scrapbook edges slide out on both sides and the viewer stands inside the scene; once the page is still framing the image, do not pan sideways.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

sparse grid layouts, neat margins, smooth digital gradients, muddy global sepia, generic collage stickers, and cuts before the portal transition completes.

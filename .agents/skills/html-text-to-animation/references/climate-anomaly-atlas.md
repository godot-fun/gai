# Climate Anomaly Atlas

## Overview

A purpose-built visual system in which regional departures evolve against a fixed baseline and honest diverging scale.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartography and Geographic Storytelling
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: regional departures evolve against a fixed baseline and honest diverging scale

## Signature Features

- Make the process whereby regional departures evolve against a fixed baseline and honest diverging scale the dominant visual mechanism.
- Establish the specific causes, constraints, and scale governing **Climate Anomaly Atlas** before revealing their consequences.
- Let labels, texture, and camera movement support the climate anomaly atlas mechanism instead of competing with it.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Climate Anomaly Atlas** style. Build the composition around a clear demonstration of how regional departures evolve against a fixed baseline and honest diverging scale. Preserve the governing spatial, temporal, material, or rhythmic relationship; add atmosphere and detail only after that relationship reads clearly.

## Avoid

- Do not replace **Climate Anomaly Atlas** with generic particles, glowing lines, a dashboard, or a slideshow.
- Reject motion that makes **Climate Anomaly Atlas** physically or conceptually inconsistent, even when the surface effect looks attractive.

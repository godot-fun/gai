# Late-Night Shopping Pitch

## Overview

A caffeinated flat-vector infomercial uses sunburst cards, comic headlines, persistent claims ticker, a transforming customer mascot, prices, comparisons, and countdown urgency.

## Metadata

- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation

## Signature Features

- Full-bleed single-hue sunburst per beat
- Chunky comic-shout headline
- Persistent lower ticker of concise claims
- Grey problem-state mascot becoming colorful
- Price, comparison, phone, countdown, checkmark, and rocket-like payoff

## Style Prompt

Treat the sequence as a knowingly exaggerated but internally consistent shopping pitch. Use a new full-bleed single-hue sunburst card for each beat while preserving one brand palette. Slam a short chunky headline into every card and keep a lower ticker scrolling distinct, supportable benefit bullets. Introduce a simple original customer mascot in a grey frustrated state, demonstrate the problem, then reveal the offer and transition the mascot into an energetic colorful result. Show one honest comparison panel, a prominent price or commitment, availability condition, phone or action channel, and a countdown only if time pressure is real. Use cartoon props such as alarm, ringing phone, checkmark, package, or rocket as visual punctuation. Keep camera locked and make hard graphic card changes with punchy sound-like timing.

## Avoid

deceptive discounts, fake scarcity, hidden conditions, real network branding, nonstop visual noise, unreadable ticker speed, unsupported claims, too many mascots, and a pitch that never demonstrates the product.

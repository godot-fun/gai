# Snow and Embers

## Overview

Cold snow falls while a few embers rise, holding a fragile warmth against loss.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: farewell, endings, resilience, and warmth surviving in cold conditions

## Signature Features

- Snow and embers obey opposing directions and speeds.
- The frame remains predominantly cold.
- A small warm region carries the emotional center.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Snow and Embers** style. Create broad blue-grey space with layered falling snow and a sparse family of orange embers rising from one source. Increase warmth only at the narrative turn and protect a clear area for text.

## Avoid

- Holiday cheer, equal warm and cold coverage, fireworks, uniform particle motion, or blizzard density that hides the message.

# Jacob's Ladder Arc Climb

## Overview

An electrical arc ignites between diverging electrodes, rises as heated air carries it upward, stretches, breaks, and restarts below.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: electrical causality, repeating energy cycles, and dramatic laboratory reveals

## Signature Features

- Two electrodes widen toward the top.
- The arc begins at the smallest gap and climbs through buoyant heated air.
- Stretch, instability, extinction, and re-ignition form one causal loop.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Jacob's Ladder Arc Climb** style. Establish the electrode geometry in a dark laboratory, ignite a tight arc at the base, and let it rise unevenly while branching and lengthening with the widening gap. Dim the environment with each discharge, break the arc near the top, and restart only after a short electrical pause.

## Avoid

- Avoid a static lightning bolt, downward travel, ignition at the widest gap, continuous unbroken looping, or decorative sparks that ignore the electrode geometry.

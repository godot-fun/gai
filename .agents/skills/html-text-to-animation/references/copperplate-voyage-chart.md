# Copperplate Voyage Chart

## Overview

A journey unfolds entirely across an engraved chart, where route, ornament, and narrative vignette share one sheet.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation

## Signature Features

- Fine burin hatching on warm laid paper
- Rhumb-line network, compass rose, graduated border, cartouche, and one ornament
- Thin coastal hand color with one stronger focal hue
- An unboxed action vignette dissolving into the map
- A dotted route inks itself while the camera glides inward

## Style Prompt

Render every value with fine engraved hatching that follows form; reserve cross-hatching for the darkest passages and impose a minimum visible line weight so detail darkens rather than disappears. Print warm black on laid cream paper with folds, foxing, and restrained age. Organize the full sheet with one or more compass roses, radiating rhumb lines, a graduated frame, hachured relief, serif labels, a cartouche, and a single period ornament. Wash only the coast or water edge with weak blue-green and land with muted olive; reserve one stronger color for the narrative focus. Place the action inside an unbordered engraved vignette whose hatching fades naturally into the chart. In one continuous move, begin on the whole sheet, animate a dotted course drawing itself, and glide toward the vignette while enough map remains visible to preserve the conceit.

## Avoid

leaving the paper world, heavy watercolor, rigid posed figures, and modern map icons.

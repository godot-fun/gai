# Practical Neon Wall Sign

## Overview

A real glass-tube sign on dark brick powers on circuit by circuit, spills colored light into mortar, buzzes imperfectly, and reflects across a wet floor. Choose this when one physical neon sign, its transformer behavior, wall spill, and letter construction are the focal subject.

## Signature Features

- Visible glass tubes, clips, transformers, and jump wires
- Dark brick with mortar and local colored spill
- Warm-up flicker resolving to stable light
- Wet-floor reflection broken by seams and puddles
- Close detail, frontal reveal, and restrained electrical sound

## Style Prompt

Design a short original sign whose tubing can be physically bent and supported. Show transparent or pale glass when off, mounting clips, dark jump wires between letters, transformer boxes, and a plausible circuit order. Place it on detailed dark brick so bright inner tube cores and broader colored spill illuminate brick faces and disappear into mortar recesses. Power sections in sequence: weak sputter, partial ionization, one or two irregular flickers, then stable glow with only gentle breathing. Let the wet floor beneath mirror the sign in elongated color broken by grout, cracks, puddles, and perspective. Begin close on unlit hardware, pull or cut to the full sign as the final circuit catches, and hold long enough for the environment to respond.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

- Choose **Cyber Neon** for nonphysical graphic light, or **Practical Neon Wet Night** for a whole exterior environment.
flat luminous text, invisible supports, constant strobing, perfect mirror reflections, rainbow colors in every letter, excessive bloom, dry surroundings, and neon treated as a post-production outline.

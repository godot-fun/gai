# Persistence-of-Vision Light Wand

## Overview

A moving column of timed LEDs paints words and images into dark space through precisely synchronized pulses.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: luminous spatial lettering, scan-based reveals, and persistence-of-vision demonstrations

## Signature Features

- One narrow physical emitter sweeps across the frame.
- Pulse timing maps image columns to measured wand positions.
- The completed image exists perceptually while the camera still reveals the moving source.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Persistence-of-Vision Light Wand** style. Start in darkness with the LED column visible, calibrate one sweep, then pulse image columns at positions tied to its velocity. Let letters or symbols form crisply across the travel path while faint exposure trails preserve the physical gesture. Show timing errors briefly before achieving stable registration.

## Avoid

- Avoid a stationary neon sign, continuous light trails with no pulse structure, an image that remains after the wand stops, or scan timing unrelated to emitter position.

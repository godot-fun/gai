# Sunlight Cyanotype Exposure

## Overview

Objects block ultraviolet light while exposed paper develops from pale green to deep Prussian blue. Choose this for camera-less sun exposure and washing of real objects on sensitized paper.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: botany, archives, time, and photographic craft

## Signature Features

- Arrange flat botanical or archival objects on sensitized paper; begin yellow-green, pass through bronze, and finish in deep Prussian blue with white silhouettes.
- Expose under a moving sun shadow, remove objects after a timed hold, then wash the sheet so blue deepens organically; use a fixed overhead worktable camera.
- Use restrained white or blueprint-blue serif captions outside object silhouettes, with date and exposure notes if relevant.

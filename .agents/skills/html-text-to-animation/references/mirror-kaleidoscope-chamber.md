# Mirror Kaleidoscope Chamber

## Overview

A finite object multiplies through angled mirrors into a symmetrical chamber that changes with small rotations.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: multiplicity, identity, spectacle, and geometric transformation

## Signature Features

- Use three or more precisely angled mirrors around one limited subject set; maintain exact reflection continuity and a strong central symmetry axis.
- Rotate the subject or mirror assembly slowly, causing motifs to tessellate and reconfigure; move the camera only along the chamber axis.
- Use a centered geometric display face that participates sparingly in reflection without multiplying into noise.

## Avoid

- Avoid arbitrary radial duplication, broken reflection angles, fisheye distortion, too many source objects, or constant fast rotation.

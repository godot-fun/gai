# Brutalist Concrete Monument

## Overview

Massive board-formed concrete planes, deep voids, and severe light turn information into civic scale.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: institutions, power, memory, and monumental statements

## Signature Features

- Use massive asymmetrical concrete planes, board-form texture, deep recesses, hard daylight, and tiny human scale.
- Reveal weight through slow lateral movement and long shadow changes; transitions should feel like passing thresholds or sliding walls.
- For **Brutalist Concrete Monument**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Brutalist Concrete Monument** style. Use massive asymmetrical concrete planes, board-form texture, deep recesses, hard daylight, and tiny human scale. Reveal weight through slow lateral movement and long shadow changes; transitions should feel like passing thresholds or sliding walls. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid smooth plastic gray, decorative grunge, weightless floating slabs, excessive glass, or playful bounce easing.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

# Layered Red Paper Cut

## Overview

A deterministic symmetrical window decoration unfolds from layered red paper, radial cutouts, visible bridges, paper fibers, and right-down cast shadows. Choose this for cinematic depth made from multiple red cut-paper planes rather than a single window-cut emblem.

## Metadata

- Collection: Chinese Art Styles

## Signature Features

- Cream window paper, bright red, and deep red only
- Radially repeated holes, drops, petals, diamonds, and center cut character
- Red bridges preserve the physical integrity of every cut layer
- Corner quarter-rosettes and layered shadows
- Outer-to-inner cut reveal followed by stamp-like center and gentle sway

## Style Prompt

Build a physical circular paper cut from exactly three color roles: cream window paper, deep red backing, and bright red top sheet. Show subtle fibers in the cream ground. Expand the deep-red outer ring first, then the bright-red disc. Cut openings as true negative space, never by painting cream over red. Organize the pattern through exact radial repetition: small outer holes, inward-pointing drops, large petals, offset diamonds and dots, an inner petal ring, and a central medallion whose short character or symbol is itself cut out. Preserve red bridges between every opening so the sheet remains physically connected. Add mirrored quarter-rosettes in the corners. Cast soft consistent shadows down and right from each paper layer. Reveal cuts from outside inward in staggered rings, settle the center with a brief stamp-like overshoot, then sway the complete paper by less than one degree before a still ending.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

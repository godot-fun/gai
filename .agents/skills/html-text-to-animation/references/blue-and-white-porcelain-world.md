# Blue-and-White Porcelain World

## Overview

A cobalt narrative grows across a warm-white glazed ceramic surface through brush variation, reserve shapes, border bands, and firing imperfections.

## Metadata

- Collection: Chinese Art Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A cobalt narrative grows across a warm-white glazed ceramic surface through brush variation, reserve shapes, border bands, and firing imperfections.

## Signature Features

- Warm white glaze and cobalt only
- Brush-loaded blue from pale wash to dense dark
- Floral scrolls, waves, clouds, rocks, and framed scenes
- Foot ring, rim bands, glaze pinholes, pooling, and kiln variation
- Camera follows painting around curved ceramic space

## Style Prompt

Treat the frame as a real porcelain vessel, tile, plate, or ceramic architectural surface. Use warm glazed white as negative space and cobalt as the only pigment, varying brush load from pale watery blue to dense dark strokes. Build the central narrative from people, animals, architecture, landscape, or objects enclosed by appropriate floral scrolls, wave bands, cloud motifs, fret borders, or medallions. Let cobalt pool at stroke endings and carved recesses, feather slightly under glaze, and vary through firing; add subtle pinholes, glaze thickness, fine crazing only where appropriate, and reflected light that reveals curvature. Preserve broad white reserves so ornament does not suffocate the scene. Animate the brush painting itself, then move the camera around the ceramic curve as water, clouds, or figures within the motif awaken gently.

## Avoid

adding red or gold, flat digital blue, perfect repeated motifs, plastic gloss, generic floral wallpaper, excessive crackle, perspective that ignores vessel curvature, and full-color imagery pasted onto porcelain.

# Marginalia Conversation

## Overview

A central text accumulates replies, arrows, corrections, sketches, and voices around its edges.

## Metadata

- Collection: Typography and Language Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: debate, scholarship, personal reading, and layered interpretation
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Keep a stable primary page surrounded by distinct annotator voices using consistent ink, handwriting, arrows, and dates.
- Add notes in chronological turns, connect each to exact source passages, and allow replies without covering the original text.
- For **Marginalia Conversation**, build hierarchy from the writing system itself: baseline, spacing, glyph anatomy, reading direction, and linguistic timing take precedence over illustration.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Marginalia Conversation** style. Keep a stable primary page surrounded by distinct annotator voices using consistent ink, handwriting, arrows, and dates. Add notes in chronological turns, connect each to exact source passages, and allow replies without covering the original text. Build hierarchy from glyph anatomy, spacing, reading direction, linguistic timing, and the specific encoding or word structure being demonstrated. Limit color to paper or screen neutrals plus one semantic accent; keep every required character readable at the intended viewing distance. Let reading order, linguistic structure, or encoding rules determine transitions instead of applying generic kinetic-type presets.

Adapt duration, aspect ratio, hierarchy, and annotation without changing supplied wording. Verify every character, pronunciation, encoding, and reading convention used by the effect.

## Customizable Elements

- Adjust type family, scale, spacing, line length, pacing, annotation depth, and the balance between primary reading and structural explanation.
- Change typographic scale, neutral surface, accent color, and motion amplitude while preserving valid glyphs, reading order, timing, and decodability.

## Avoid

- Avoid random doodles, identical handwriting for every voice, arrows to nowhere, instant clutter, or rewriting the source passage.
- Do not invent characters, pronunciations, encodings, reading conventions, or linguistic relationships to complete the composition.
- Keep essential text readable and provide a stable reduced-motion state that preserves the final composition.

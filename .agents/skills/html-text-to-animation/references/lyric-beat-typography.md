# Lyric Beat Typography

## Overview

Lyrics appear syllable by syllable in sync with voice and rhythm, turning phrasing into motion. Choose this for lyrics paced by musical meter, rhyme, and recurring chorus structure.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Styles
- Medium: HTML animation, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: songs, choruses, spoken rhythm, and musical interludes
- Complexity: Moderate to advanced

## Signature Features

- Timing follows vocal delivery.
- Scale and motion follow stress.
- Phrases retain reading continuity.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **Lyric Beat Typography** style. Map syllables and held notes before designing motion, group words by breath, and reserve the largest visual change for the musical hook.

Adapt the subject, copy, duration, aspect ratio, and implementation method while preserving the defining narrative and motion logic. Keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, copy, duration, aspect ratio, palette, pacing, and implementation method.

## Avoid

- Choose **Word-Synced Narration** for nonmusical speech, or **Impact Typography** for isolated emphatic words.
- Do not copy sample brands, characters, or wording from the source material.
- Do not add motion that has no explanatory, rhythmic, or emotional purpose.
- Preserve readability and one dominant idea per beat.

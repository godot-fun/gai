# Single-Source Amber Chiaroscuro

## Overview

A dark oil-painted chamber where one overhead source sculpts faces, hands, and authority from warm shadow.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Roughly seventy percent legible warm darkness
- One overhead pool of light with eyes beneath the brow
- Thin glazed shadows and thick ridged highlights
- A single concentrated deep-red accent
- Slow pushes and withdrawals within a heavy vignette

## Style Prompt

Illuminate the scene from one dominant source above, allowing no second key. Keep most of the image in warm, readable umber darkness rather than featureless black; reserve near-black for corners and the deepest overlaps. Build a smaller amber middle range and let upward-facing planes—forehead, cheek, hand, table, shoulder—reach warm cream. Position the lead fully inside the pool so a substantial portion of the body is visibly modeled, while the brow naturally buries the eyes. Permit one compact saturated red object or garment and no competing bright hues. Paint shadows in thin smooth glazes, transitions softly, and highlights in thicker directional strokes whose ridges catch light. A window, door slit, or distant horizon may form one warm background opening, while minor lamps remain tiny local embers subordinate to the overhead source. Add amber varnish, fine canvas tooth, and a dense vignette. Move only with slow, deliberate pushes or withdrawals that expose relationships in the dark.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

lifted milky shadows, flat fill light, floating faces, black clipping that erases form, scratchy hatch, and multiple theatrical spotlights.

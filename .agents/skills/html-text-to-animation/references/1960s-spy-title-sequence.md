# 1960s Spy Title Sequence

## Overview

A four-ink cut-paper title sequence uses silhouettes, brass-hit cuts, and credits as physical scenery.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: fictional openings, intrigue, capers, and stylish character introductions

## Signature Features

- Cut-paper silhouette construction.
- Credits become doors, stairs, traps, or architecture.
- Four-ink print palette.
- Every major cut answers a brass accent.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished visual narrative in the **1960s Spy Title Sequence** style. Build a miniature chase or infiltration from the typography itself, move silhouettes through letterforms, and assemble the final title from pieces introduced earlier.

## Avoid

- Direct copying of a known film title, photoreal espionage, generic gun silhouettes, smooth 3D camera moves, or credits treated as an overlay.

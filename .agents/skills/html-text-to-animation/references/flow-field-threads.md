# Flow-Field Threads

## Overview

Hundreds of fine strands reveal an invisible current, like thoughts settling into a shared direction.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Motion Studies
- Medium: HTML, Canvas, SVG, or WebGL
- Best for: mental flow, emotional release, calm transitions, and invisible systems
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- Fine lines move as a coherent field rather than independent decoration.
- Persistent trails reveal the direction of the underlying flow.
- Density and curvature change gradually across the frame.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Flow-Field Threads** style. Use a dark quiet ground and a narrow family of cool line colors. Let strands follow smooth directional currents, fade slowly into trails, and open a calmer region around key text.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining behavior above and keep all supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject matter, on-screen wording, duration, aspect ratio, typeface, and accent color.
- Rendering may use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL according to the needs of the effect.

## Avoid

- Screensaver noise, tangled uniform density, heavy glow, abrupt direction changes, or unrelated particles.
- Do not reproduce historical sample copy or hard-coded layouts from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice readability for motion density.

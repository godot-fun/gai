# Capsule Editorial

## Overview

Elegant Bodoni-like typography is balanced by playful capsule forms on cream paper. Choose this when high-fashion serif typography is softened by playful capsule forms and cream-paper elegance.

## Metadata

- Collection: Ready-Made Layouts
- Medium: HTML, CSS, SVG, or Canvas
- Best for: beauty, lifestyle, human-centered products, and sophisticated playful stories
- Complexity: Moderate

## Signature Features

- High-contrast serif headline.
- Rounded capsule shapes as framing devices.
- Cream paper and a small candy palette.
- Graceful tension between formal type and soft geometry.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Capsule Editorial** style. Use capsules to mask, reveal, or carry short phrases while the serif headline remains calm and precise. Let shapes glide and rotate gently, then align into a refined editorial composition.

Adapt the subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and rendering technique to the user's material. Preserve the defining visual grammar and keep supplied text unchanged unless rewriting is requested.

## Customizable Elements

- Subject, wording, duration, aspect ratio, palette emphasis, and scene content.
- Use HTML, CSS, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets as appropriate.

## Avoid

- Choose **Cartesian Editorial** for analytical restraint, or **Creative Mode** for saturated geometric play.
- Pill-shaped app buttons, medical UI clichés, bubbly overshoot, many gradients, or cartoon outlines.
- Do not copy example brands, characters, or sample copy from the source material.
- Do not sacrifice hierarchy or readability for surface decoration.

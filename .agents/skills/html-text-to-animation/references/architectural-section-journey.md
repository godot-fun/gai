# Architectural Section Journey

## Overview

The camera travels through a clean building section while rooms, circulation, and services animate in context.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: architecture, institutions, processes, and spatial explanation

## Signature Features

- Present a clean inhabited section with structural layers, circulation, rooms, and services aligned to real elevations.
- Track horizontally or vertically through connected spaces, activate one system at a time, and use cutaway transitions at boundaries.
- For **Architectural Section Journey**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Architectural Section Journey** style. Present a clean inhabited section with structural layers, circulation, rooms, and services aligned to real elevations. Track horizontally or vertically through connected spaces, activate one system at a time, and use cutaway transitions at boundaries. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid perspective interiors mislabeled as sections, floating rooms, hidden circulation, fast drone flight, or labels detached from spaces.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

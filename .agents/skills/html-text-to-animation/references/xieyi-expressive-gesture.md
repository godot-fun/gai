# Xieyi Expressive Gesture

## Overview

A few decisive brush events capture the spirit of a subject through pressure, speed, dry breaks, splashes, and suggestive incompleteness. Choose this when the animation centers on spontaneous calligraphic force, abbreviated gesture, and expressive emptiness rather than a complete ink landscape.

## Signature Features

- Large loaded strokes with visible entry, turn, and release
- Dry flying-white texture beside wet ink pools
- Forms implied by selected marks rather than fully described
- Strong asymmetry and active negative space
- Animation follows the causality of each brush gesture

## Style Prompt

Reduce the subject to the minimum strokes that preserve its energy, posture, direction, and emotional weight. Use a large brush loaded unevenly with water and ink so one gesture can move from wet dark core to pale edge and dry flying-white break. Let pressure widen a body or rock, a twist describe motion, and a fast release create branch, tail, sleeve, grass, or spray. Add a few concentrated dots or hooked accents for eyes, joints, leaves, or focal detail. Keep the composition asymmetric and allow forms to remain open where the viewer can complete them. Animate in the actual order of painting: charged stroke lands, moisture spreads, secondary marks answer it, then the image settles while a small natural motion continues. A seal may close the piece.

## Avoid

- Choose **Xieyi Ink Wash** when atmosphere, landscape depth, and tonal wash matter more than a single forceful gesture.

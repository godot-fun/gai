# Narrative Transit Map

## Overview

A journey becomes a functional network map: colored routes draw across cream stock, stations activate, transfers clarify relationships, and a moving train reaches a final terminus sign. Choose this when a transit diagram carries a chronological story and recurring narrative agents.

## Metadata

- Collection: Code Video Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Cream map stock and limited route colors
- Geometrically disciplined horizontal, vertical, and diagonal lines
- Station nodes, transfer rings, labels, and route key
- Train marker traveling continuously along the active path
- Ending terminus sign as the resolved statement

## Style Prompt

Translate the user's process into a small transit network with meaningful route names, stops, transfer points, and a clear destination. Draw colored lines across cream paper in disciplined horizontal, vertical, and forty-five-degree segments, rounding corners consistently and maintaining spacing between parallel routes. Add station circles, transfer rings, concise labels, a route legend, and only the geographic hints needed for orientation. Reveal the network in travel order. Light each station as it is reached and move a train marker continuously along the line, slowing at stops and choosing branches only after the relevant decision. Use transfers to express dependencies or changed modes, not decoration. Let inactive routes remain subdued until needed. Finish by arriving at the terminus and transforming its station label into a larger physical sign or end card.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

# Backlit Widescreen Safari

## Overview

A bright, low-saturation 1980s landscape spectacle with realistic scale, layered amber distance, and waves of unsynchronized animal movement.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A bright, low-saturation 1980s landscape spectacle with realistic scale, layered amber distance, and waves of unsynchronized animal movement.

## Signature Features

- Low sun behind the moving lead
- Cream sky, amber veil, three or four fading ridge layers
- Bright middle-to-high values with warm grey shadows
- Detailed true-scale vehicles, terrain, and rigging
- Large flocks or herds reacting in staggered waves

## Style Prompt

Build a true-scale real-time 3D landscape with smooth terrain and fully resolved practical detail—supports, wires, rivets, harness, vegetation, rocks, and vehicle parts—while avoiding faceted low-poly ground. Place a low slanting sun behind the moving lead from the camera's viewpoint. Use a pale cream sky, thin amber atmosphere, three or four progressively lighter mountain layers, ochre earth, and long soft shadows. Keep roughly seventy percent of the image in middle or high values, restrict near-white to small zones, and hold shadows in warm grey rather than dark sepia. Apply a gentle film curve, low saturation, fine grain, and slight vignette. Populate the landscape with hundreds or thousands of birds or animals using controlled color variation and unique phase offsets. As the lead passes, let groups lift, turn, or scatter in delayed waves rather than as one synchronized sheet. Favor an over-the-shoulder vista from the moving subject, a chase ten to twenty meters above ground, and one direct backlit angle toward the sun.

## Avoid

cold pristine CG light, dark brown grading, uniform flock motion, empty terrain, low-detail hero assets, and exaggerated white bloom.

# Studio-Silver Art Deco Lobby

## Overview

A polished 1930s monochrome 3D interior organized by symmetry, reflections, and one moving color accent.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Deep studio blacks and glossy silver whites
- Marble, chrome, chandeliers, checkerboard floors, and revolving doors
- High shafts of white light with restrained halation
- Overhead concentric geometry used once as an establishing device
- Characters walk real paths through reflective depth

## Style Prompt

Build a complete three-dimensional environment with Art Deco materials: veined marble, chrome rails, pleated walls, chandeliers, and a reflective checkerboard floor. Grade the scene to luminous studio black-and-white, retaining deep blacks over roughly two fifths of the image and bright silver whites without milky grey wash. Let one elevated source cast visible white shafts; keep bloom close to fixtures and highlights. Use exact symmetry for the principal wide, showing both floor and ceiling so reflections extend the depth. An overhead view may briefly reduce balconies and people to concentric rings and chess pieces, but follow it with an eye-level lateral track. Give period figures faces, hats, limbs, and routes whose footfalls match their translation through space. One lead figure may remain in a subdued warm accent.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

stationary walk cycles, abstract geometry taking over the narrative, warm lamp color, and flat ambient illumination.

# Watercolor Illustration

## Overview

Translucent washes, feathered edges, and paper grain create a delicate emotional image. Choose this as the general watercolor language for translucent washes, feathered edges, and paper grain.

## Metadata

- Collection: Cartoon Styles
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or generated visual assets
- Best for: memory, mood, nature, and reflective passages

## Signature Features

- Transparent layered color.
- Soft blooms and backruns.
- Visible cold-press paper.
- Selective crisp drawn accents.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Watercolor Illustration** style. Let washes appear in controlled blooms, preserve luminous white paper, and use a few sharper edges to guide attention.

## Avoid

- Choose **Watercolor Journal** for annotated travel observation, **Torn Watercolor Landscape** for collage rupture, or **Warm Watercolor Learning Card** for instruction.
- Opaque digital airbrush, uniformly blurred edges, saturated neon color, or simulated water spreading over text.

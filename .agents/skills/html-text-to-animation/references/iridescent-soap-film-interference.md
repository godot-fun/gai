# Iridescent Soap-Film Interference

## Overview

Microscopic film thickness produces drifting rainbow bands that thin, merge, and rupture like a suspended soap membrane.

## Metadata

- Collection: Light and Optical Materials
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: delicacy, changing boundaries, surface physics, and ephemeral beauty

## Signature Features

- Suspend one large asymmetric membrane against charcoal; use transparent cyan, magenta, amber, and green bands whose width follows local film thickness.
- Let gravity drain color downward, merge interference bands at slow boundaries, then puncture one edge; use a close macro camera with almost no parallax.
- Use a light geometric sans serif outside the membrane, never printed across its busiest spectrum.

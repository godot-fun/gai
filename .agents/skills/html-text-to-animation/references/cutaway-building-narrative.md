# Cutaway Building Narrative

## Overview

Exterior walls peel away to expose simultaneous stories and systems across multiple floors.

## Metadata

- Collection: Architecture and Spatial Installation
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: organizations, homes, infrastructure, and parallel activity

## Signature Features

- Keep the exterior silhouette while removing selected walls to reveal stacked rooms, occupants, utilities, and shared shafts.
- Open the envelope in panels, stage synchronized activity across floors, and shift focus without changing building registration.
- For **Cutaway Building Narrative**, choose a restrained architectural palette led by material color and one light or wayfinding accent; preserve human scale and believable structure.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Cutaway Building Narrative** style. Keep the exterior silhouette while removing selected walls to reveal stacked rooms, occupants, utilities, and shared shafts. Open the envelope in panels, stage synchronized activity across floors, and shift focus without changing building registration. Choose a restrained palette led by structural material, environmental light, and one wayfinding or performance accent; preserve human scale. Use quiet modernist typography on plans, plaques, or negative space, never as weightless decoration inside the built volume. Organize the message around spatial sequence, structural behavior, or installation mechanics so every transformation remains inhabitable and legible.

## Avoid

- Avoid dollhouse clutter, all rooms moving equally, impossible floor heights, missing structure, or camera angles that obscure adjacency.
- Do not add architectural detail that ignores structure, circulation, material span, installation mechanics, or human scale.

# Original Arcade Duel

## Overview

A self-contained 16-bit fighting-game scene that converts any conflict into readable sprites, timing, interface, and impact feedback without copying an existing franchise.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A self-contained 16-bit fighting-game scene that converts any conflict into readable sprites, timing, interface, and impact feedback without copying an existing franchise.

## Signature Features

- Native 320 by 180 canvas enlarged by integer nearest-neighbor scaling
- Roughly thirty-two colors and chunky fifty-pixel fighters
- Custom five-by-seven bitmap interface
- Three-to-four-frame hit stop, sparks, flash, knockback, and result state
- Fixed side-on arena with no camera cuts

## Style Prompt

Render at an internal resolution of 320 by 180 and enlarge only by an integer nearest-neighbor factor. Apply faint CRT scan lines by darkening alternating rows, but preserve crisp pixel clusters and never anti-alias. Reduce the user's setting to a flat side-view floor and one readable background layer. Design two original chunky fighters about fifty pixels tall, each with a dark outline, one-pixel highlights, distinct silhouette, idle cycle, walk, guard, attack, hit, fall, and victory pose. If the source has no literal opponent, personify its obstacle—deadline, storm, debt, fear, malfunction, or distance—as the rival. Draw a bespoke five-by-seven bitmap interface with two health bars, yellow current health, delayed red damage, timer, round prompt, combo count, and final result. On impact, create directional sparks, pause action for three or four frames, flash white briefly, and apply convincing recoil; launch the loser before showing K.O. and WINNER. Hold one fixed side-on view for the full duel.

## Avoid

copied characters, logos, move names, UI layouts, subpixel movement, smoothed pixels, random particle spam, and cinematic camera cuts.

# Red Window Paper Cut

## Overview

Jointed red paper cutouts animate on a light ground, with carved voids functioning as the drawing. Choose this for red Chinese window-cut symbolism, bilateral cutting logic, and festive folk-paper transformation.

## Metadata

- Collection: Lemo Opuscar
- Medium: HTML animation, composited media, SVG, Canvas, or WebGL
- Best for: Chinese folk stories, festivals, family, and symbolic transformation
- Complexity: Advanced

## Signature Features

- One dominant vermilion paper.
- Positive and negative shapes carry equal meaning.
- Cuts remain connected enough to read as paper craft.
- Articulated motion respects flat material.

# Smart-City Sensor Grid

## Overview

Distributed urban sensors report transport, air, energy, water, and occupancy across synchronized layers.

## Metadata

- Collection: Interfaces and Information Systems
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: cities, infrastructure, sustainability, and civic systems

## Signature Features

- Layer a legible city plan with separate transport, energy, air, water, and occupancy networks plus a shared time control.
- Activate one domain at a time, reveal cross-system consequences, and aggregate only after local sensors establish the pattern.
- For **Smart-City Sensor Grid**, use a near-black, slate, or warm-white operational surface with one semantic accent and color reserved for status, confidence, or ownership.

## Style Prompt

Create a polished HTML animation in the **Smart-City Sensor Grid** style. Layer a legible city plan with separate transport, energy, air, water, and occupancy networks plus a shared time control. Activate one domain at a time, reveal cross-system consequences, and aggregate only after local sensors establish the pattern. Use a disciplined operational surface with color assigned consistently to status, confidence, ownership, or risk. Set typography in a compact UI sans serif or monospaced face; align values, units, timestamps, and labels to a stable grid. Organize the message around the operational decision so every state change, alert, route, or dependency has a readable cause.

## Avoid

- Avoid all sensors glowing simultaneously, surveillance spectacle, unlabeled units, or implying complete city coverage.
- Do not add dashboard chrome, alerts, metrics, or map layers that do not support the operational decision being shown.

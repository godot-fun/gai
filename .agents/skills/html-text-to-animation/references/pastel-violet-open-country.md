# Pastel Violet Open Country

## Overview

A soft-pastel dusk landscape on grey-violet paper, with smooth sky, densely worked grass, warm horizon, and one slow lateral approach.

- Smoothed sky with a few long cloud bars
- Dense upright grass marks concentrated near camera
- Gold-to-rose-to-violet dusk and deep blue shadow
- Long central horizon with medium subject in lower half

## Style Prompt

Work directly on grey-violet toothed paper and allow its fine granular texture to remain visible in every region. Rub the sky into a soft continuous field, interrupting it only with a few narrow horizontal cloud bands. Concentrate mark-making on the ground: establish muted ochre masses, then layer thousands of upright grass strokes whose width stays hair-fine, length varies, brightness shifts visibly, density is greatest in the foreground, and spacing opens toward the horizon. Use soft broken diagonal strokes for vehicles, buildings, people, rocks, or water edges, keeping boundaries friable rather than digitally sharp. Hold a long horizon near the middle of frame. Grade the sky from warm gold at the horizon through dusty rose into violet above, with distant land and clouds in purple and deepest shadow in twilight blue. Place a medium-scale subject and its belongings in the lower half. As darkness increases, allow existing warm lamps to strengthen. Execute one slow lateral track combined with a mild push.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

oil-like impasto, crisp vector edges, smooth empty ground, dense marks in the sky, hyper-saturated sunset, and a tiny unreadable subject.

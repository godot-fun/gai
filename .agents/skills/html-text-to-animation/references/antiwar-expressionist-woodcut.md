# Antiwar Expressionist Woodcut

## Overview

A severe two-block woodcut language for grief, exhaustion, and moral conflict. Choose this when jagged carved form, compression, and emotional protest define the image.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation

## Signature Features

- Sixty percent or more solid black
- Tapered white gouges that separate compressed figures
- Large hands and mask-like pale faces
- Warm paper, near-black ink, and one misregistered color block
- Still gestures under a single opening of light

## Style Prompt

Treat the frame as a hand-cut relief print, not a shaded drawing. Mass figures against the crop so bodies press into one another and the border. Hold most of the image in unbroken near-black ink; reveal form with white gouges whose entries and exits taper, chip, and vary in rhythm. Faces are pale carved islands with only a few black features, while hands remain oversized and emotionally legible. Let cuts widen as they approach a window, cloud break, or other single source of light. Print on warm fibrous stock with restrained wood grain and under-inked flecks. A single muted color block may identify the central subject, but black cuts must bite through it and slight registration drift should remain visible. Keep motion minimal—breath, a hand extending, a distant procession—and use slow pans or pushes.

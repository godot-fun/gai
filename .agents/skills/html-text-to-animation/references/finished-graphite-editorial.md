# Finished Graphite Editorial

## Overview

A controlled mid-century pencil illustration that visibly constructs itself before settling into facial drama.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Source type: visual style reference

## Signature Features

- Pressure-tapered contours and near-parallel form-following hatch
- Rubbed middle tones with eraser-cut highlights
- Fully described settings that fade only at the outer margin
- Colored pencil restricted to the lead costume
- Visible construction sequence completed early

## Style Prompt

Draw on lightly toothed warm paper as a finished editorial illustration rather than a loose sketch. During the first second, establish faint perspective rays, head ovals, and gesture axes; replace them with pressure-tapered contours that grow heavier through the middle and slightly overshoot corners. Add one to four passes of nearly parallel hatch following each form, increasing passes rather than crossing them into a grid. Rub selected areas into controlled middle tone and lift clean edges, reflected light, and facial accents with an eraser. Fully articulate furniture, architecture, crowd, and props nearly to the edge, letting only the outer tenth dissolve into unfinished marks. Keep most values in the middle range with a compact share of deep darks. Apply colored pencil solely to the lead character's clothing, never skin or environment. After the drawing finishes, hold it and move with a slow pullback followed by close facial cuts.

## Customizable Elements

Adapt the subject, setting, wording, duration, aspect ratio, and narrative emphasis. Adjust individual hues only within the palette roles defined above, and preserve the medium, composition, material behavior, and motion grammar that make the style recognizable.

## Avoid

scribbled shorthand, empty paper, mechanical crosshatch meshes, and animation that keeps redrawing forever.

# Semantic Shape-Morph Chain

## Overview

One hero silhouette continuously becomes a sequence of meaningful objects through planned intermediate forms, conserved mass, and beat-locked landings.

## Metadata

- Collection: Motion Graphics Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: One hero silhouette continuously becomes a sequence of meaningful objects through planned intermediate forms, conserved mass, and beat-locked landings.

## Signature Features

- Six to eight related silhouettes in one chain
- Continuous contour correspondence without popping
- Intermediate bridge shapes and controlled point mapping
- Morph combined with purposeful translation, rotation, and squash
- Stable hold at every semantic landing

## Style Prompt

Choose six to eight forms that express a real progression in the user's content and identify the shared visual feature between each pair: hole, handle, corner, stem, axis, circle, or directional wedge. Normalize scale and apparent mass, then design intermediate silhouettes that preserve those landmarks while redistributing the remaining contour. Move, rotate, and squash the form during the transition so difficult topology changes feel motivated. Accelerate out of the old state, pass fluidly through one readable bridge, overshoot slightly, and settle exactly on the musical or narrative beat. Hold each result long enough to recognize before starting the next morph. Use color changes only when they clarify category or carry the transition into a new scene. End by revealing how the final form relates back to the first.

## Avoid

cross-dissolves, hidden hard cuts, point-order twists, arbitrary icon sequences, shapes changing area wildly, constant speed, morphs that never land, and decoration more noticeable than the semantic link.

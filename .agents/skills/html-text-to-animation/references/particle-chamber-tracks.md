# Particle-Chamber Tracks

## Overview

Charged particles leave curved condensation trails whose radius and branching reveal collisions and energy.

## Metadata

- Collection: Scientific Imaging
- Medium: HTML, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or composited media
- Best for: physics, evidence, invisible events, and discovery

## Signature Features

- Use a dark chamber volume with fine droplets and a uniform magnetic field; distinguish thin electron curls, straighter heavy tracks, and collision vertices.
- Nucleate tracks from discrete events, grow them at plausible speed, and freeze briefly for annotation; view from above or at a shallow scientific angle.
- Use precise monospaced callouts with curvature guides and restrained white linework.

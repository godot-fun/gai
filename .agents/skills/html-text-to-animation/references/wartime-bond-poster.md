# Wartime Bond Poster

## Overview

A frontal civic spectacle built from four flat wartime inks, coarse screen shadows, and an oversized emblem.

## Metadata

- Collection: Academy Award Film Styles
- Medium: Cinematic HTML animation
- Best for: A frontal civic spectacle built from four flat wartime inks, coarse screen shadows, and an oversized emblem.

## Signature Features

- Red, navy, olive, and khaki on fibrous cream stock
- Three-step form modeling: paper, solid, and coarse screened ink
- Brown-black keyline printed last
- Small figure against a giant flag, badge, ray field, or symbol
- Slow push followed by one decisive pullback

## Style Prompt

Reduce every form to three hard value steps: exposed paper, flat ink, and a coarse forty-five-degree screened overprint. Use only brick red, navy, olive, and khaki on aged cream paper, then print a thin brown-black contour plate last. Preserve paper fiber, foxing, rubbed edges, crayon grain inside the screen, and slight color-plate drift. Build the composition as a frontal stage: a comparatively small lead stands before an emblematic structure that fills most of the image—a flag, seal, starburst, machine, building, or diagram relevant to the message. Represent light as a hard-edged pale shape or untouched paper, including outdoor pools on the ground. Keep pose, gaze, and hand action legible at poster distance. Add a brief slogan only when the content genuinely benefits from it, in the user's language and integrated with the geometry. Advance slowly toward the figure or emblem, then make one clean pullback that reveals their scale relationship.

## Avoid

airbrushed transitions, exact registration, fine screens that read as grey, pseudo-photographic lighting, and propaganda text unrelated to the user's subject.
